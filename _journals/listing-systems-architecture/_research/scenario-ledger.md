## The group

- **Veldmark Group** — a privately held, multi-country, multi-vertical marketplace group. Majority-owned since 2025 by a private-equity investor; a founding-family trust holds a minority stake. Headquarters in **Rotterdam**; engineering hubs in **Lisbon**, **Warsaw**, **Lyon** and **Düsseldorf**.
- **2026 figures (fiscal year = calendar year):** revenue **€1.31 billion**; adjusted EBITDA about **€470 million**; about **5,200 employees**, of whom about **1,450** work in product and technology (about **1,150 engineers**).
- Operates in **8 countries** (ten country marketplaces): Netherlands, Belgium, Poland, Portugal (Tradeyard); Germany, Netherlands, Poland, Austria (Motorvane); France, Spain (Hearthly); France, Belgium, Spain (Keybridge).

## The 2027 situation — problems Veldmark starts with

1. **Four of everything.** Four identity systems, four trust-and-safety stacks, three search engines, three clouds, four product catalogs, three data warehouses.
2. **Tradeyard's publication time:** p95 **6 minutes** from submit to searchable (Solr batch commits plus moderation queue); target **90 seconds** p95 by end of 2028.
3. **Taxonomy:** four Tradeyard country trees that cannot share features; a new category takes about **6 weeks** (target: 2 days).
4. **Duplicates at Hearthly** (1.7 listings per property) degrade search and anger agencies.
5. **Payment-link phishing** on Tradeyard messaging (NL/BE).
6. **Yardstick** is a single deployable that blocks 140 Tradeyard engineers; deploys twice a week.
7. **Keybridge neutrality:** Keybridge publishes listings to Hearthly's competitors; agencies fear Veldmark uses their data to favor Hearthly.
8. **Overlap:** Tradeyard Poland's cars section competes with Motorvane Poland for the same dealers.

## Common Ground program

- Approved by the board in **January 2027**: **€140 million** over **2027–2030**. Goals: shared platform capabilities (Veldmark ID, trust and safety, media, search platform, messaging, product catalog and entitlements, event backbone, data platform, taxonomy and attribute service), retirement of Yardstick by **end of 2029**, run-cost reduction of **€38 million per year** by 2030.
- Principle: **share platforms, not products** — marketplaces keep their own brands, UX and go-to-market; the group shares capabilities beneath them.

## Recurring worked examples (canonical)

1. **The Rotterdam city bike (Tradeyard NL).** Private seller **Femke** (she/her) lists a used city bike for **€240** on **Sunday 7 March 2027 at 19:42 CET**. Searchable at **19:48** (the 6-minute baseline). Matches **312** saved searches; first contact **19:55**; **14** conversations within 24 hours. At **20:11** a "buyer" sends a fake Safe Deal payment link (phishing); the message is delivered because the domain was registered that day; Femke reports it at **20:19**; the account is suspended at **20:26**; a new rule then withholds **37** similar messages to other sellers. Sold **Monday 8 March 2027** for **€225** via Safe Deal "pay at pickup"; buyer fee **€11.95** (5% × €225 = €11.25 + €0.70). Marked sold at **18:10**; removed from search at **18:11**, but the evening alert digest still featured it the next morning (Ghost Inventory).
2. **The Essen Volkswagen Golf (Motorvane DE).** A 2021 Volkswagen Golf 1.5 TSI, **48,000 km**, **€21,900**, from dealer **Autohaus Ruhrblick** (fictional) in Essen via its DMS feed on **Wednesday 14 April 2027**. VIN decoding fills 31 attributes; Motorvane's market-value model estimates **€22,800** ("good price"). **23 leads in 10 days** (14 e-mail, 7 calls, 2 test-drive bookings). Price cut to **€21,400** on **Thursday 22 April** triggers price-drop alerts to 1,180 watchers. Sold **Thursday 29 April 2027**; the feed's removal arrives 26 hours later.
3. **The Lyon apartment (Hearthly FR).** A three-room (T3) **68 m²** flat in Lyon 7e for sale at **€389,000**, energy class (DPE) **D**. Listed in **May 2027** by **three agencies** (via two real-estate software vendors and Keybridge); one agency is **Agence Rhône Habitat** (fictional), a network of **14 branches**. Entity resolution clusters the three listings into one canonical property. Valuo estimate: **€372,000–€401,000**.
4. **The Barcelona fake-rental ring (Hearthly ES).** **July 2027**: **26** accounts post **180** fake rental listings in Eixample using photos stolen from real listings, priced about **35%** below market, asking for deposits by bank transfer outside the platform. Detected through perceptual-hash matches against existing listings plus price anomalies; **63** people report losses totaling about **€71,000** before takedown.
5. **The Sunday-evening brown-out (Tradeyard NL).** **Sunday 17 January 2027, 20:35 CET**: search p99 latency rises from about **400 ms** to about **7 s** for **48 minutes**; about **9%** of searches fail. Causes: a TV campaign, a scheduled reindex job and Solr cache eviction at the weekly peak.
