# Spec: Part VIII — LEADERSHIP: The Chief Architect's Agenda

## Intent

Orient the reader to Part VIII: why the hard problems of a marketplace group are problems of change rather than design, and how consolidation, organization, modernization, AI and long-term evolution together form the Chief Architect's agenda. Say which question each of the five chapters answers and when to read it, and frame the part through the Veldmark decisions of 2027 that move Common Ground from plan to practice.

## Audience

Chief and group architects, CTOs, principal engineers and engineering leaders who must sequence platform change across acquired marketplaces, assign ownership and keep the result coherent over years.
