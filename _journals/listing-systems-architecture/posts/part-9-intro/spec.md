# Spec: Part IX — PRACTICE: How Others Built It

## Intent

Orient the reader to Part IX: six publicly documented cases that test the book's patterns against what real marketplace groups did. Explain how to read them (documented fact with publication dates, the author's interpretation, labeled inference) and why fictional Veldmark is kept out of the case narratives. Give a short cross-case comparison so the reader sees the shared lessons before reading any single case.

## Audience

Chief and enterprise architects, principal engineers and engineering leaders who want evidence, not only argument, before adopting the book's platform, consolidation and model-governance recommendations.

## Success criteria

- [ ] Opens with IN THIS PART, WHY THE BUSINESS CARES, WHY ARCHITECTS SHOULD CARE, KEY POINTS (exactly three bullets) and a FAILURE PATTERNS callout using exact names linked to [[where-listing-systems-go-wrong]].
- [ ] A "How to read the cases" section with the three kinds of statement, matching the wording the six cases use in their own "How to Read This Case" sections (documented fact with year; "my reading" or the Chief Architect's view; `INFERENCE, NOT DOCUMENTED` callouts), and the reason Veldmark appears only in each case's final comparison.
- [ ] A "Chapters in this part" table lists all six cases in config order with the question each answers and when to read it.
- [ ] A cross-case table (company | platform strategy | what the public record shows | lesson) whose facts are taken only from the six cases' KEY POINTS and leads.
- [ ] Connects back to [[long-term-evolution]] and the chapters the cases test, and forward to [[toolkit]].
- [ ] A Veldmark thread, labeled fictional, listing 2027 choices that the cases test, consistent with the scenario ledger (Common Ground principle, shared OpenSearch decision of 14 Sep 2027 with Motorvane on Elasticsearch until a 2029 review, Supply Contract v1 Sep 2027, Hearthly Direct declined 2027).
- [ ] Ends with a hand-off to [[case-adevinta]]; about 800 words of body.

## Non-goals

Re-telling any case; adding company facts not already sourced in the case pages; new Veldmark decisions (Part IX tests the scenario, it does not extend it).

## Modalities

Article only for now.

## Open questions

None.
