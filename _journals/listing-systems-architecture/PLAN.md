# LISTED — Book Plan and Chapter Contracts

**LISTED: Architecting Large-Scale Classifieds and Marketplace Platforms**

*How architects and engineering leaders design, scale, govern and evolve the systems behind classifieds and listing marketplaces — from the listing aggregate and the ingestion pipeline to search, trust, monetization and the multi-country platforms that hold a portfolio of marketplaces together.*

**Status:** first edition in progress · **Created:** 2026-10-19

## Positioning

The reader is an experienced architect or engineering leader working on a listing marketplace: chief and enterprise architects of marketplace groups, principal and staff engineers, search, data and ML leads, platform-engineering leads, product and engineering directors of classifieds, real-estate, automotive and jobs portals, and technology leaders integrating acquired marketplaces. The book takes the perspective of a **Chief Architect of a multi-country, multi-vertical marketplace group** who must make one coherent system out of many marketplaces.

The central idea: **a listing marketplace is a liquidity machine, and its architecture should be judged by how reliably it turns fresh, trustworthy supply into matches with demand** — in every country, category and vertical — while letting each marketplace evolve at its own pace. Listings, search, trust and monetization are not separate products; they are stages of one pipeline whose throughput is liquidity.

The book is **company-aware but company-neutral**. It draws on the public engineering record of Adevinta, OLX Group, eBay Classifieds Group, AVIV Group, Kleinanzeigen, Marktplaats, leboncoin, mobile.de, Auto Trader, Rightmove, Zillow, Idealista, CoStar and others, but it organizes the argument around architectural questions, not company profiles. Company-specific statements are documented facts with a source and a publication date; everything else is labeled as industry practice, reference-architecture inference, recommendation or illustration.
