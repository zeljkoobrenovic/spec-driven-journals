| 12.3.4 | Hardware and software technologies in use are reviewed at least once every 12 months, including at least the following: () Analysis that the technologies continue to receive security fixes from vendors promptly. () Documentation of any industry announcements or trends related to a technology, such as when a vendor has announced “end of life” plans for a technology.
