![Comic panel: an engineer points at a working old platform building while sketching a gleaming new tower on a blueprint.](assets/images/rearchitecting-platforms/comic-01-the-aesthetic-itch.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the rewrite urge — but age is not a constraint, and a rearchitecture is not a technical adventure.*

![Comic panel: two figures walk through a graveyard of blueprint-shaped tombstones with epitaphs about lost funding and sponsors.](assets/images/rearchitecting-platforms/comic-02-the-rewrite-graveyard.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the graveyard of platform rewrites is full of good designs that lost their funding, their sponsors, or their customers.*

![Comic panel: an empty new platform on an island linked to the busy old one by a rickety rope bridge nobody crosses.](assets/images/rearchitecting-platforms/comic-03-the-v2-island.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the v2 island and the big bang — years of building with nothing in production, then a migration cliff nobody climbs.*

![Comic panel: Vera at a go/no-go gate holding a checklist of constraint, costs, customer, and leadership while Kai waits.](assets/images/rearchitecting-platforms/comic-04-gate-it-like-an-investment.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: gate it like a business investment — real constraint, honest costs, a committed customer, and leadership that will protect the work.*

![Comic panel: an engineer swaps columns of a working building one at a time while people keep working inside.](assets/images/rearchitecting-platforms/comic-05-rebuild-in-place.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it plays out: incremental over 'v2' — rebuild in place, every step tested against real load, no big bang to land.*

![Comic panel: a three-tier podium of audacious goal, valuable fallback, and production proof under a twelve-month calendar.](assets/images/rearchitecting-platforms/comic-06-no-zero-years.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *How it plays out: twelve-month wins with three levels of success — audacious goal, valuable fallback, production proof. The effort cannot have a zero year.*

![Comic panel: a balance scale where a tall migration coin stack from consumer teams outweighs the platform team's build stack.](assets/images/rearchitecting-platforms/comic-07-the-migration-bill.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *How it plays out: honest migration accounting — the larger half of the cost is paid by other people, so it is counted before commitment.*

![Comic panel: an engineer paves a smooth guardrailed path with a shield embedded in it while a rocky detour sits overgrown.](assets/images/rearchitecting-platforms/comic-08-the-paved-safe-path.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *How it plays out: security by design and by default — protections that depend on structure, not on human behavior, and paved paths where the safest option is the easiest.*

![Comic panel close-up: Vera holds a notebook showing continue, revise, and stop arrows beside an annual review calendar.](assets/images/rearchitecting-platforms/comic-09-would-we-start-it-today.jpeg)
**Panel 9:** *The closer: once a year, in writing — would we still start this knowing what we now know? Stop, revise, or delay without shame.*
