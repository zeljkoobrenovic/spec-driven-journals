![Comic panel: a polished platform tower stands on a cracking org-chart foundation while an executive points at the cracks.](assets/images/organizing-for-platforms/comic-01-fails-organizationally-first.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: platforms fail organizationally before they fail technically.*

![Comic panel: a platform engineer buried behind a service counter with an endless spool of tickets and a queue of developers.](assets/images/organizing-for-platforms/comic-02-the-ticket-queue.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the gravitational pull is toward a ticket queue with a logo — doing work for teams instead of enabling them.*

![Comic panel: a developer faces four mismatched portal doors labeled network, storage, security, and compute under org-chart lines.](assets/images/organizing-for-platforms/comic-03-the-shipped-org-chart.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: ship the org chart, and users navigate your internal seams instead of one platform.*

![Comic panel: an executive shows a small-company chart with five named roles while an engineer wears two stacked hats.](assets/images/organizing-for-platforms/comic-04-a-small-product-company.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: run the platform team as a small product company — every role owned, even when one person wears several hats.*

![Comic panel: an engineer slides one clean facade with a single door in front of mismatched infrastructure machines.](assets/images/organizing-for-platforms/comic-05-east-west-one-interface.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *East–west: infrastructure functions align on common outcomes behind one interface — and the seams stay inside.*

![Comic panel: a two-way footbridge where a platform leader and a developer exchange notes while a torn mandate poster lies discarded.](assets/images/organizing-for-platforms/comic-06-north-south-relationship.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *North–south: adoption is a relationship earned through value — mandates buy compliance, not commitment.*

![Comic panel: teams pull an engineer toward hands-on help while the roadmap gathers cobwebs and an executive holds a balance scale.](assets/images/organizing-for-platforms/comic-07-the-consulting-drift.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: high-touch help eats the team — the engagement portfolio needs deliberate rebalancing, or consulting wins by default.*

![Comic panel: two figures at a signpost choosing between building a platform and enabling teams with skills.](assets/images/organizing-for-platforms/comic-08-do-we-need-it.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: confirm the platform is needed at all — sometimes enablement and a thinner platform win.*
