![Comic panel: Kai presents a glossy borrowed strategy binder while Vera holds it at arm's length, unimpressed.](assets/images/platform-strategy/comic-01-the-copied-strategy.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the most common platform strategy is someone else's — and the context that made it work does not copy.*

![Comic panel: an hourglass-shaped document with business goals on top, technology below, and an empty middle that Vera points at.](assets/images/platform-strategy/comic-02-the-it-hourglass.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the IT hourglass — business outcomes at the top, a technology inventory at the bottom, and nothing where the reasoning should be.*

![Comic panel: Kai points at a slide reading only DEVOPS while Vera crosses her arms skeptically.](assets/images/platform-strategy/comic-03-buzzword-mechanisms.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: buzzword mechanisms — 'we will do DevOps' is a label where an explanation should be.*

![Comic panel: Vera draws four connected chain links labeled context, objectives, mechanisms, decisions on a whiteboard.](assets/images/platform-strategy/comic-04-the-four-layer-chain.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: context, objectives, mechanisms, design decisions — four layers with explicit connections, so no hourglass can form.*

![Comic panel: a shiny new platform machine behind Kai, who still hand-stamps a stack of old paper approval forms.](assets/images/platform-strategy/comic-05-new-engine-old-habits.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it fails quietly: new technology under old ways of working is optimization sold as transformation.*

![Comic panel: Vera and Kai study an expedition map with a destination flag, a winding path with forks, and drawn terrain.](assets/images/platform-strategy/comic-06-point-path-terrain.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *How it plays out: the roadmap is point, path, and terrain — with named decision points, alternative paths, and the data needed to choose.*

![Comic panel: Vera hands keys to small developer figures while Kai tends one central toolbox; red approval tape lies in a wastebasket.](assets/images/platform-strategy/comic-07-relinquish-control.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: governance earns autonomy or it is just control — centralizing expertise means relinquishing some central control so users can innovate.*

![Comic panel: Vera holds a document with four stamps labeled A, C, E, D, pen hovering over the signature line.](assets/images/platform-strategy/comic-08-the-aced-gate.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: the ACED gate — Alignment, Clarity, Evolution, Decisions. A strategy that decides nothing does not get signed.*
