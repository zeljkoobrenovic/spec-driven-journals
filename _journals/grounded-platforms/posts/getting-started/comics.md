![Comic panel: Kai wheels a huge machine labeled K8S into a tiny startup office while Vera asks if ten engineers need it.](assets/images/getting-started/comic-01-day-one-kubernetes.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the temptation to start with big-company infrastructure long before anything demands it.*

![Comic panel: Vera points at a shelf of simple tooling boxes while Kai launches a paper boat labeled FEATURE down a stream.](assets/images/getting-started/comic-02-stay-lightweight.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *Early stage: fast feedback and boring off-the-shelf tooling — platform work stays a shared responsibility.*

![Comic panel: a smoking server rack labeled SHARED while engineers all point at each other and Kai holds an extinguisher.](assets/images/getting-started/comic-03-nobody-owns-it.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The signal: 'everyone owns it' has quietly become 'nobody owns it' — coordination costs are now visible.*

![Comic panel: Kai celebrates a headcount milestone with a ribbon while Vera holds up a chart of rising friction.](assets/images/getting-started/comic-04-the-calendar-team.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The wrong way: forming a platform team on a calendar or headcount milestone instead of observable friction.*

![Comic panel: a balance scale weighing one shared gear serving many teams against a knot of custom adapters.](assets/images/getting-started/comic-05-the-centralization-test.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *The test: centralize only where one implementation serves many teams — leverage, not apparent efficiency.*

![Comic panel: Kai repairs a cracked pipe labeled SHARED LIBS while a grand blueprint labeled FUTURE sits ignored.](assets/images/getting-started/comic-06-fix-todays-pain.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The first team: fix the most painful existing problems first — trust is the currency for any later redesign.*

![Comic panel: a job candidate unpacks a giant crate labeled BIGCO full of heavy machinery while Vera and Kai look on.](assets/images/getting-started/comic-07-the-bigco-import.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: hire first-principles thinkers for the organization you have — not importers of big-company machinery.*

![Comic panel: Kai hands a mug labeled FOR OUR ENGINEERS to a developer while Vera watches approvingly.](assets/images/getting-started/comic-08-a-discipline-not-a-tech.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: platform engineering is a product and cultural discipline — the customers are engineers, not the technology.*
