![Comic panel: application-team buildings under construction on top of a slab labeled PLATFORM while Vera tells Kai they are building on his promise.](assets/images/introduction/comic-01-built-on-a-promise.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: a platform is a promise other teams build on — roadmaps, budgets, and careers all bet on it.*

![Comic panel: Kai holds a locked blueprint inside a thought bubble while surrounding team leads show question marks and Vera points at the bubble.](assets/images/introduction/comic-02-living-in-one-head.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: when the model lives in someone's head, everyone who depends on the platform has to guess.*

![Comic panel: three stakeholders pull a platform block in different directions while Kai is caught in the middle.](assets/images/introduction/comic-03-guessing-the-bar.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: leave the bar unwritten and every stakeholder invents their own — including 'the platform should do everything.'*

![Comic panel: Vera presents a three-shelf bookcase of twenty-nine record binders to Kai.](assets/images/introduction/comic-04-write-it-down-three-shelves.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: write the operating model down — twenty-nine records anyone can read, cite, and hold the platform to.*

![Comic panel: Kai holds three books labeled HOW, WHETHER, and HANDS-ON while Vera points between them.](assets/images/introduction/comic-05-three-books-three-questions.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it is built: three books, three questions — engineering answers how, strategy answers whether and why, and the reference implementation shows what it concretely looks like.*

![Comic panel: Vera reads a principle page while Kai runs a ticked checklist against a small platform of server racks.](assets/images/introduction/comic-06-article-plus-checklist.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *How it reads: every record states the principle in the article and ships the runnable self-assessment in its Checklist tab.*

![Comic panel: Kai stamps a record binder DRAFT while Vera points at its revisit-conditions tag.](assets/images/introduction/comic-07-draft-on-purpose.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: the records start as drafts, adopted ahead of being worn in — and each one names when it must be revisited.*

![Comic panel: Vera and Kai study an unrolled map of three linked regions of records, one drawn as a numbered build road.](assets/images/introduction/comic-08-read-the-map-three-regions.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: this introduction is the map — stop guessing what the platform bar is, and read it.*
