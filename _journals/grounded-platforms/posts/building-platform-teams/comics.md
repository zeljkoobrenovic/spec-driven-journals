![Comic panel: Kai presents a row of five identical engineers while Vera warns that sameness is the failure mode.](assets/images/building-platform-teams/comic-01-a-team-of-clones.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: platform teams fail from imbalance more often than from lack of talent — a single hiring profile is the failure mode.*

![Comic panel: a desk buried in scripts and a giant rulebook while an engineer blames a confused user holding a broken gear.](assets/images/building-platform-teams/comic-02-too-much-systems.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *Failure mode one, 'too much systems': automation, templates, and rulebooks that manage complexity without removing it — and a team that blames its users.*

![Comic panel: an engineer admires blueprints labeled NEW ARCH while a server room burns behind them and a pager buzzes ignored.](assets/images/building-platform-teams/comic-03-too-much-development.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *Failure mode two, 'too much development': new architecture outranks reliability, and the platform spends its customers' trust to entertain itself.*

![Comic panel: Vera stands between a coder and a systems engineer shaking hands under two equal medals.](assets/images/building-platform-teams/comic-04-the-mixed-team.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: mix people who write substantial production code with broad systems and operational expertise — and reward both equally.*

![Comic panel: a candidate whiteboards a real system they built while Kai listens and generic puzzles sit in the wastebasket.](assets/images/building-platform-teams/comic-05-interview-for-the-job.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it plays out: the loop mirrors the job — platform design, an inverted interview on a real system, operations and incidents, not borrowed puzzles.*

![Comic panel: a small folder of adoption and incident-response evidence outweighs a huge stack labeled 10,000 LINES on a scale.](assets/images/building-platform-teams/comic-06-promotion-evidence.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Careers reward what the platform needs: adopted tools, support quality, incident leadership — or the team rationally stops doing that work.*

![Comic panel: Kai admits one specialist whose expertise matches a real need while other roles wait outside the team door.](assets/images/building-platform-teams/comic-07-earned-not-collected.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The trade-off: reliability engineers, deep specialists, PMs, and TPMs join when a demonstrated need exists — not before, and never as a collection.*

![Comic panel: Kai listens to a frustrated developer and hands over an improved gear while Vera nods approvingly.](assets/images/building-platform-teams/comic-08-users-are-customers.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: customer empathy is a hiring bar, not a nice-to-have — when a user struggles, the platform improves, nobody lectures.*
