![Comic panel: Kai celebrates a 100% adoption dashboard while Vera watches glum developers file through a gate labeled ONLY OPTION.](assets/images/platform-as-a-product/comic-01-hundred-percent-adoption.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the dashboard says 100% adoption — but mandatory usage proves nothing about the product.*

![Comic panel: developers secretly build their own tool labeled OUR OWN while the mandatory platform sits unloved behind them.](assets/images/platform-as-a-product/comic-02-the-shadow-platform.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: captivity hides dissatisfaction until a customer team builds a competing solution in anger.*

![Comic panel: Kai overwhelmed at a counter labeled FEATURES while a figure with a megaphone shouts an order past a long queue.](assets/images/platform-as-a-product/comic-03-the-feature-shop.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the feature shop — building every request as specified, for whoever asks loudest, until no common capability ever emerges.*

![Comic panel: Kai takes notes while watching a developer walk through a tangled workflow on screen, Vera approving behind.](assets/images/platform-as-a-product/comic-04-observe-the-customer.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: internal engineers are customers — observe how they actually work, not what they say they want.*

![Comic panel: a customer lead signs an alpha pledge with an hourglass and coins on the table while a crossed-out applause balloon floats behind.](assets/images/platform-as-a-product/comic-05-real-commitments.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it plays out: product-market fit is validated with real commitments of time, effort, or budget — enthusiasm in a meeting is not validation.*

![Comic panel: Kai checks a nearly empty jar labeled CHANGE BUDGET before offering a new module, with a migration wagon standing ready.](assets/images/platform-as-a-product/comic-06-the-change-budget.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The constraint: every migration spends the customer's finite change budget — plan adoption, coordinate launches, and never assume the technically better thing wins.*

![Comic panel: Vera taps a flat chart labeled CUSTOMER SPEED next to a rising chart labeled REQUESTS as Kai lowers a party horn.](assets/images/platform-as-a-product/comic-07-impact-not-activity.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The measure: request counts can rise while customers get no faster — write the impact hypothesis down and let evidence steer the strategy.*

![Comic panel: a developer freely chooses a warm doorway labeled PLATFORM over a dim one labeled BUILD OUR OWN while Vera and Kai watch.](assets/images/platform-as-a-product/comic-08-would-they-choose-us.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: the bar is a platform customers would choose if they had a choice — anything less is failing slowly.*
