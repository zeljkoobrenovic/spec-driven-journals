![Comic panel: Kai unveils a shelf packed with separate boxed tools like a gift basket while Vera looks unconvinced.](assets/images/designing-platforms/comic-01-the-tool-catalog.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: anyone can assemble a fruit basket — buy the tools, install them, publish a catalog page.*

![Comic panel: a tired developer tapes and strings separate tools together while the untouched platform shelf stands behind.](assets/images/designing-platforms/comic-02-the-integration-burden.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: a basket adds almost nothing — users could have assembled it themselves, and now they carry our quirks on top.*

![Comic panel: a dull gray one-button box covers a vibrant machine sprouting new features, with a chained-off DOCS bookshelf nearby.](assets/images/designing-platforms/comic-03-the-grim-wrapper.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the grim wrapper — fewer API calls, more cognitive load, lagging the platform it hides and severing users from its community.*

![Comic panel: Vera presents a fruit salad bowl with connecting lines between pieces, next to a dull basket of separate whole fruits.](assets/images/designing-platforms/comic-04-the-fruit-salad.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: a fruit salad, not a fruit basket — integration, defaults, automation, and information flowing between components.*

![Comic panel: Kai places some lettered weights on a balance scale while others stay on the table and Vera takes notes.](assets/images/designing-platforms/comic-05-weigh-the-seven-cs.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it plays out: the 7 Cs are trade-off dimensions — pick which matter most, document what you traded away.*

![Comic panel: an overloaded sinking boat versus a boat where Kai offloads a crate and rides high on the rising sea.](assets/images/designing-platforms/comic-06-float-do-not-sink.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *How it survives: the base platform keeps rising — a floating platform retires what got commoditized underneath it; a sinking one only gains weight.*

![Comic panel: Kai opens a hood on a platform machine and traces a glowing dotted line from an error flag to one inner component.](assets/images/designing-platforms/comic-07-open-the-hood.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost of honesty: design for the unhappy path — errors trace to their origin, the hood opens, and someone still understands the layers below.*

![Comic panel: behind a simple one-button facade, Vera pulls out a long unrolling receipt with clock, coin, and broken-link icons.](assets/images/designing-platforms/comic-08-it-just-moved-the-bill.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: an abstraction that hides essential complexity has not simplified anything — it has just moved the bill.*
