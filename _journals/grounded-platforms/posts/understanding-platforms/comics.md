![Comic panel: Vera and Kai look at a wall of pitch slides all stamped PLATFORM while Vera taps one skeptically.](assets/images/understanding-platforms/comic-01-the-inflated-word.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: every pitch, reorg, and budget request eventually reaches for the word 'platform'.*

![Comic panel: Kai sticks a PLATFORM sticker over a PRODUCT label on a lone box in an empty room.](assets/images/understanding-platforms/comic-02-the-relabeled-product.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: a product relabeled 'platform' is worth nothing extra — and a platform without participants is not a platform.*

![Comic panel: an assembly line of identical cars distinguished only by tiny badges, inspected by Vera with a magnifying glass.](assets/images/understanding-platforms/comic-03-badge-engineering.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: badge engineering — standardize so much that the 'differentiated' products on top are near-identical.*

![Comic panel: Vera shows one shared chassis layer supporting many visibly different vehicles while Kai looks on.](assets/images/understanding-platforms/comic-04-harmonize-below-vary-above.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: harmonize the expensive, undifferentiated engineering underneath so diversity increases on top.*

![Comic panel: Kai at a whiteboard with four simple icons while Vera holds up four fingers.](assets/images/understanding-platforms/comic-05-the-four-questions.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it plays out: who are the participants, what interaction creates the value, what is harmonized underneath, what stays variable on top.*

![Comic panel: a powerful machine room behind glass, reachable only through a tiny TICKETS window with a long queue of developers.](assets/images/understanding-platforms/comic-06-the-ticket-queue-door.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The access test: a powerful platform behind a ticket queue is a weak platform — reducing friction and cognitive load is platform work, not a user problem.*

![Comic panel: Kai adjusts a wall slider between scattered parts on one end and identical clone cars on the other while Vera watches.](assets/images/understanding-platforms/comic-07-where-the-line-goes.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The trade-off: get the harmonization line wrong one way and nothing is reused; wrong the other way and you get badge engineering.*

![Comic panel: Vera peels the PLATFORM sticker off the box to reveal PRODUCT and hands Kai a budget folder.](assets/images/understanding-platforms/comic-08-fund-it-honestly.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: if the answers are 'one team, none, everything, nothing' — rename it back and fund it honestly as a product.*
