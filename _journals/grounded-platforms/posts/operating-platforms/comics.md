![Comic panel: a platform slab with small team buildings stacked on top, all wobbling from a crack underneath.](assets/images/operating-platforms/comic-01-a-promise-to-be-there.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: a platform is a promise to be there — every team on top inherits every outage.*

![Comic panel: Kai coding at his sticker-covered laptop while a buzzing pager and a heap of unread alert notes pile up unmeasured.](assets/images/operating-platforms/comic-02-ops-as-interruption.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: operations treated as an interruption — and an operational load nobody measures.*

![Comic panel: an ops worker behind a wall shrugs and passes an escalation note straight back to Kai, while Vera looks on with her notebook.](assets/images/operating-platforms/comic-03-the-context-free-ops-team.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: hand the pager to a context-free ops team and every incident becomes an escalation chain.*

![Comic panel: Vera points at three circles labeled on-call, support, and feedback while Kai clips on the pager himself.](assets/images/operating-platforms/comic-04-the-builders-operate-it.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: the engineers who build the platform operate it — on-call, support, and operational feedback are core work.*

![Comic panel: Kai points at a wall gauge showing on-call limits of one week in four and fewer than five pages per week, needle nearing red, as Vera watches.](assets/images/operating-platforms/comic-05-load-has-numbers.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it plays out, on-call: load has numbers — at most one week in four, fewer than five meaningful pages — and a breach is a stability problem.*

![Comic panel: Kai at a support desk holds up one of many identical question cards like a clue.](assets/images/operating-platforms/comic-06-support-is-a-sensor.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *How it plays out, support: engineers hear users directly — a repeated question is a product defect with a name and a frequency.*

![Comic panel: a critical incident lane and a business-hours support lane feed a review table where Vera and Kai move a card onto a priorities board.](assets/images/operating-platforms/comic-07-two-lanes-one-review.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *How it plays out, feedback: noncritical support runs in its own lane, and the weekly review turns operational signals into engineering priorities.*

![Comic panel: Kai sets aside a new-feature box to repair a cracked platform slab while Vera watches, ignoring a crossed-out offer of extra pay.](assets/images/operating-platforms/comic-08-stability-outranks-features.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The cost: when the numbers breach, stability work outranks new features — extra pay is never the fix for an unsustainable pager.*

![Comic panel close-up: Vera shows a notebook sketch of a healthy gauge while Kai stands with a quiet pager.](assets/images/operating-platforms/comic-09-unmeasured-is-unmanaged.jpeg)
**Panel 9:** *The closer: a platform whose operational load is unmeasured is a platform whose stability is unmanaged.*
