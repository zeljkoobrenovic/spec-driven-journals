![Comic panel: a developer chooses between a long trail of sticky-note signposts and a short paved lane to a portal arch labeled NEW SERVICE.](assets/images/starter-kits/comic-01-the-first-hour.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: a team makes its platform decision in the first hour of a new service — not in the architecture review.*

![Comic panel: a developer builds a crooked machine from a huge wiki binder while three slightly different machines stand nearby.](assets/images/starter-kits/comic-02-the-wiki-scaffold.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the wiki scaffold — every new service hand-assembled, subtly unique, and off the paved path from day one.*

![Comic panel: a machine stamps out one repository box and departs while a developer holds four ticket slips labeled namespace, claim, pipeline, catalog.](assets/images/starter-kits/comic-03-scaffold-and-vanish.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: scaffold and vanish — a template that creates the repository and leaves namespace, claim, pipeline, and catalog as follow-up tickets.*

![Comic panel: one button press dispenses a repository, a gear pipeline, a claim scroll, a namespace plot, and a catalog card.](assets/images/starter-kits/comic-04-one-action-whole-birth.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: one portal action produces a working service — repository, pipeline, infrastructure claim, namespace, and catalog entry, with ownership recorded from minute one.*

![Comic panel: service boxes with version stamps are checked against a list held by Kai while Vera inspects with a magnifying glass.](assets/images/starter-kits/comic-05-versioned-product.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *The product: the template is a versioned product — metadata stamped at generation time turns upgrades into a query and a campaign instead of archaeology.*

![Comic panel: a small robot service runs a treadmill gauntlet of install, build, test, lint, and health checkpoints while Kai checks a clipboard.](assets/images/starter-kits/comic-06-tested-like-software.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The bar: tested like software — generate a test project and make it install, build, test, lint, start, and answer its health check; no publish with known failures.*

![Comic panel: a cracked template machine stamps out cracked service boxes discovered by three alarmed developer teams downstream.](assets/images/starter-kits/comic-07-every-team-the-test.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *What it costs: an untested template makes every consuming team the test — the defect ships at the exact moment teams were most willing to trust the paved path.*

![Comic panel: Kai walks one continuous path from portal arch through repository, local checks, and deployment to a green pipeline light while Vera stamps PROVEN.](assets/images/starter-kits/comic-08-the-end-to-end-proof.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The proof: walk the whole path once, honestly — create through the portal, validate locally, deploy, push a change, watch the pipeline complete.*

![Comic panel: Vera holds two cards, a spotlight labeled DEMO and a stopwatch with a deployed service labeled CAPABILITY.](assets/images/starter-kits/comic-09-demo-or-capability.jpeg)
**Panel 9:** *The closer: portal click to deployed service in minutes, traceable by template version — anything less is a demo, not a capability.*
