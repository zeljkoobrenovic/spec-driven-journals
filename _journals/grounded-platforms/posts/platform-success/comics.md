![Comic panel: Kai celebrates a 100 percent adoption dashboard while Vera points at a tag on the chart reading mandated.](assets/images/platform-success/comic-01-adoption-theater.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: adoption climbs when a migration is mandated, whatever users think — the dashboard proves nothing on its own.*

![Comic panel: developers sneak parts along a back path to a duct-taped shadow system while Kai peeks over the platform wall.](assets/images/platform-success/comic-02-routing-around.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: roadmaps ship and surveys come back fine — while customers quietly build shadow platforms around you.*

![Comic panel: glue code shrinks on one side while a conveyor dumps the tangle into an overcrowded meeting room.](assets/images/platform-success/comic-03-complexity-relocation.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: glue code goes down, meetings and escalations go up — the platform's dashboard improves while the organization's cost of change grows.*

![Comic panel: Vera shows four gauges labeled aligned, trusted, managing complexity, loved wired to one gray unlit bulb that stays dark because one gauge is low.](assets/images/platform-success/comic-04-the-four-questions.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: success has four dimensions — aligned, trusted, managing complexity, loved — and the test is conjunctive: one weak axis keeps the light off.*

![Comic panel: Kai pushes a low-risk workload cart up a checkpointed staircase past a crossed-out big-bang catapult while Vera tends a legacy plant.](assets/images/platform-success/comic-05-trust-in-stages.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it plays out, trust: earned in stages with incremental checkpoints and maintained legacy — never staked on a distant big bang.*

![Comic panel: a developer sips coffee as a tiny tamed dragon labeled deploy naps on a self-running machine with a small escape hatch.](assets/images/platform-success/comic-06-boring-is-loved.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *How it plays out, loved: the platform makes difficult things feel boring — opinionated enough to be easy, pierceable enough to escape.*

![Comic panel: Kai waves a hiring form while Vera points at a large toil pile with a broom leaning against it.](assets/images/platform-success/comic-07-toil-before-headcount.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: restraint — headcount is not the default answer; toil gets reduced first, and growth funds genuinely new areas, not poor abstractions.*

![Comic panel: Vera and Kai look back at their platform from the customer's side as four lights glow green.](assets/images/platform-success/comic-08-the-customers-side.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: four yeses, judged from the customer's side of the platform — that is what success looks like, whatever the dashboard says.*
