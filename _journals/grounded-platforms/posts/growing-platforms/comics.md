![Comic panel: a gleaming platform pavilion with an open banner and an empty walkway while developers walk past toward other tools.](assets/images/growing-platforms/comic-01-nobody-has-to-use-it.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: an internal platform nobody is forced to use lives or dies on voluntary adoption.*

![Comic panel: an engineer celebrates a board full of shipped feature cards next to a users chart with a flat line.](assets/images/growing-platforms/comic-02-feature-victory-lap.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the feature-count victory lap — a roadmap celebrating shipped capabilities while adoption stays flat is a platform in denial.*

![Comic panel: one happy customer on a custom throne of platform parts while other developers shrug and walk away.](assets/images/growing-platforms/comic-03-perfect-for-one-customer.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the perfect platform for one customer — customer obsession that quietly becomes a professional-services team for one account.*

![Comic panel: a tree with branches labeled reach, canopy labeled breadth, and roots labeled depth, tended by two characters.](assets/images/growing-platforms/comic-04-reach-breadth-depth.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: platforms grow along three dimensions — reach, breadth, and depth — and starving two to max one is how platforms fail.*

![Comic panel: a path through explore, expand, and extract stages with a checkpoint gate where two characters pause.](assets/images/growing-platforms/comic-05-explore-expand-extract.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *The lifecycle: Explore validates that users value the platform, Expand removes adoption obstacles, Extract improves the economics — and between phases the team itself gets reassessed.*

![Comic panel: a smooth on-ramp with handrails beside a path that bends into an unclimbable hockey-stick wall.](assets/images/growing-platforms/comic-06-the-hockey-stick-onramp.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The first hour decides: minimize the cliff, keep simple tasks easy and complex tasks possible — a hockey-stick experience loses users just as they commit.*

![Comic panel: an executive at a sorting desk with an impact-fit board hands a developer a declined card with an explanation sheet.](assets/images/growing-platforms/comic-07-the-principled-no.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The discipline: saying no is how the roadmap protects the product — every request accepted is cohesion spent, so triage on impact and fit and explain the principle behind the no.*

![Comic panel: an engineer hands a developer one slice of a layered platform cake while a users chart curves upward behind them.](assets/images/growing-platforms/comic-08-slices-that-grow.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: scale down as deliberately as up — tiers and slices on shared assets give small customers a way in, and adoption does the growing.*
