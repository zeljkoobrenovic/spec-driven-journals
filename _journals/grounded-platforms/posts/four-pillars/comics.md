![Comic panel: Kai nails a Platform sign onto a rickety tool shed while Vera asks if it is the same shed.](assets/images/four-pillars/comic-01-the-platform-badge.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the platform badge — renaming a tooling team 'platform' changes the sign, not what it builds, runs, or serves.*

![Comic panel: a slab teeters on one pillar next to three broken stumps while Kai strains to hold it and developers slide off.](assets/images/four-pillars/comic-02-one-pillar-teeters.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: most failed platforms are strong on one pillar and absent on the rest — a curated catalog nobody can self-serve, an abstraction nobody operates.*

![Comic panel: a huge stack of wiki binders and config folders with a bow while a developer drowns in pages and Vera shakes her head.](assets/images/four-pillars/comic-03-the-documentation-platform.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the documentation platform — wikis, templates, and config repos relocate and annotate complexity; only real software absorbs it.*

![Comic panel: Vera presents a slab standing on four pillars labeled Product, Software, Breadth, Operations while Kai takes notes.](assets/images/four-pillars/comic-04-four-pillars-at-once.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: curated product, software abstractions, a broad developer base, operated as a foundation — a conjunction, not a menu.*

![Comic panel: developers ride a paved road while one takes a marked exit ramp onto a dirt trail, waved on by Kai.](assets/images/four-pillars/comic-05-paved-path-with-exits.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Pillar one in action: the paved path is the product — and a paved path without an exit is a cage, so the exit is marked, not forbidden.*

![Comic panel: developer teams onboard through self-service kiosks labeled UI, CLI, API while Kai holds a chart with a declining cost line.](assets/images/four-pillars/comic-06-self-service-scale.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Pillar three in action: self-service onboarding, multiple interfaces, guardrails — and economics where each new adopting team costs less than the last.*

![Comic panel: cutaway of developers working on a deck while Kai with an on-call headset fixes a sparking machine among layers labeled Cloud, OSS, Vendor.](assets/images/four-pillars/comic-07-owning-the-basement.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Pillar four in action: the platform team owns what breaks, all the way down through cloud, OSS, and vendor layers — that ownership is what makes the other pillars credible.*

![Comic panel: Vera shows Kai a four-row scorecard with the total circled while a sign behind them is repainted from Platform to Tooling.](assets/images/four-pillars/comic-08-the-truth-serum.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The cost: the scorecard is a truth serum — four questions, scored 0–2; score low and the honest move is to rename the offering and fund it as what it is.*

![Comic panel: a balance scale contrasts complexity absorbed into a platform block versus dumped on a developer's back; Vera points at the first.](assets/images/four-pillars/comic-09-the-running-test.jpeg)
**Panel 9:** *The closer: the test that never stops running — does the platform manage complexity for application developers, or merely move it somewhere else?*
