# Consistency Issues and Canonical Resolutions

Numbered, append-only. Writers and reviewers: the resolutions here win over PLAN.md wording and over research-commission prompts.

1. **incadea is not a Cox product today.** Cox Automotive owned incadea for about three years (from 2015) and sold it in 2018; it has belonged to Volaris (Constellation Software) since then (`brief-dms-integration`, `case-cox-automotive`). Never describe it as a current Cox product.
2. **No "2018 CDK–Reynolds consent" exists.** The documented history is the 2015 CDK lock-out of third-party scrapers, the Authenticom litigation (7th Circuit 2017; MDL), the CDK–Reynolds 2015 data-exchange agreement and later settlements (see `brief-dms-integration`, `brief-case-cox-cdk`).
3. **Kerridge K8 is not Keyloop's DMS** (`brief-dms-integration`). Keyloop = former CDK International (renamed 2021).
4. **Auto Trader UK's Deal Builder dealer pushback was in November 2025**, not 2024 (`brief-case-autotrader-carfax-vwe`).
5. **Carfax** is now part of **Mobility Global Inc.** (NYSE: MBGL), spun off from S&P Global and trading from **1 July 2026** (`brief-case-autotrader-carfax-vwe`). Say "as of 2026".
6. **VWE's public API documentation is XML only** (Interdata, XMLmode); the Productscape DOMAIN.md's "JSON APIs" line is not supported by public sources.
7. **Cox Automotive and Autorola:** no source found for a Cox stake in Autorola; do not claim one.
8. **Wheelsoft** could not be found; do not name it as a Dutch DMS.
9. **Velocity pricing is not current vAuto/Pollak doctrine.** Dale Pollak moved from velocity pricing to ROI-based tiers in 2023 (`brief-dealer-workflows-economics`). Present velocity pricing as a documented historical practice, and say what replaced it.
10. **incadea and the CDK outage cost figures:** the CDK 2024 outage cost to AutoNation is reported differently by brief (about $1.50 vs $2.17 per share). Before citing, check the AutoNation filing yourself and cite the exact figure and the filing date; otherwise say "AutoNation reported a material per-share impact in its 2024 filings".
11. **Used-EV drift evidence:** UK used EVs fell 22.7% year on year (2023–24) vs about 4% for petrol/diesel; Hertz took a $245m EV write-down (`brief-valuation-market-condition`). Drift monitoring must be per segment — this supports the EV Overvaluation story.
12. **Auto Trader valuation accuracy** (99/97/95% at 1/3/6 months) is measured against its own later valuation, not sale prices; say so whenever citing it.
13. **"Authorised information provider" is not RDW terminology found on any RDW page.** Use the terms `brief-identity-registers` documents (RDW-listed providers/ketenpartners for trade transactions; dealers hold a 2-year RDW certificate). For Axleby, the ledger's "authorised information provider since 1999" means: Axleby has held a contractual RDW data-supply and transaction-provider relationship since 1999 — describe it in those plain words, without claiming an official title.
14. **RDW open data is plate-keyed with no VIN column and is a snapshot, not an event log** (16.87M rows, 22 Oct 2026); changes are found by diffing snapshots. The **DVSA MOT history API** (weekly bulk + daily deltas) is the only explicit change feed among the registers studied. DVLA's vehicle enquiry API is closed to new registrations (as of Oct 2026).
15. **The RDW forbids completing a registration during an outage**; queued transactions wait. This is canonical for the Month-End Register Outage.
16. **EU registration-document reform** is COM(2025) 179 (replaces Directive 1999/37/EC; not adopted as of Oct 2026), not COM(2023) 127 (driving licences).
17. **Kilometra batch gate (Odometer Correction):** `observability-and-data-operations` describes a median-based batch gate (0.85–1.15) as the fix; `ingestion-and-entity-resolution` shows a median gate would **not** have caught the 2,870 bad readings (one UK workshop chain's export, 3,050 records, 94% failing) and that a **per-source-segment (per workshop chain) distribution check** did. Canonical: the gate that works is per-segment; observability should describe the median gate as the first, insufficient version and the per-chain check as the fix. `journey-data-correction` must follow this.
18. **Cox Bridge Federation evidence:** `identity-access-and-entitlements` fetched the Cox Bridge Federation page and quotes it; `case-cox-automotive` says the page could not be fetched. The reviewer of the case should align the case with the fetched evidence (cite the same URL and date) or hedge both identically.
19. **Awaiting-inspection status (*wachten op keuren*)** is withheld from RDW open data; `registration-and-trade-services` was corrected to use a live register query (editor fix, 22 Oct 2026).

31. **Entity–owner decisions (`vehicle-bounded-contexts`, board v1 of 3 June 2027).** Canonical where chapters differ: (a) **Stock unit vs stock item** — at DMS-connected rooftops the DMS owns the *accounting stock unit* (number, purchase cost, posting status); the dossier owns the *stock item* (the platform's record of the stay), linked by Hitch's xref; `dms-integration`'s "DMS owns stock unit existence and status" and `inventory-and-vehicle-dossier`'s "dossier owns stock item existence" are both correct about different entities. (b) **Tenant vs legal entity** — a stock item belongs to a tenant (the contracting organization) and is *owned by one legal entity* within it; `inventory-and-vehicle-dossier`'s "tenant (the legal entity that owns it)" should read that way. (c) **Golden record** — each attribute belongs to the context that owns its evidence (odometer → Tracewell, registration status → Turnstile, edition → Specbook); the golden record Inlet materializes beside Anchor is a projection; Anchor's write path is identity only. (d) **Authority register** is owned by the register-transactions context (Axleby Trade) and read by the access path as a gate (`identity-access-and-entitlements` lists it among its components; treat that as "consumed by"). (e) **Customer** = three entities: prospect (Switchboard, per legal entity), buyer/customer master (DMS when connected, else Sales Admin), tenant (tenant directory). (f) **Sold** follows item 24 (signing reserves; Sold on conditions met and invoice/delivery); `inventory-and-vehicle-dossier`'s state table (Reserved → Sold on "contract signed") still needs aligning.
