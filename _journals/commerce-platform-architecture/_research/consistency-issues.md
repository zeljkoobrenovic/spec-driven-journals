20. **GMV.** In this book, gross merchandise value (GMV) means the value of goods sold before refunds and returns. A company's reported GMV follows that company's own definition. For example, Shopify reports a figure net of refunds. When a page cites a company's GMV, it says "as <company> defines it". Pages are not reworded unless they conflict with this.
21. **SKU.** A stock-keeping unit (SKU) is the code that identifies one sellable variant (size × color). Saying "a SKU" for the variant itself is accepted shorthand, as the ledger uses it. Chapter 06 owns the precise definition, and the glossary follows chapter 06.
