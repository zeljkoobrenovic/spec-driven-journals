| **Industry practice** | What accommodation marketplaces commonly do, phrased as "commonly" or "a typical pattern", without invented percentages. | [[introduction]] |
| **Reference-architecture inference** | A plausible design derived from engineering principles. When it concerns a real company it is labeled "INFERENCE, NOT DOCUMENTED" and is never a description of that company's systems. | [[introduction]] |
| **Recommendation (evidence label)** | The author's architectural judgement, given with its reasoning and the conditions under which it does not hold. | [[introduction]] |
| **Illustration** | Anything about Corvane, the fictional travel group the book follows. Its numbers are invented for teaching and agree with one shared scenario ledger. | [[meet-corvane]] |
| **Failure pattern** | A named, recurring way stay platforms go wrong, with warning signs that can be checked in a design review and a note on which marketplace type it hits hardest. The book uses sixteen. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]] |
| **Phantom Availability** | Search shows rooms and prices that are gone or different at checkout, so "sold out" and "price changed" become steps in the funnel. Hits OTAs hardest, because they copy counted inventory and derived prices at extreme look-to-book ratios. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[availability-engine]] |
| **The Double-Booked Night** | One home or room is sold twice because calendars on different channels or services disagree for minutes or hours. Hits hosted stays hardest, where one unit has no spare room to absorb the error. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[calendar-sync]] |
| **Rate Plan Sprawl** | Properties carry dozens of rate plans and derived rates nobody can explain, and every new product adds another combination to cache, map and test. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[rates-and-availability-model]] |
| **Price Drift** | The price in search, on the property page, at checkout and on the card statement are four different numbers, because fees, taxes and currency are added in different places. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[property-page-and-room-selection]] |
| **The Same Hotel Twice** | One property appears as several products from different suppliers or channels, with mismatched rooms, content and prices. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[content-and-media]] |
| **Connectivity Roulette** | Partner integrations run without contracts, certification, ordering guarantees or health monitoring, so every rate update is a surprise. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[connectivity-and-channel-managers]] |
| **Lost at the Front Desk** | Reservations are sent to the property or host without acknowledgement or reconciliation, and guests arrive to "we have no booking for you". | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[reservation-lifecycle]] |
| **The Hotel-Shaped Home** | One supply model is forced onto both marketplaces: homes squeezed into room types and rate plans, or hotels flattened into one-unit calendars. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[property-and-listing-model]] |
| **Pay-to-Rank Creep** | Commission levels, programs and boosts accumulate in ranking until guests stop trusting the order and regulators ask for an explanation. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[stays-ranking]] |
| **Policy Spaghetti** | Cancellation, refund and change rules are encoded separately in checkout, support tools, payments and partner systems, and give different answers. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[changes-and-cancellations]] |
| **Ledgerless Money** | Money moves between guests, the marketplace, properties, hosts and tax authorities without one ledger that can say who is owed what. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[payouts-and-settlement]] |
| **The Tax Patchwork** | VAT, tourist taxes and platform reporting are added market by market as special cases, and nobody can say what was collected for whom. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[taxes-on-stays]] |
| **Trust After the Incident** | Verification, screening and protection arrive as reactions to incidents and headlines, each a separate tool with its own data. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[stays-trust-and-safety]] |
| **City-by-City Firefighting** | Each new short-term-rental rule is hand-coded as an emergency project, so compliance is always late and never reusable. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[stays-regulation]] |
| **Built for Sunny Days** | The platform handles normal booking traffic but not mass cancellations, rebookings, peaks or supplier outages, which arrive all at once. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[global-scale-and-reliability]] |
| **The Unwatched Model** | A pricing, ranking, screening or fraud model makes consequential decisions without evaluation, monitoring or an owner. | [[where-stay-platforms-go-wrong]], [[stays-ml-platform]] |
