## The group

- **Corvane Group** — a privately held travel group that runs **two marketplaces of different types** plus a connectivity business and a payments institution. Owned by two private-equity funds (combined majority) and the founders of Roomstead. Headquarters in **Lisbon** (since 2021); engineering hubs in **Dublin** (Roomstead), **Lisbon** (Nookaway and group functions), **Barcelona** (search, data and ML), **Kraków** (payments and platform) and **Utrecht** (Corvane Connect).
- **2026 figures (fiscal year = calendar year):** revenue **€1.692 billion**; adjusted EBITDA about **€410 million**; about **6,800 employees**, of whom about **2,300** work in product and technology (about **1,750 engineers**).

## The 2027 situation — problems Corvane starts with

1. **Two of almost everything.** Two identity systems, two trust stacks, two search engines, two experimentation platforms, two warehouses, two customer-service tools, three clouds.
2. **Roomstead's price accuracy** on metasearch is 91% against a 97% target; Pricegrid staleness is the main cause.
3. **Nookaway double bookings** from iCal-only multichannel listings (see the Seville double booking).
4. **Regulation:** the EU Short-Term Rental Regulation applies from 20 May 2026; Spain's registration rules forced listing removals; ViDA will make platforms the deemed VAT supplier for short-term accommodation from 2030 (writers: verify current dates).
5. **Merchant growth on legacy rails:** Roomstead's move toward 40% merchant bookings runs through Keystone's agency-era reservation model.
6. **Keystone**, the Java/Oracle monolith, still owns reservations, partner contracts and commission invoicing for Roomstead.

## The One Stay program

Approved in **April 2027** by the board on a proposal from Tobias Lindqvist (CTO) and Mariana Costa (Group Chief Architect); three-year horizon (2027–2030). Scope:

- **Shared:** Corvane ID (one identity across brands), payments and ledger (already shared; extend), one trust platform, one messaging and notifications platform, one data platform and metric layer, one customer-service platform, one tax engine.
- **Shared contracts, separate engines:** a common **offer** and **reservation** contract across both marketplaces; Roomstead keeps Tally/Pricegrid for room-type inventory, Nookaway keeps Almanac for one-unit calendars (the decision is argued in `converging-two-marketplaces`).
- **Not shared (for now):** supply models, connectivity (Switchyard vs Nookaway partner API), ranking models.
- Budget: **€145 million** over three years; target to retire Keystone's reservation module by **end of 2029**.

## Recurring worked examples (canonical numbers)

Reuse these when relevant; amounts and dates must match.

1. **The €12 rate at Hotel Miradouro (Lisbon).** Tuesday **9 March 2027**, 07:14–07:52 WET. Duarte's bulk rate update in the PMS for **14–16 May 2027** passed through Corvane Connect, where a decimal-mapping error in a newly mapped rate plan sent **€12.00** instead of **€120.00** for "Standard Double – Flexible" to Roomstead and two other channels. **41** Roomstead reservations (**63** room nights) were made in **38** minutes. Roomstead's price-anomaly check compared each rate only with the same rate plan's previous value, and the plan was new. Roomstead honoured all 41 reservations; Corvane Connect compensated the hotel for the difference: 63 × €108 = **€6,804**.
2. **The Seville double booking (Carmen's apartment).** On **2 February 2027** at 10:12 CET a Nookaway guest family (the Bauers, from Munich) booked **20–25 April 2027** (Feria week; 5 nights × €260 = €1,300 + €60 cleaning = **€1,360** before guest fees). At 10:40 a booking for 21–24 April arrived on the competing platform, which had last imported Nookaway's iCal export at 10:00. Nookaway's own import ran at 09:00 and 12:00. Carmen canceled the Nookaway reservation at 13:05. Nookaway's guest guarantee rebooked the Bauers in a comparable apartment for **€1,780**; Nookaway funded the **€420** difference; Carmen was charged a host cancellation fee of **€130** (10% of the nightly subtotal) from her next payout, and the dates were blocked.
3. **King's Day overbooking (Amsterdam).** **27 April 2027**. Hotel Prinsenkade's channel manager queued updates for **47 minutes** during an outage on **26 April**; the hotel ended **9 rooms** oversold across channels. **4** of the walked guests were Roomstead guests; Roomstead customer service relocated them to hotels in Haarlem and Amstelveen at a cost of **€2,150**, of which **€1,600** was recovered from the hotel under the partner terms.
4. **The Spanish registration purge.** In **2026**, under Spanish registration rules, Nookaway removed **6,900** Spanish listings without a valid registration number. Their future reservations were honoured if check-in was within 30 days (**2,900**) and canceled with full refund otherwise (**8,300**). (Writers: describe the real Spanish rules only from sources.)
5. **The Dubrovnik damage claim.** On **14 August 2027** a party took place at Ivan Horvat's villa despite reservation screening. Ivan claimed **€3,800**; after evidence review Nookaway Cover reimbursed **€3,450**; Nookaway recovered **€900** from the guest.
6. **The extranet phishing wave.** **November 2027**. Attackers phished staff credentials at **230** Roomstead partner properties and used the in-platform messaging to send fake "payment verification" messages to **4,100** guests. **310** guests paid the fraudsters; Roomstead reimbursed them **€212,000** in total (about €684 each).
7. **The 212 duplicate bookings.** **14 January 2027**: a PSP latency spike made Roomstead's mobile app retry checkout without reusing its idempotency key; **212** duplicate reservations were created, **198** of them with duplicate charges.
8. **The ATC strike weekend.** **5–7 June 2027**: an air-traffic-control strike over France disrupted travel across Europe. About **17,000** Roomstead and **2,600** Nookaway reservations were affected; Roomstead received **6,200** cancellation requests in 48 hours.
9. **The €1.8 million reconciliation gap.** In **Q1 2027** Corvane Payments found **€1.8 million** unreconciled between virtual-card issuing statements and Roomstead merchant reservations, mostly cards charged by hotels for reservations later canceled or modified.
10. **The Novak family's Lisbon trip.** On **11 February 2027** the Novaks searched for Lisbon, 2–9 August, 2 adults + 2 children, abandoned the search, received a price alert, and on **14 February** booked a Nookaway apartment in Alfama: 7 nights × **€145** = €1,015 + cleaning **€70** = €1,085 subtotal; guest service fee 12% = **€130.20**; Lisbon tourist tax **€4** per adult per night (children under 13 exempt; maximum 7 nights) = 2 × 7 × €4 = **€56**; total **€1,271.20**, **25%** above the €1,015 the nightly price suggested. (Writers: verify the Lisbon tax rate and rules in sources and say "as of" a date.)
11. **Black Friday 2027.** **26 November 2027**: Roomstead availability lookups peaked at **96,000/s** (1.5× the 2026 peak); Pricegrid's cache hit rate fell from 94% to 71% and Roomstead entered a planned degradation mode for 40 minutes.
