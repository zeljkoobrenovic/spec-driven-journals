# Spec: Team Charter

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I define teams before I staff or task them: every team I run has a
written charter answering who it serves, what it owes, and what it depends
on. The post turns two tools from the *Scaling People* companion workbooks
into an operating principle — the eight-section Team Charter template
(mission, vision, customers, metrics with targets, strategic importance,
major risks, provided interfaces, dependent interfaces) and the
Organizational Foundations self-test (the individual/team/division table of
mission, objectives with DRI, key metrics, accountability mechanisms, and
operating cadence). The load-bearing idea: the operating system is only real
if the foundations table can be filled in from documentation, not memory.

## Audience

Managers in my organization (so they know a written charter is the price of
running a team, not optional paperwork); the people on those teams (so they
know what a charter owes them: a mission, named customers, metrics with
targets, and declared dependencies); peer executives comparing operating
models. First-person declarative.

## Non-goals

- Not [[writing-okrs]] — that record covers setting objectives and key
  results; the charter is the durable identity those objectives ladder from.
- Not [[quarterly-business-reviews]] — that record covers the cadence where
  charter metrics and risks get inspected; this one covers writing them down.
- Not [[operating-principles]] — that record covers how I personally operate;
  this one covers how a team declares what it is.
- Not a team-design or topology exercise — the charter documents a team that
  exists (or is being founded); it does not decide how to slice teams.
- Two intentional cross-journal ties in Related Records: [[organizational-values]]
  (`grounded-engineering-executive` — a charter's mission/vision must not
  contradict company-wide values) and [[measuring-engineering-organizations]]
  (`grounded-engineering-executive` — the charter's metrics section is where
  that discipline starts at team scope).
