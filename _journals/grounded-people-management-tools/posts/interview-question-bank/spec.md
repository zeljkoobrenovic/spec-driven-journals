# Spec: Interview Question Bank

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I run interview questioning: from a shared, curated question bank,
not from each interviewer's improvisation. The post turns the *Scaling
People* "Sample Interview Questions" tool into an operating record: ask the
same questions of every candidate; mix situational, behavioral, and strict
competency questions; and never ask a question without knowing what a strong
answer sounds like. The bank has four groups — working with others, getting
work done, personal motivation, leadership — each question shipping with its
follow-ups and explicit "seeking" guidance, plus the chapter's
decision-making question with its Poor/Good/Strong rubric folded in as
leadership material.

## Audience

Anyone in my organization who sits on an interview loop (so they ask from
the bank and score against the "seeking" notes, not against likability);
hiring managers assembling loops; peer executives comparing hiring
practices. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states that consistency across
      candidates is what makes signal comparable, and that every question
      carries an explicit "seeking".
- [x] **All four groups survive** — working with others, getting work done,
      personal motivation, leadership — each question with its follow-ups
      and "seeking" guidance in the Checklist tab.
- [x] **The red flags survive** — "Nothing ever bothers me", complainers vs.
      fixers, and blame in the failed-project answer are named as signal.
- [x] **The rubric is folded in** — the decision-making question and its
      Poor/Good/Strong rubric (data, consultation, values, reflection)
      appear as the bank's leadership deep dive.
- [x] **The question-type mix is named** — situational, behavioral, and
      strict competency, with the workbook's own examples.
- [x] **Credit is explicit** — Claire Hughes Johnson's workbooks, with many
      questions credited to Stripe CTO David Singleton.

## Non-goals

- Not [[interview-loop]] — that record designs the loop itself (stages,
  interviewer assignments, focus areas); this one supplies the questions the
  loop asks.
- Not [[hiring-written-exercises]] — the written-exercise signal is its own
  tool; this bank covers live questioning.
- Not [[candidate-review]] — how the answers get debriefed and decided on is
  that record; this one ends when the feedback is written.
- Not the overall hiring system — that is [[hiring]] in the
  engineering-executive journal.
