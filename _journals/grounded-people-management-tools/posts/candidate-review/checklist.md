*The working tool behind this record. The Article tab carries the rationale and anti-patterns.*

## 1. Candidate submission

- [ ] Recruiter assembles the candidate packet before CR
- [ ] Packet includes the candidate's résumé
- [ ] Packet includes a completed scorecard for every interview
- [ ] Packet includes a completed hiring-committee scorecard
- [ ] Packet includes a suggested outcome (pick one):

| Suggested outcome | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Yes for company, no for my team | Should be very rare; requires a clear rationale |
| Company hire | Standard positive outcome |
| Team hire | Standard positive outcome, scoped to the requesting team |
| No hire | Standard negative outcome |

- [ ] Packet includes a suggested level
- [ ] Full packet is shared with every CR reviewer before the meeting
- [ ] Do not schedule CR review for a packet missing any of the above

## 2. Scorecard and hiring-manager rationale review

**Hiring-committee scorecard review**

- [ ] Read the hiring-committee scorecard first, before the rationale
- [ ] Confirm each interview has a score mapped to this scale:

| Score | Symbol meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Strong no | Clear negative signal |
| No | Negative signal |
| Neutral | No clear signal either way |
| Yes | Positive signal |
| Strong yes | Clear positive signal |

- [ ] Confirm each score maps directly to that interview's rubric
- [ ] Use this step to ground the review in the most objective evidence available
- [ ] Do not review any packet missing a completed hiring-committee scorecard

**Hiring-manager rationale review**

- [ ] Read the rationale for the more subjective observations from interviews and the hiring committee
- [ ] Check that the rationale explains how the committee reached its decision
- [ ] Pay particular attention when the committee's decision diverges from what the objective scorecards alone would suggest
- [ ] Confirm the rationale and/or the "resolved FUD" (fear, uncertainty, doubt) section calls out any such divergence early
- [ ] Treat early-called-out divergence as the mechanism that reduces packets sent back for more information
- [ ] Accept a brief rationale ("clear no hire" / "clear hire") when the outcome is obvious
- [ ] Require a fuller rationale when the outcome is not obvious or does not match the objective scorecard outcome

## 3. Scorecard verification

For each individual interview scorecard, confirm it:

- [ ] Reflects and is related to the rubric
- [ ] Is not based on subjective measures (subjective observations belong in the write-up and discussion, not the scorecard outcome)
- [ ] Is not too lenient
- [ ] Is not too strict
- [ ] Is correct — it reflects the rubric accurately

## 4. Discussion, decision, and résumé review

**Before discussion, confirm the panel has:**

- [ ] A sense of the expected CR outcome based on the objective interview outcomes in the hiring-committee scorecard and the rubric items from the individual scorecards
- [ ] A sense of any relevant subjective information from the individual scorecard write-ups and hiring-manager rationale

**Discussion**

- [ ] CR lead opens a discussion to clarify any FUD or disagreement
- [ ] CR lead identifies whether any feedback should go back to the hiring committee

**Résumé and leveling check**

- [ ] Group reviews the candidate's résumé
- [ ] Verify the suggested outcome makes sense given the résumé
- [ ] Verify the suggested level roughly aligns with the org's years-of-experience leveling guidance (engineering levels framework)

**Panel agrees; CR lead selects one final outcome:**

| Final outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Hire at suggested level | Panel agrees with the submitted level |
| Hire at higher level | Panel raises the level |
| Hire at lower level | Panel lowers the level |
| No hire: committee outcome overturned | Panel reverses the hiring committee's recommendation |
| No hire: committee outcome confirmed | Panel confirms the hiring committee's recommendation |
| TBD: sent back for additional information | Panel cannot decide without more information |

## 5. Submit CR outcome with feedback

- [ ] CR admin submits a scorecard for the CR review itself
- [ ] CR admin emails feedback to interviewers
- [ ] CR admin emails feedback to recruiters
- [ ] CR admin emails feedback to hiring managers
- [ ] Interviewers, recruiters, and hiring managers are invited to give feedback on CR via a feedback form
- [ ] Treat the feedback form as closing the loop — its purpose is continual iteration and improvement of the CR process itself
