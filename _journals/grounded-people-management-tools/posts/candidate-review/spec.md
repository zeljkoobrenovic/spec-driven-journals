# Spec: Candidate Review

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I run Candidate Review (CR): a committee, not a single hiring
manager, gives final approval on every hire and every level — reviewing the
scorecards, the hiring-manager rationale, and the résumé against
years-of-experience leveling guidance before anyone is offered a job. The
post carries the operating principle — objective rubric evidence gets read
before subjective narrative, and leveling is checked for consistency across
the org, not decided team by team. The Checklist tab is the runnable
five-step tool: candidate submission, scorecard and rationale review,
scorecard verification, discussion and decision, and outcome submission with
closed-loop feedback.

## Audience

Hiring managers and CR panel members in my organization (so they know what a
complete packet contains and what the committee is checking for); recruiters
who assemble and submit packets; interviewers and hiring managers who receive
CR's feedback; peer executives comparing hiring-governance models.
First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states that CR is both a hiring
      process and an equity/consistency process, and that rubric evidence is
      read before narrative.
- [x] **The five steps survive in order** — submission → scorecard and
      rationale review → scorecard verification → discussion, decision, and
      résumé check → outcome submission with feedback.
- [x] **The numbers and mechanics survive** — the four suggested outcomes
      (yes-for-company-no-for-my-team as rare with clear rationale, company
      hire, team hire, no hire), the four scorecard scores (strong no, no,
      neutral, yes, strong yes as symbols), the six final CR outcomes (hire
      at suggested/higher/lower level, no hire with committee outcome
      overturned, no hire with committee outcome confirmed, sent back for
      more information), and the "no completed scorecard, no review" rule.
- [x] **The objective-first move is explicit** — scorecard scores mapped to
      the rubric are read before the hiring-manager rationale, and the
      rationale exists specifically to explain divergence and resolved FUD.
- [x] **The checklist is runnable** — a hiring manager or CR panel member can
      run a review end to end from the Checklist tab alone.
- [x] **Credit is explicit** — How to Read This and References name Claire
      Hughes Johnson's *Scaling People* companion workbooks as the source of
      the framework.

## Non-goals

- Not [[interview-loop]] — that record is the loop design that produces the
  scorecards; this one starts once the loop has already run.
- Not [[hiring-written-exercises]] — the written-exercise scoring is one
  input the scorecard may carry; this record does not re-derive it.
- Not [[calibrating-your-standards]] (engineering journal) — that record is
  the ongoing bar-setting practice; CR is the per-candidate committee gate
  that enforces a bar, not the practice of calibrating it.
- Not a leveling framework in its own right — CR checks résumé years of
  experience against the org's leveling guidance; it does not define that
  guidance.
