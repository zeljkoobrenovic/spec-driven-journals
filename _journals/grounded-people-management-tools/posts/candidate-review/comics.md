A single hiring manager should never give the final yes alone — Candidate Review is the committee gate that reads evidence before narrative and checks the level before the offer goes out, in eight panels.

**Panel 1:** *The hook: a hiring manager alone is a single point of bias, however well-intentioned.*

![Comic panel: two differently-set bars, one low and easy, one high and strict, with Noa standing confused between them.](assets/images/candidate-review/comic-02-inconsistent-bar.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: without a shared gate, one team's leniency becomes another team's inconsistency.*

![Comic panel: Noa absorbed in a glowing narrative document while a rubric scorecard sits ignored and dim on the table.](assets/images/candidate-review/comic-03-narrative-first.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: read the story first, and the scores become confirmation instead of evidence.*

![Comic panel: Vera pointing to a glowing rubric scorecard placed in front of a narrative document, teaching Noa the order.](assets/images/candidate-review/comic-04-objective-before-subjective.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: objective scorecard evidence gets read before subjective narrative — always.*

![Comic panel: Noa walking a five-step path on the floor, checking items off a clipboard, with Vera walking alongside.](assets/images/candidate-review/comic-05-five-step-checklist.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: submission, scorecard review, verification, discussion, and outcome — in that order.*

![Comic panel: close-up of a clipboard showing a five-symbol scale from strong no to strong yes with Vera tapping the middle symbol.](assets/images/candidate-review/comic-06-strong-no-to-strong-yes.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: strong no, no, neutral, yes, strong yes — each score mapped directly to the rubric.*

![Comic panel: Vera and Noa checking a stack of scorecards against a rubric sheet, spotting one that glows a different color, both looking tired.](assets/images/candidate-review/comic-07-verification-catches-drift.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: verifying every scorecard against the rubric is real work — and it is what keeps the bar honest.*

![Comic panel: Noa sending feedback envelopes to three figures representing interviewers, recruiters, and hiring managers, while Vera checks the last clipboard box.](assets/images/candidate-review/comic-08-loop-closes.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: the decision is not the finish line — the loop closes when feedback lands.*
