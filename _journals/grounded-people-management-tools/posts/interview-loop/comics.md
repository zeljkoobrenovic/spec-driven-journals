![Comic panel: Noa stands outside an interview room holding a nearly blank clipboard, unsure what to ask.](assets/images/interview-loop/comic-01-walking-in-blind.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: no interviewer should be improvising the questions in the room.*

![Comic panel: two interviewers ask the same question in overlapping bubbles while a resilience checkbox stays empty.](assets/images/interview-loop/comic-02-gaps-and-overlap.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: without a stage map, two interviewers cover the same ground and one competency goes unchecked.*

![Comic panel: Noa writes a rubric on a notepad only after the candidate has left, with erased scores nearby.](assets/images/interview-loop/comic-03-rubric-after-the-fact.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: a rubric written after the answer is not a rubric, it is a feeling.*

![Comic panel: Vera draws a five-link chain on a whiteboard — competency, stage, question, follow-ups, rubric.](assets/images/interview-loop/comic-04-the-design-chain.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: competency to stage to question to follow-ups to rubric, all written down before scheduling starts.*

![Comic panel: a wall timeline shows a recruiter screen, a team screen with a written project, and four onsite boxes.](assets/images/interview-loop/comic-05-the-stage-map.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: a screen, a team screen paired with a written project, and four onsites, each owning named competencies.*

![Comic panel: a scorecard shows structure, grammar, and tone scored 1 to 3 with pass, second-reviewer, and fail bands.](assets/images/interview-loop/comic-06-the-scoring-band.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: 1–3 per area, three areas, and the band decides — 7–9 passes, 5–6 gets a second reviewer, 3–4 fails.*

![Comic panel: Noa writes a tall stack of Situation, Action, Result follow-up questions late at night.](assets/images/interview-loop/comic-07-the-cost-of-rigor.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: writing every follow-up and rubric in advance is real work — the price of comparable scores across candidates.*

![Comic panel: Noa confidently holds up four papers — competency list, stage map, questions, rubrics — at the interview door.](assets/images/interview-loop/comic-08-four-artifacts-go-live.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: no loop goes live without a competency list, a stage map, questions, and rubrics, whatever the calendar says.*
