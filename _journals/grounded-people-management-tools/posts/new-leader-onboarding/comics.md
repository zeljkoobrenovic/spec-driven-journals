![Comic panel: Noa sits buried behind a towering stack of folders all dumped onto the desk in week one.](assets/images/new-leader-onboarding/comic-03-week-one-cram.jpeg)

![Comic panel: Vera points to a whiteboard listing five named onboarding roles while Noa checks them off a clipboard.](assets/images/new-leader-onboarding/comic-04-named-cast.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The tool: a New Leader Experience with a named cast, not an implied one.*

![Comic panel: Vera and Noa stand beside a long wall timeline showing phased onboarding stages from offer acceptance to week nine.](assets/images/new-leader-onboarding/comic-05-phased-timeline.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: pre-populated, phase by phase, so nobody invents it under pressure.*

![Comic panel: Noa fills out a short personality inventory form marked week four while Vera nods reassuringly.](assets/images/new-leader-onboarding/comic-06-week-four-assessment.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: a leadership assessment at week four — a mirror, not a gate.*

![Comic panel: Noa writes quickly in a notebook labeled First Impressions while text on an earlier page visibly fades.](assets/images/new-leader-onboarding/comic-07-fading-notebook.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: fresh outside perspective decays fast, so capturing it is a scheduled job, not an afterthought.*

![Comic panel: Vera hands Noa a one-page coaching goals document at the ninety-day mark while a new coach waves from the doorway.](assets/images/new-leader-onboarding/comic-08-coaching-goals.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: at ninety days, first impressions become coaching goals — and the loop closes.*
