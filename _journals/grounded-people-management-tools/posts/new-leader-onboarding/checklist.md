## 1. Before you start: assign the cast

*The hiring manager is already in place from the hire — no action needed here.*

- [ ] Assign a people partner
- [ ] Assign an onboarding point person to coordinate the mechanics
- [ ] Assign a spin-up buddy — a manager or team member who answers day-to-day questions
- [ ] Assign a guide — an expert **outside** the new leader's own team who offers a safe space for questions and observations
- [ ] Confirm the new leader will meet all five before or during week 1

## 2. When the new leader accepts the offer

| Who | What |
| --- | --- |
| Recruiter | Send the leadership hiring welcome/onboarding email, including an onboarding 101 FAQ sheet and upcoming vacations |

## 3. ~2 weeks before start date

| Who | What |
| --- | --- |
| Onboarding point person | Confirm any unique needs for the new leader with the leadership recruiter |
| Onboarding point person | Send a welcome email introducing the new leader to their onboarding support team |
| Hiring manager + people partner | Assign the onboarding point person (can be admin support) |
| Onboarding point person | Populate the new-leader checklist and coordinate across the onboarding support team — buddy, guide, and hiring manager (async via Slack and/or meetings is fine) |
| Onboarding point person + new leader | Schedule the full meeting sequence below (week 1 through week 9+) |

- [ ] Recruiter has sent the leadership welcome/onboarding email
- [ ] Onboarding point person confirmed unique needs with the recruiter
- [ ] Onboarding point person sent the welcome email to the support team
- [ ] Hiring manager and people partner assigned the onboarding point person
- [ ] Onboarding point person populated the checklist and coordinated the cast
- [ ] Full meeting sequence (weeks 1–9+) is scheduled in advance

## 4. Scheduled meeting sequence

- [ ] **Week 1**: manager, onboarding buddy, guide, and people partner — weekly, for four weeks
- [ ] **Weeks 2–4**: team deep dives, new direct reports, new skip-level reports, finance business partner, recruiting partner; add the new leader to the team's all-hands meeting
- [ ] **Week 4**: people-transition meetings with new direct reports and their former managers; possibly take the Hogan assessment and review results
- [ ] **Weeks 5–8**: key partners, other divisional leaders, team immersion with the people partner; add to company-wide leadership and manager forums; meet with the leadership recruiter; add a reminder to document first impressions and reflections
- [ ] **Week 9 onward**: reminders to create a personal development plan and a "Working with Me" document; share 90-day reflections

## 5. ~1 week before start date

| Who | What |
| --- | --- |

- [ ] Hiring manager sent the company-wide introduction email

## 6. First week (leader also joins company-wide spin-up/onboarding sessions)

| Who | What |
| --- | --- |
| People partner or onboarding point person | Send the leader their welcome doc: onboarding support team, this checklist, important 1:1s, team deep dives, suggested reading (company and division level), suggested comms channels (Slack channels, email groups) |

- [ ] Welcome doc sent, covering support team, checklist, 1:1s, deep dives, reading, and comms channels
- [ ] New leader added to company-wide spin-up/onboarding sessions

## 7. ~3 months in

| Who | What |
| --- | --- |
| People partner | Conduct 360° review interviews, synthesize findings, discuss and identify goals for coaching |
| Org dev | Complete the coach-matching process |
| Coach | Run a six-month coaching engagement, starting with the first session |

- [ ] 360° review interviews conducted
- [ ] Findings synthesized into a written set of coaching goals
- [ ] Coach matched by org dev
- [ ] First session of the six-month coaching engagement scheduled
