# Spec: New Leader Onboarding

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I onboard a new leader as a designed experience rather than a
calendar of ad-hoc intros: a named support cast, a prescheduled sequence of
meetings and pre-reads from offer acceptance through roughly three months, a
leadership assessment, six months of coaching, and a 360° review at the
90-day mark that turns first impressions into coaching goals. The post turns
the workbook's New Leader Experience (NLE) into an operating principle —
senior hires fail from missing context and missing feedback, not missing
skill, so both get engineered in rather than left to chance.

## Audience

Hiring managers bringing in a new leader (so they own the spin-up, not just
the offer); people partners and onboarding point people (so the phased
schedule and named roles are unambiguous); the new leader themselves (so they
know what support to expect and by when); peer executives comparing
onboarding models. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states that senior hires fail
      from missing context and missing feedback, not missing skill, and that
      onboarding is a designed experience with named owners and a 90-day
      feedback loop.
- [ ] **The support cast is named** — hiring manager, people partner,
      onboarding point person, spin-up buddy, and guide, each with a distinct
      job.
- [ ] **The phased schedule survives** — offer acceptance through week 1,
      weeks 2–4, week 4, weeks 5–8, week 9+, and ~3 months, with the WHO and
      WHAT preserved for each phase.
- [ ] **The mechanics survive** — welcome emails, the Hogan Personality
      Inventory, six months of coaching, first-month actions, the 90-day
      360° review synthesized into coaching goals, coach matching, and the
      first coaching session.
- [ ] **The checklist is runnable** — the phased WHO/WHAT table is
      reproduced as an operational checklist in the Checklist tab
      (`checklist.md`).
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — References name Claire Hughes Johnson's
      *Scaling People* companion workbooks as the source of the NLE tool.

## Non-goals

- Not [[manager-transitions]] — that record covers a manager moving into a
  new role or team; this one covers onboarding a leader hired or promoted
  into the seat, cast and schedule included.
- Not [[working-with-me]] — that record is the document the new leader (and
  everyone else) writes about themselves; the NLE schedule references it as
  a week 9+ deliverable but does not define its shape.
- Not [[career-conversations]] — the 90-day 360° review here feeds coaching
  goals, not a career or promotion discussion.
- Not [[engineering-onboarding]] — that record covers onboarding engineers
  into a codebase and team; this one covers onboarding a *leader* into
  context, relationships, and judgment.
- No claim that the NLE replaces line management — the hiring manager still
  manages; the NLE is the scaffolding around the first 90 days.
