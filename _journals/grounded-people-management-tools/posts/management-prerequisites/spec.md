# Spec: Management Prerequisites

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State the entry bar I hold myself and every manager in my organization to:
before any of the advanced tools in this journal apply, the baseline
management hygiene must be in place. The post turns the Management
Prerequisites Checklist from *Scaling People*'s companion workbooks into an
operating principle across its five layers — knowing your legal
responsibilities as a manager; the individual-report basics (regular,
rarely-rescheduled 1:1s with two-sided agendas, quarterly goals tracked
together, bidirectional feedback every three to six months, formal
performance conversations every 3, 6, or 12 months covering both results and
*how*); team meetings that earn their synchronous time; division-level
infrastructure (job descriptions, interview rubrics, ladders and levels,
compensation philosophy); and the same bar applied upward to my own job
description, goals, 1:1s, and access to company context.

## Audience

Every manager in my organization (so they know the non-negotiable baseline
before we discuss anything more advanced); their reports (so they know what
they are entitled to expect from any manager here); me, auditing myself
against the same list. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states that none of the advanced
      tools work until the basics are in place, and that this checklist is
      the entry bar for every manager including me.
- [x] **The cadences survive** — weekly/biweekly 1:1s rarely rescheduled,
      quarterly goals tracked together, bidirectional feedback every 3–6
      months, formal performance conversations every 3/6/12 months (more
      often the faster the company changes), weekly/biweekly team meetings.
- [x] **Results AND how** — formal performance conversations explicitly
      cover both what the person achieved and how they achieved it.
- [x] **All five layers present** — legal responsibilities, individual
      reports, teams, division infrastructure, and the manager's own
      upward hygiene each get explicit treatment.
- [x] **The checklist survives intact** — the workbook checklist's five
      groups reproduced as the runnable tool in the Checklist tab
      (`checklist.md`).
- [x] **Credit is explicit** — How to Read This and References name Claire
      Hughes Johnson's *Scaling People* companion workbooks as the source.

## Non-goals

- Not [[performance-reviews]] — that record runs the formal review cycle;
  this one only establishes that the cycle exists on a cadence.
- Not [[writing-okrs]] — that record covers how to write quarterly goals;
  this one only requires that goals exist and are tracked together.
- Not [[compensation-conversations]] — that record covers the conversation;
  this one only requires the division-level philosophy and framework behind it.
- Not [[interview-loop]] — that record designs the loop; this one only
  requires job descriptions and rubrics to exist for open roles.
- Not a legal-compliance guide — knowing your jurisdiction's manager
  obligations is required here; enumerating them is not attempted.
