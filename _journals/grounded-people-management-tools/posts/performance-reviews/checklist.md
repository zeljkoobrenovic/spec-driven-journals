*The working tool behind this record. The Article tab carries the rationale and anti-patterns.*

## 1. Header

- [ ] Name
- [ ] Current level
- [ ] Start date in role
- [ ] Time present during the review period, noting any leave and whether the period was less than a half-year
- [ ] Proposed designation
- [ ] Proposed promotion: yes or no
- [ ] One sentence: why not a higher designation? (N/A only for "greatly exceeds")
- [ ] One sentence: why not a lower designation? (N/A only for "does not meet")
- [ ] One sentence: the takeaway I want this person to leave the review with — communicate it to them during the review

## 3. Strengths

Identify 1–2 strengths demonstrated during the review period. Consider, but don't limit to, the core capabilities of the role; for people managers, weigh the manager ladder and manager principles too.

- [ ] Strength 1 described briefly
- [ ] Approximate designation for strength 1 (relative to current level)
- [ ] Summary for strength 1, referencing 1–2 supporting examples
- [ ] Strength 2 described briefly (if applicable)
- [ ] Approximate designation for strength 2 (relative to current level)
- [ ] Summary for strength 2, referencing 1–2 supporting examples

## 4. Development areas

Identify 1–2 development areas demonstrated during the review period. If the person did well overall, these may still be rated "successfully meets" or higher; if not, they may be "partially meets" or "does not meet" relative to current level. Consider, but don't limit to, the core capabilities of the role.

- [ ] Development area 1 described briefly
- [ ] Approximate designation for development area 1 (relative to current level)
- [ ] Summary for development area 1, referencing 1–2 supporting examples
- [ ] Concrete ideas for how to improve, for development area 1
- [ ] Development area 2 described briefly (if applicable)
- [ ] Approximate designation for development area 2 (relative to current level)
- [ ] Summary for development area 2, referencing 1–2 supporting examples
- [ ] Concrete ideas for how to improve, for development area 2

## 5. Promotion proposal: yes or no

### If no

- [ ] State when the person might be ready to uplevel: this half (H1), next half (H2), or later
- [ ] Briefly state what they need to do for promotion in the future, or for higher levels, to grow in their career

### If yes

- [ ] State how long the person has been demonstrating next-level capabilities and behaviors
- [ ] Give 1–2 examples of how the person has been demonstrating next-level capabilities and behaviors
- [ ] In 1–2 sentences, name the primary development area at the next level — framed as proactively identifying the person's next step, not as a promotion blocker

## 6. Before the review conversation

- [ ] Header, impact, strengths, development areas, and promotion proposal are all complete
- [ ] Both edge sentences (why not higher, why not lower) are written for the proposed designation
- [ ] The one-sentence takeaway is written and ready to say out loud
- [ ] The review is ready to defend in calibration, not only to deliver to the person
