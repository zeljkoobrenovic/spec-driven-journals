# Spec: Performance Reviews

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I write performance reviews so they serve two audiences at once —
the person being reviewed and the calibration room that follows. The post
turns the workbook's Performance Review Template into an operating tool:
header discipline (level, start date, time present net of leave), an explicit
proposed designation and promotion call, the why-not-higher/why-not-lower
edge discipline that forces an honest designation, the 2–3 highest-impact
areas with context, difficulty, duration, collaborators, role/expectation,
and resulting impact against expectation (company-building work included when
significant), 1–2 strengths and 1–2 development areas each with an
approximate designation and supporting examples, and the promotion-proposal
branch logic — if no, a readiness timeline and what to demonstrate; if yes,
how long next-level behavior has been shown plus the primary next-level
development area, framed as proactive next-step identification rather than a
blocker.

## Audience

Managers in my organization writing a half's performance review (so the
review is defensible before it reaches calibration, not just readable);
people preparing to receive a review (so they know the document has a fixed,
evidence-based shape); peer executives comparing calibration practices.
First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states the why-not-higher/why-not-lower
      discipline and the one-sentence takeaway requirement in one paragraph.
- [x] **Header discipline is explicit** — level, start date, and time present
      net of leave precede any narrative or designation.
- [x] **The edge test survives** — one sentence each on why not a higher and
      why not a lower designation, with the two true N/A exemptions
      ("greatly exceeds" / "does not meet") stated correctly.
- [x] **Impact is evidence-shaped** — 2–3 areas each carry context/difficulty,
      duration, collaborators, role/expectation, and resulting impact vs.
      expectations with metrics; company-building work (mentorship,
      recruiting, DEI) is explicitly counted.
- [x] **Strengths and development areas are structured** — 1–2 each, with an
      approximate designation relative to level and 1–2 supporting examples;
      development areas add concrete improvement ideas.
- [x] **Promotion logic survives intact** — the no-branch (readiness timing,
      what to demonstrate) and yes-branch (duration and examples of
      next-level behavior, next-level development area framed as proactive,
      not a blocker) are both fully reproduced in the checklist.

## Non-goals

- Not [[career-conversations]] — that record covers the trajectory
  conversation; this one covers the half's written verdict and its
  calibration defense.
- Not [[compensation-conversations]] — comp decisions consume this review's
  outputs but are not defined here.
- Not [[managing-underperformance]] — that record covers the process once a
  designation trends toward "does not meet"; this one covers writing any
  review, including strong ones.
- Not the level ladder or calibration process itself — those are
  [[careers-and-performance]] and [[calibrating-your-standards]] in the
  engineering journal; this record is one manager's input into both.
