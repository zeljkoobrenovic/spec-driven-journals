**Panel 1:** *The hook: a stuck disagreement doesn't disappear — it just goes quiet.*

![Comic panel: Noa hides her clipboard behind her back near a manager's door marked with a red X.](assets/images/unblocking-process/comic-02-escalation-as-shame.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: most teams treat escalating as an admission of failure.*

![Comic panel: Vera and Noa stand exhausted on either side of a whiteboard full of crossed-out arguments, a fast-spinning clock behind them.](assets/images/unblocking-process/comic-03-debate-to-a-standstill.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: arguing a reversible call to a standstill instead of shipping and iterating.*

![Comic panel: Vera holds up a notebook page reading UNBLOCKING = DUTY as Noa stands up straighter.](assets/images/unblocking-process/comic-04-duty-not-defeat.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: instigating the unblocking process is a duty, not a defeat.*

![Comic panel: Noa and a colleague from another team co-write a half-page document with three short fields.](assets/images/unblocking-process/comic-05-half-page-together.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: both parties co-write one half-page document — goal, options, trade-offs.*

![Comic panel: Vera sends the document to two managers by email under a wall clock counting down five marked days.](assets/images/unblocking-process/comic-06-five-day-clock.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: one email to both managers, a five-business-day clock starts ticking.*

![Comic panel: Noa holds up two fingers knocking on a door while Vera stands arms crossed behind a gate with three checkboxes.](assets/images/unblocking-process/comic-07-invite-twice-then-gate.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: unilateral only after two invitations — and managers must run the gate before listening. Even three yeses don't end it: the manager still messages the missing colleague and invites them in.*

![Comic panel: Vera and Noa shake hands over a document stamped DECIDED, with the once-locked door now open behind them.](assets/images/unblocking-process/comic-08-decided-and-documented.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: a decision, written down and committed to, beats a disagreement left to rot.*
