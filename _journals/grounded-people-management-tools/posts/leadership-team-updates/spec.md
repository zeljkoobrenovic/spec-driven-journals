# Spec: Leadership Team Updates

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I run my leadership team's recurring meeting cadence: on a written
pre-read with a hard deadline, not on live status reporting. The post turns
the "Leadership Team Snippets and Updates" workbook template into an
operating principle — the Sunday-night deadline, actions tracked by name and
due date in a shared spreadsheet, a timed standing agenda that culminates in
a large block for top goals and strategic priorities, four standing
questions asked every meeting (including what must be communicated down to
wider leadership), a standing customer issues/wins section, and a
per-executive snippet covering action updates, whereabouts, discussion
topics, initiative status, customer notes, launch/PR plans, and top-talent
news.

## Audience

Leadership team members and executives who submit and read the weekly
snippet (so they know the deadline and the fields are non-negotiable);
managers building or fixing their own leadership team's cadence; peer
executives comparing operating models. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states the pre-read runs on a
      hard Sunday-night deadline and that the meeting explicitly decides what
      gets communicated down, in one paragraph.
- [x] **The mechanics survive** — 10 p.m. Sunday deadline, action tracker
      with name/action/due date, the full timed agenda (actions 5 min, user
      guest 10 min, metrics/launch review 30 min alternating weekly,
      recruiting 10 min, internal audit quarterly 10 min, CEO topic 10 min,
      top goals and strategic priorities 60–120 min).
- [x] **The four standing questions are named explicitly**, including the
      downward-communication question as the load-bearing one.
- [x] **Customer issues and wins have owners** — both sections named with
      the requirement for an owner/contributor.
- [x] **The per-executive snippet fields are complete** — action updates,
      whereabouts, discussion topics and why, initiative status/learnings,
      customer meeting notes, launch/PR plans, top talent.
- [x] **The checklist survives intact** — pre-read fields, timed agenda
      table, standing questions, and snippet fields reproduced in the
      Checklist tab (`checklist.md`).

## Non-goals

- Not [[quarterly-business-reviews]] — that record covers the deeper
  business-health review this cadence draws its weekly reflections from;
  this one covers the leadership team's own recurring meeting.
- Not [[team-offsites]] — that record covers the less-frequent, larger-scope
  strategic session; this one covers the weekly-to-biweekly operating
  rhythm between offsites.
- Not a general meetings policy — the engineering-journal [[meetings]]
  record covers what makes any meeting worth holding; this record is the
  leadership-team-specific pre-read and agenda template.
- No claim this replaces async communication tools — the spreadsheet and
  snippet are the source of truth; this record does not prescribe which
  software hosts them.
