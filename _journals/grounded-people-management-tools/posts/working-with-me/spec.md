# Spec: Working with Me

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State why I write and keep current a "Working with Me" document — a short,
first-person guide to my operating cadence, management style, and
communication and feedback norms — grounded in Claire Hughes Johnson's
"Working with Claire" and its companion "Working with Me Template." The post
argues that writing the document is a self-awareness exercise as much as a
communication device, and that it only functions as intended if the people
who report to me can quote it back: the cadence (1:1s, team meetings,
quarterly planning, an early career session, a quarterly personal-goals
review), the decision style (collaborative but slow, with an explicit
escape hatch for urgent calls; hands-off by default, hands-on on purpose
early in a new initiative; decisions handed back to the person), the
communication contract (email read within 18 hours, respond only if asked,
resend if owed a response, "FYI = no response required"), and the feedback
commitment (redirect venting into direct conversation; measure, measure,
measure).

## Audience

Managers in my organization deciding whether to write their own version
(so they see the exercise as self-awareness, not paperwork); people joining
my team who will receive this document in their first week (so they know
what it commits me to and what it asks of them); peer executives comparing
onboarding practices. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states the document is "a
      self-awareness exercise as much as a communication device" and that
      "it is real only if my reports can quote it back to me."
- [x] **The cadence survives with numbers** — 1:1 frequency, weekly team
      meetings, quarterly planning, the early career session, and the
      quarterly review of 3–5 personal goals.
- [x] **The decision style is stated honestly** — collaborative-but-slow
      with the "if you need a decision quickly, make sure I know it"
      escape hatch; hands-off by default, hands-on early in a new
      initiative, decisions handed back with "what do you want to do?"
- [x] **The communication contract is explicit** — 18-hour email read
      window, respond-only-if-asked, resend-if-owed, "FYI = no response
      required."
- [x] **The feedback redirect rule is named** — venting about a colleague
      gets turned into help telling them directly, not held.
- [x] **The checklist reproduces the actual template** — My role /
      About me / Operating approach / Management style / Supporting you
      and your team, reproduced as a fillable, runnable tool.
- [x] **The checklist operationalizes the cadence and communication
      mechanics** — the numbered commitments from "Working with Claire"
      (cadence table, communication contract, feedback redirect) turned
      into concrete, checkable items, not just the section headings.
