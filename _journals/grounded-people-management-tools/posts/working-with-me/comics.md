![Comic panel: Noa at a new desk on day one, checking items off the Working with Me document alongside the checklist.](assets/images/working-with-me/comic-05-first-week-handoff.jpeg)

![Comic panel: Vera points at a wall clock marked 18 hours beside an inbox, explaining the FYI rule to Noa.](assets/images/working-with-me/comic-06-eighteen-hour-rule.jpeg)
