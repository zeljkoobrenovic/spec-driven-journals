## 1. Current manager previews the transition

- [ ] Meet with the individual one-on-one, before anyone else is looped in
- [ ] Let them know about the transition
- [ ] Confirm the formal date of the move in the HR system
- [ ] Confirm when the new manager will gain access to their information and performance reviews
- [ ] Give a heads-up that a transition meeting with them and their new manager is coming
- [ ] State that the meeting will cover current role and projects, plus strengths and development plans
- [ ] Answer or acknowledge any questions the person raises

## 2. Current manager schedules the three-way transition meeting

- [ ] Invite the employee and the new manager
- [ ] Allow enough time to cover all agenda topics and for questions
- [ ] Send the meeting invite using the agenda template below

### Transition meeting email template

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| To | Employee |
| From | Current manager |
| Cc | New manager |
| Subject | Manager transition meeting |

> I'm looking forward to the transition meeting on [date]. The main goal is to make sure [new manager] is well-informed by both of us on your work scope and work in flight, and has an overview of past performance and development conversations we've had. Let's plan to cover the following:

- [ ] **Current scope** — employee shares current work responsibilities, projects, and goals; current manager adds anything that might help the new manager
- [ ] **Strengths and development areas** — employee shares thoughts on their own strengths and areas for development; current manager adds what the two of them have discussed and worked on together
- [ ] **Supporting ambitions** — three-way discussion on how the new manager can support the employee's ambitions and development areas
- [ ] **Ways of working and feedback** — employee shares preferred ways of working for the new manager to hear and reinforce (or learn from); employee also shares any feedback on the current manager that would be useful for the new manager to hear
- [ ] **Next steps** — all three recap the concrete next steps that came out of the discussion

> Let me know if you have questions. I look forward to the discussion.

## 3. New manager follows up

- [ ] Follow up on any action items that came out of the transition meeting
- [ ] Take ownership of the relationship going forward
- [ ] Verify that access to the employee's information and past reviews (promised in the preview conversation) is actually in place once the HR-system date passes
- [ ] Reconnect the employee's development plan to their stated ambitions from the transition meeting
