![Comic panel: Noa stands at a new desk holding an empty folder while a stack of old development files sits abandoned behind them.](assets/images/manager-transitions/comic-01-restart-from-zero.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: a manager change can quietly erase months of context.*

![Comic panel: Vera taps her temple with a thought bubble showing scope, strengths, and ambitions stored only in her mind.](assets/images/manager-transitions/comic-02-context-in-one-head.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: context lives in one manager's head, and heads don't hand themselves over.*

![Comic panel: Noa reads a calendar invite with a new manager already cc'd while Vera walks away without a word.](assets/images/manager-transitions/comic-03-cold-cc-email.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: a silent handoff, where the person is the last to know.*

![Comic panel: Vera draws three connected whiteboard boxes labeled Preview, Three-Way Meeting, and Follow-Up while Noa watches.](assets/images/manager-transitions/comic-04-three-step-sequence.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: a deliberate three-step handoff, not a passive introduction.*

![Comic panel: Vera sits one-on-one with an employee in a quiet room, speaking warmly before anyone else is looped in.](assets/images/manager-transitions/comic-05-preview-conversation.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: the current manager previews the move, one-on-one, first.*

![Comic panel: Noa holds up a clipboard with a five-point numbered agenda during a three-way meeting with Vera and a new manager.](assets/images/manager-transitions/comic-06-five-point-agenda.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: one meeting, three people, five agenda points, always the same order.*

![Comic panel: an employee speaks candidly about the outgoing manager at the three-way table while Vera listens and Noa takes notes.](assets/images/manager-transitions/comic-07-hardest-agenda-item.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: honest feedback on the old manager is required, not optional.*

![Comic panel: Noa holds an action-item list with her name as sole owner while Vera waves goodbye and steps out of frame.](assets/images/manager-transitions/comic-08-clean-ownership.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: ownership transfers completely — no shadow manager, no restart from zero.*
