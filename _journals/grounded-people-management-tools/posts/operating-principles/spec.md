# Spec: Operating Principles

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State why my organization needs written operating principles — covering how
we work, who we are, and what we expect of leaders — and turn the workbook's
worked example (Stripe's operating principles as of December 2021) into a
tool for writing our own. The example is studied for its form: three short
sections of named, two-to-three-sentence principles (five on how we work,
five on who we are, six demands on leaders), plus a secondary layer of
"classic" slogans, all carrying a date stamp. The record's stance is
explicit: study the model, then write your own in your own words — never
copy it. The runnable write-your-own kit lives in the Checklist tab.

## Audience

Executives and senior managers who own (or should own) their organization's
written culture; leaders in my organization who will be held to the leaders'
section; anyone tempted to paste another company's values into their
handbook. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states that culture must be
      written down in three sections (how we work, who we are, what we
      expect of leaders) and that the model is studied, not copied.
- [x] **The form survives** — three named sections, roughly five principles
      each (six for leaders), name-plus-short-body shape, the "as of
      December 2021" date stamp, and the seven classic slogans as a
      secondary layer.
- [x] **The leaders' section is load-bearing** — leader expectations are
      demands (talent bar, 1/4/10-year ambition horizon, pace, decision
      accountability, clarity and context, problem-solving), not aspirations.
- [x] **Study-don't-copy is explicit** — the record argues why verbatim
      adoption fails and the checklist produces original text.
- [x] **The checklist is a write-your-own kit** — sections to produce plus
      quality tests derived from the example, not a transcription of
      Stripe's set.
- [x] **Credit is explicit** — References name Claire Hughes Johnson's
      *Scaling People* companion workbooks and Stripe as the source of the
      example set.

## Non-goals

- Not [[team-charter]] — that record defines one team's mission and scope;
  this one defines organization-wide behavior and character.
- Not [[working-with-me]] — that is a single leader's personal user guide;
  this is the standard all leaders share.
- Not [[organizational-values]] — the engineering-journal record on how
  values are lived and tested; this record is the tool for writing the
  document itself.
- Not a performance-review rubric — the principles feed reviews and
  [[performance-reviews]] but do not define the process.
