![Comic panel: Vera writes CAREER CONVERSATION on a whiteboard with the instruction 'Walk me through your history' circled below.](assets/images/career-conversations/comic-04-the-tool.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The tool: a one-off, 60-minute career conversation with every report, early.*

![Comic panel: Noa announces the plan to her team, then a reminder note pinned to a calendar the day before the meeting.](assets/images/career-conversations/comic-05-announce-then-remind.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: announce the plan up front, then send the reminder exactly one day before, not earlier.*

![Comic panel: Noa's clipboard shows a timeline from first job to favorite and least favorite roles to a five-year projection labeled type of work, not title.](assets/images/career-conversations/comic-06-five-years-by-type-of-work.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: walk the history forward, then project five years out by type of work, not title.*

![Comic panel: Noa pauses mid-conversation, holding back a quick conclusion, pen ready over her clipboard.](assets/images/career-conversations/comic-07-ask-why-not-diagnose.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: it takes a whole patient hour of asking why, not a quick diagnosis.*

**Panel 8:** *The closer: one deliberate hour, referenced months later, pays back for years.*
