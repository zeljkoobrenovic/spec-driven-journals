# Spec: Compensation Conversations

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I approach compensation conversations as a manager: pay links to
performance and shapes trust and fairness, so I own the message personally
rather than treating it as something "the system decided." The post turns
the workbook's "Compensation Conversations Preparation and Guide" into an
operating principle — knowing the company's comp timeline and resources,
understanding and being able to explain the compensation philosophy,
preparing each person's individual story (career stage, date of last
uplevel, size of last increase, likely expectation), being ready to own the
message and flex with the moment, expecting and staying present through
emotion when expectations misalign without apologizing for the outcome, and
communicating even when nothing changes — plus the three-phase conversation
outline: check for understanding, describe the outcome, open it up for
discussion.

## Audience

Managers in my organization delivering compensation outcomes (so they know
this is a personal obligation, not a form letter); people receiving
compensation conversations (so they know what a well-run one looks like);
peer executives comparing operating models. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states the manager owns the
      compensation message personally and never outsources it to "the
      system decided."
- [x] **Individual-level prep is explicit** — career stage, date of last
      uplevel, size of last increase, and likely expectation survive as a
      named prep step, not a generic reminder.
- [x] **The three-phase outline survives** — check for understanding,
      describe the outcome, open it up for discussion, in that order.
- [x] **The hard-conversation guidance is precise** — own the result,
      empathize and listen, do not apologize for the outcome, do not
      promise a future outcome you can't guarantee.
- [x] **"Communicate even when nothing changes" and "say I don't know"
      survive** as explicit, distinct commitments.
- [x] **The checklist reproduces the workbook mechanics** — timeline and
      resources, individual prep fields, and the three-phase outline as
      running checklist sections.

## Non-goals

- Not [[performance-reviews]] — that record covers the written performance
  record; this one covers delivering the compensation outcome that traces
  back to it.
- Not [[managing-underperformance]] — that record covers the documented path
  through sustained underperformance; this one covers the comp conversation
  itself, even when it surfaces a performance gap.
- Not [[careers-and-performance]] — the engineering-journal record on career
  ladders and calibration; that record is how the number gets set, this
  record is how it gets delivered.
- Not [[career-conversations]] — that record covers the broader ongoing
  conversation about motivation and growth; this one covers compensation as
  one specific, high-stakes instance of it, not the general pattern.
- No claim that this record defines the compensation philosophy, bands, or
  calibration process itself — only how I prepare for and run the
  conversation once the outcome is set.
