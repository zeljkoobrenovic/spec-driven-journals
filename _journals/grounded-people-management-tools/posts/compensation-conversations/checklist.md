## 1. Timeline and resources

- [ ] Outline the company's compensation timeline (cycle dates, decision dates, communication dates)
- [ ] Link to fact pages or reference material about the compensation program
- [ ] Link to manager education guides on compensation
- [ ] Confirm I understand and can explain the compensation philosophy myself before I try to explain it to anyone else

## 2. Prepare at the individual level (per person)

| Field | Notes |

- [ ] Think through each person's story: career stage, date of last uplevel, size of last increase
- [ ] Gauge what the person may be expecting based on that history
- [ ] Anticipate whether this cycle's outcome will align with, exceed, or disappoint that expectation

## 3. Be prepared to own the message

- [ ] Come ready to move flexibly — teaching, active listening, congratulating, or working through a tough message
- [ ] If there's a promotion or increase: celebrate it, and name the specific impact and behaviors that earned it
- [ ] Do not set the expectation that the same increase repeats every cycle
- [ ] If expectations are misaligned: expect an emotional reaction — money is personal
- [ ] Own the result, even when the message is difficult or disappointing
- [ ] Empathize and listen — but do not apologize for the outcome
- [ ] Do not promise a future outcome I can't guarantee
- [ ] Where useful, tie the outcome to performance or to how the program itself is designed
- [ ] Communicate even when there is no change in compensation
- [ ] If I don't know the answer to a question: say so, and commit to circling back with more information

## 4. Run the conversation — Phase 1: Check for understanding

- [ ] Ask whether the person has reviewed and understands the compensation philosophy
- [ ] If the answer is no, take a moment to explain it and point them to resources
- [ ] Answer any questions they raise
- [ ] If unsure of an answer, commit to coming back with one at a later date

## 5. Run the conversation — Phase 2: Describe the outcome

- [ ] Remind them of their current compensation
- [ ] Share or affirm their base salary for the coming year
- [ ] If there's an increase: celebrate it and point to the critical work and positive trajectory behind it
- [ ] If there's no change: refer back to the program design and, if appropriate, tie the outcome to performance and the development areas you're working on together
- [ ] Share their target bonus for the coming year
- [ ] If there's a change to the bonus target, highlight it

## 6. Run the conversation — Phase 3: Open it up for discussion

- [ ] Create space for questions
- [ ] Move with the conversation — congratulations, or working through concerns, as needed
- [ ] Reinforce that I value their contributions to the company and the team

## 7. After the conversation

- [ ] Note any open question I owe a follow-up on, and close the loop by the date I committed to
- [ ] Record what was discussed for my own continuity into the next cycle
