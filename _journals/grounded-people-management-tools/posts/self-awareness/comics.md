Management quality is downstream of self-awareness — the first tool I run on myself, in eight panels.

![Comic panel: Noa looks uneasily at a closed office door labeled MANAGER, holding her clipboard.](assets/images/self-awareness/comic-01-a-stranger-manages-me.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *A manager who has not written down who they are is asking people to work for a stranger.*

![Comic panel: Noa studies a wall of mood-labeled sticky notes with no explanations, looking puzzled.](assets/images/self-awareness/comic-02-undocumented-preferences.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *An unexamined filter isn't neutral — the team experiences it as policy.*

![Comic panel: Vera holds up a shiny poster of ten glowing value words while shaking her head.](assets/images/self-awareness/comic-03-the-flattering-list.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *Ten flattering words is a mood board, not self-awareness.*

![Comic panel: Vera writes a shrinking sequence 10 to 5 to 3 on a whiteboard beside a small rocking-chair sketch labeled AGE 80.](assets/images/self-awareness/comic-04-narrow-until-it-hurts.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *Imagine yourself at 80, list ten values that gave that life meaning, narrow to five, then three.*

![Comic panel: Noa fills in a four-column worksheet labeled Value, Activities, Outcomes, Trade-Offs on her clipboard.](assets/images/self-awareness/comic-05-noa-runs-the-worksheet.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *The worksheet has four columns: value, activities, positive outcomes, and trade-offs.*

![Comic panel: a 2x2 grid labeled Analyzer, Director, Collaborator, Promoter with two marker dots, HOME and WORK, placed apart.](assets/images/self-awareness/comic-06-quadrant-twice.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Analyzer, Director, Collaborator, Promoter — placed once at home, once at work. The gap is the signal.*

![Comic panel: Noa winces while writing in the trade-offs column under the value Excellence, a missed-deadline calendar in a thought bubble.](assets/images/self-awareness/comic-07-the-cost-of-excellence.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Every value taxes someone. Writing the tax down is the cost — and the point.*

![Comic panel: Noa confidently walks through the office door from panel one, holding her completed worksheet, as Vera nods beside her.](assets/images/self-awareness/comic-08-i-earn-the-right.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *Only after the written self-audit do I earn the right to manage anyone else's.*
