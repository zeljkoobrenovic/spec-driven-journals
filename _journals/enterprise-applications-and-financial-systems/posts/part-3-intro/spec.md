# Spec: PART III — INTEGRATED: The Finance Estate as One System

## Intent

Orient the reader to Part III: the shift from judging platforms one at a time to following money and data across the boundaries between them. Show that a finance estate fails at its hops even when every platform works, define hop and authority, and frame the part through the auditor's 2027 two-direction walkthrough request and the €14 million suspense balance at Northmere.

## Audience

Chief Architects, finance-systems and integration architects, and finance-transformation leaders responsible for how platforms connect, reconcile and change together.
