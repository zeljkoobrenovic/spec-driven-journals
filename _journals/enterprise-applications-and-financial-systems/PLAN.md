# RECONCILED — Book Plan and Chapter Contracts

**RECONCILED: Enterprise Architecture for Financial Systems**

*How a Chief Architect designs, integrates, governs and evolves the finance technology of a multinational enterprise — ERP, payments, close, consolidation and the custom platforms between them — as one coherent system.*

**Status:** first edition in progress · **Created:** 2026-10-18

## Positioning

The reader is an experienced architect or technology leader who works across ERP, finance, payments and enterprise applications: Chief and Enterprise Architects, finance-systems and solution architects, integration architects, senior engineering and platform leaders, and finance-transformation executives. The book takes the perspective of a **Chief Architect responsible for Enterprise Architecture, Enterprise Applications and Financial Systems** in a large, multinational, technology-driven organization.

The title names the book's central idea. **Reconciliation** is the discipline finance uses to prove that two records of the same reality agree; the book argues that an enterprise finance estate must be architected so that it can always be reconciled — systems with systems, ledgers with money, brands with the group, vendors' roadmaps with the enterprise's strategy, and Finance's needs with Engineering's constraints.

The book is **vendor-aware but vendor-neutral**. It explains SAP, Workday, Oracle, BlackLine, OneStream, Adyen, Stripe and custom-built platforms in depth, but organizes the argument around architectural questions and business capabilities, not product catalogs.

## Provenance

The book generalizes a Productscape domain model of a large multi-brand travel group's enterprise-application and financial-systems landscape (built from public filings and job descriptions). It is **not** a book about that company. A digest of the model, stripped of company names, lives in `_research/productscape-digest.md`. Verified research briefs on each platform and foundation topic live in `_research/brief-*.md`.

## Running Example

**Northmere Group** is fictional. See `_research/scenario-ledger.md` — the canonical shared facts (brands, entities, systems, cast, figures, timeline). Every chapter's example must agree with it; extend it only by adding to the ledger.
