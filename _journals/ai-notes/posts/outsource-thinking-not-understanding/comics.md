An eight-panel walk through the whole idea: you can hand Rex the thinking, but the understanding has to stay in a human head.

![Comic panel: an eager robot prints a fountain of finished blueprints and hands one to an engineer who looks at it uncertainly.](assets/images/outsource-thinking-not-understanding/comic-01-the-printer.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *AI produces the artifact in seconds. Having it and understanding it are not the same thing.*

![Comic panel split into THINKING (robot printing) and UNDERSTANDING (engineer with a lightbulb over her head).](assets/images/outsource-thinking-not-understanding/comic-02-two-acts.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *Thinking produces the artifact. Understanding is grasping it — and only one of the two is outsourceable.*

![Comic panel: the robot rises on a steep upward arrow while the engineer stands unchanged on flat ground.](assets/images/outsource-thinking-not-understanding/comic-03-ai-improves-you-dont.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *AI keeps improving. You stay roughly the same — which is exactly why the next panel matters.*

![Comic panel: the robot fire-hoses a pile of artifacts at the engineer, who is overwhelmed at a narrow doorway labeled bottleneck: understanding.](assets/images/outsource-thinking-not-understanding/comic-04-the-bottleneck.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *Multiply the thinking and the strain moves to the one thing left human: understanding. 'I am becoming the bottleneck.'*

![Comic panel: the robot in a booth pushes correct symbol-cards through a slot but has an empty thought bubble; the engineer peers in unimpressed.](assets/images/outsource-thinking-not-understanding/comic-05-symbols-not-meaning.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Producing the right symbols is not understanding them. Consume the output blindly and you become the booth too.*

![Comic panel: the robot has vanished in a puff of smoke; the engineer holds cobwebbed code pages tagged legacy on arrival.](assets/images/outsource-thinking-not-understanding/comic-06-legacy-on-arrival.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The real asset was the theory in a head. If the author was a model that forgot the chat, the code is legacy on arrival.*

![Comic panel: the engineer climbs a staircase of growing architecture sketches, a lightbulb lighting at each step, past a discarded finished diagram.](assets/images/outsource-thinking-not-understanding/comic-07-re-derive-staircase.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *You understand a design by re-deriving it — naive version first, one decision at a time — not by nodding at the finished thing.*

![Comic panel: the engineer at a doorway labeled the real world connects an artifact to a person and a system, a bright lightbulb over her head, the robot printing behind.](assets/images/outsource-thinking-not-understanding/comic-08-the-last-mile.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *Outsource the thinking. Keep the understanding — it's what stands at the door where the artifact finally meets the world.*
