*The working checklist behind this record. The Article tab carries the rationale and anti-patterns.*

## 1. What to expect from a good manager

- [ ] Has regular 1:1s with me
- [ ] Uses 1:1s for discussion, not just status updates
- [ ] Treats me like a person, not just a worker
- [ ] Builds trust and human connection
- [ ] Gives feedback regularly
- [ ] Gives praise publicly when appropriate
- [ ] Gives criticism privately and promptly
- [ ] Helps me improve my work beyond day-to-day execution
- [ ] Helps me navigate workplace challenges
- [ ] Helps me understand promotion expectations
- [ ] Identifies stretch opportunities for growth
- [ ] Connects my work to team and company goals
- [ ] Helps me find training, learning, or career resources
- [ ] Advocates for my growth and advancement
- [ ] Helps me focus on what matters most

## 2. What I am responsible for as a report

- [ ] Think about what I want from my career
- [ ] Bring topics and agendas to my 1:1s
- [ ] Ask for help when I am stuck
- [ ] Ask for feedback, including constructive feedback
- [ ] Speak up when I am unhappy
- [ ] Ask for projects, opportunities, or stretch work I want
- [ ] Ask directly about promotion or raise expectations
- [ ] Take ownership of my growth and happiness at work
- [ ] Set my own work-life boundaries
- [ ] Use my manager as a resource, not a mind reader

## 3. How to work well with a manager

- [ ] Bring solutions, not only problems
- [ ] Ask for advice instead of only demanding fixes
- [ ] Give my manager grace when they are imperfect
- [ ] Recognize that I can only control my side of the relationship
- [ ] Decide whether a problem is my manager, my situation, or me
- [ ] Choose managers carefully when evaluating jobs

## 4. Self-assessment

- [ ] I have had at least one manager I would call good
- [ ] I can name what made that manager effective
- [ ] My current 1:1s are useful
- [ ] My manager knows enough about me to support me well
- [ ] I receive meaningful feedback
- [ ] My manager has helped me set work goals
- [ ] I know what I need to ask for next
