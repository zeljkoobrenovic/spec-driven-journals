![Comic panel: Arlo shrugs at a canceled 1:1 on his calendar while Vera raises an eyebrow.](assets/images/management-101/comic-01-never-seen-good.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: many engineers have never had a manager they would call good — so they stop expecting one.*

![Comic panel: Arlo is buried by surprise annual-review papers while his career path drifts behind him.](assets/images/management-101/comic-02-calibrated-to-neglect.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: without the contract, feedback arrives once a year as a surprise and careers quietly drift.*

![Comic panel: Arlo silently imagines a promotion while his baffled manager sees nothing, and Vera notes managers cannot read minds.](assets/images/management-101/comic-03-the-mind-reader.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the mind-reader assumption — never say what you want, then resent not getting it.*

![Comic panel: Vera unrolls a two-column contract with two signature lines and hands Arlo a pen.](assets/images/management-101/comic-04-two-signatures.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: the manager–report relationship is a contract with two signatures — and both sides get held to it.*

![Comic panel: a discussion-shaped 1:1 with a poster showing praise in public and criticism in private and prompt.](assets/images/management-101/comic-05-the-manager-side.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs, manager side: 1:1s that are discussions, praise in public, criticism private and prompt, and active work on growth.*

![Comic panel: Arlo slides a written agenda across the 1:1 table and asks directly for a project.](assets/images/management-101/comic-06-the-report-side.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *How it runs, report side: bring the agenda, ask for what you want, speak up while it is still fixable.*

![Comic panel: Arlo weighs a complaint cloud against a solution lightbulb while Vera points at the solution.](assets/images/management-101/comic-07-a-skill-you-practice.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: being managed well is a skill — bring solutions, not just complaints, and work the one side you control.*

![Comic panel: Arlo picks the job offer that includes a good manager over the one with only more money.](assets/images/management-101/comic-08-part-of-compensation.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: a good manager is a meaningful part of compensation — and this contract is what 'good' means.*
