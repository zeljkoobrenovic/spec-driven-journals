*The working checklist behind this record. The Article tab carries the rationale and anti-patterns.*

## 1. Build trust

- [ ] Create an environment where people can tell me what they really think
- [ ] Make it safe for reports to bring me their biggest challenges
- [ ] Give and receive critical feedback without taking it personally
- [ ] Ask myself honestly whether my team would choose to work for me again
- [ ] Let 1:1s include some awkward but meaningful conversations

## 2. Be human

- [ ] Show care and respect for each report as a person, not just as a performer
- [ ] Invest regular time in each report
- [ ] Hold at least one weekly 1:1 with each direct report
- [ ] Focus 1:1s on the report's priorities, growth, and obstacles, not just status updates
- [ ] Ask more questions instead of jumping straight to advice

## 3. Run useful 1:1s

- [ ] Discuss top priorities and most important outcomes
- [ ] Calibrate what "great" looks like
- [ ] Share feedback in both directions
- [ ] Check how the person is feeling overall
- [ ] Ask: "What's top of mind for you right now?"
- [ ] Ask: "How can I help?"
- [ ] Ask: "What was most useful about today's conversation?"

## 4. Be clear about performance

- [ ] Make sure each report knows what I expect
- [ ] Be transparent about how I think they're doing
- [ ] Address concerns early instead of letting ambiguity linger
- [ ] Diagnose whether a problem is about skill, motivation, or fit

## 5. Model vulnerability and accountability

- [ ] Admit my own mistakes quickly
- [ ] Apologize clearly when I get something wrong
- [ ] Share growth areas and uncertainties honestly
- [ ] Lead with empathy during hard moments

## 6. Help people play to their strengths

- [ ] Notice and name each person's strengths specifically
- [ ] Create opportunities for people to use their strengths more
- [ ] Spend disproportionate time helping strong people get even better
- [ ] Avoid letting low performance consume all my attention

## 7. Protect team culture

- [ ] Refuse to tolerate brilliant jerks or toxic behavior
- [ ] Hold a high bar for collaboration and kindness
- [ ] Remember that one divider can reduce the whole team's effectiveness

## 8. Be honest about fit

- [ ] Recognize when someone's values, aspirations, or working style don't fit the role
- [ ] Stop trying to "make it work" forever when evidence says otherwise
- [ ] Explore better-fit roles when possible
- [ ] Move people out of roles quickly and respectfully when needed

## 9. Keep the big picture in mind

- [ ] Remember that management is about getting better outcomes through people
- [ ] Prioritize people first, then process
- [ ] Build a small-team culture of honesty, debate, and collaboration
- [ ] Leave conversations with clear next steps
