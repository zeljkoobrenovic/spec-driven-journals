![Comic panel: Arlo proudly shows a board full of in-progress tickets while the shipped column stands empty.](assets/images/competent-software-engineer/comic-01-busy-all-week.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the most common failure mode is not weak code — it is busy weeks that ship nothing.*

![Comic panel: a flagged top-priority ticket gathers cobwebs while Arlo follows a trail of small shiny tasks.](assets/images/competent-software-engineer/comic-02-the-displaced-priority.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the top priority was never named — or was quietly displaced by whatever felt easier.*

![Comic panel: Arlo grinds silently on a bug for two days as Vera arrives asking what he has tried.](assets/images/competent-software-engineer/comic-03-the-silent-grinder.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the silent grinder — days without progress and no signal to anyone.*

![Comic panel: Vera presents a two-layer stack of delivery habits over craft as Arlo takes notes.](assets/images/competent-software-engineer/comic-04-the-right-things-done.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: competence is not cleverness — delivery habits on top of craft, and both layers are learnable.*

![Comic panel: Arlo confirms his flagged top-priority ticket while a big project splits into small shippable boxes.](assets/images/competent-software-engineer/comic-05-name-it-confirm-it-ship-it.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: name the week's most important task, confirm it when unsure, and break work into shippable pieces.*

![Comic panel: a timer rings and Arlo works through duck, docs, and search before asking a teammate with his attempts listed.](assets/images/competent-software-engineer/comic-06-the-thirty-minute-timer.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: no progress after 30-60 minutes means blocked — run the repertoire, then ask with 'here is what I tried.'*

![Comic panel: Arlo does daily tests, small refactors, and defensive checks while a flashy trophy sits ignored.](assets/images/competent-software-engineer/comic-07-the-unglamorous-floor.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: the craft is a floor, not a flourish — tests, small refactors, and defensive code, practiced daily.*

![Comic panel: Arlo runs his five-minute weekly self-review as a faded scaffold and Vera step back.](assets/images/competent-software-engineer/comic-08-the-scaffolding-comes-down.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: five minutes a week — the self-review is what remains when the coaching scaffolding comes down.*
