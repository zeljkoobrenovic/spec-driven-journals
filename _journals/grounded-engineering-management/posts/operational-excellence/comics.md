![Comic panel: a manager celebrates a deploy with confetti while a pager on the desk starts to glow.](assets/images/operational-excellence/comic-01-it-shipped.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: 'done' is declared at ship time — while the pager is already warming up.*

![Comic panel: an exhausted manager at 3 AM with a blank dashboard and an empty runbook binder.](assets/images/operational-excellence/comic-02-three-am-archaeology.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the first serious incident becomes archaeology — the team digs for dashboards and runbooks that were never built.*

![Comic panel: a manager waves off a leaning tower of deferred operational task cards while pointing at a feature roadmap.](assets/images/operational-excellence/comic-03-well-get-to-it.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: 'we'll get to it' — every deferred runbook, DR test, and alert cleanup is invisible until the night it isn't.*

![Comic panel: a mentor crosses out 'done equals deployed' and draws icons for monitoring, paging, runbooks, recovery, and improvement.](assets/images/operational-excellence/comic-04-done-means-runnable.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: a system is done when it can be launched, monitored, paged on, recovered, and improved without heroics — and the manager owns whether the answers are yes.*

![Comic panel: a manager runs a pre-launch review with a checklist while a rocket waits on the pad.](assets/images/operational-excellence/comic-05-the-launch-review.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: the launch review — dashboards, runbooks, SLOs, tested recovery — is the cheapest, highest-leverage operational meeting a manager runs.*

![Comic panel: a manager shows a shrinking alert chart while an on-call engineer sleeps beside a quiet pager.](assets/images/operational-excellence/comic-06-measure-the-pain.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: measure alert volume and on-call pain, treat high numbers as defects to fix — the same neglect shows up in attrition and in time-to-recovery.*

![Comic panel: a mentor holds up a cobwebbed disaster-recovery document while an engineer runs a timed restore drill.](assets/images/operational-excellence/comic-07-the-untested-plan.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: the bar demands drills — recovery plans tested, backups restored, time to full recovery measured — not documents that age in a drawer.*

![Comic panel: a relaxed manager deploys with a canary on a green dashboard and a rollback lever within reach.](assets/images/operational-excellence/comic-08-the-calm-deploy.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: with canaries, flags, monitoring, and a rollback path always ready, deployment becomes a source of confidence instead of fear.*
