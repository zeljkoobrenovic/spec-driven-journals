**Panel 1:** *The hook: the same behavior earns applause in one environment and a frown in another.*

![Comic panel: Arlo holds a great-engineer trophy, confused between doorways marked with different environment icons.](assets/images/thriving-in-different-environments/comic-02-no-context-free-great.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: there is no context-free 'great engineer' — each environment defines standout work differently.*

![Comic panel: Arlo calmly polishes a small ornament while the building burns and colleagues run with buckets behind him.](assets/images/thriving-in-different-environments/comic-03-playing-the-previous-game.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: playing the previous game — polishing in wartime, or bulldozing in peace — after a silent mode change.*

![Comic panel: Vera hands Arlo a compass in front of a chart asking team, mode, and company.](assets/images/thriving-in-different-environments/comic-04-read-the-environment.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: reading the environment — team type, company mode, company type — is a core engineering skill.*

![Comic panel: Arlo talks with an end user on one side and hands a feedback card to an engineer on a platform slab on the other.](assets/images/thriving-in-different-environments/comic-05-product-vs-platform.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: on a product team the customer is an end user; on a platform team the customer is another engineer.*

![Comic panel: Arlo and Vera study a wall dial between storm and sun zones with small gauges beside it.](assets/images/thriving-in-different-environments/comic-06-wartime-peacetime-dial.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: diagnose the mode from the instruments — deadlines, meetings, conflict tolerance — because the transition is silent.*

![Comic panel: Arlo ships boxes fast in a storm as his battery icon runs low and Vera steadies his shoulder.](assets/images/thriving-in-different-environments/comic-07-wartime-has-a-fuse.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: wartime pace is real and so is the burn — manage stress actively and treat stability as uncertain.*

![Comic panel: Arlo carries a glowing kit with star, heart, handshake, and bridge icons through different environment zones as colleagues wave.](assets/images/thriving-in-different-environments/comic-08-the-universal-set.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: environments are temporary — standout work, helping others, kept relationships, and unburned bridges pay everywhere.*
