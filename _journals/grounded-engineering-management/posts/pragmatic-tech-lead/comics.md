![Comic panel: Arlo holds a tech-lead badge, puzzled between a door labeled manager and a door labeled engineer.](assets/images/pragmatic-tech-lead/comic-01-the-nicer-name.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: tech lead is not a junior manager and not a superior engineer — so what is it?*

![Comic panel: the team codes while the project board behind them shows question marks under goal, scope, and owner.](assets/images/pragmatic-tech-lead/comic-02-failing-quietly-at-kickoff.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: a project without a goal, a written scope, and an owner is not early — it is already failing quietly.*

![Comic panel: engineers queue behind Arlo's desk as he stamps every decision himself.](assets/images/pragmatic-tech-lead/comic-03-the-bottleneck.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: centralizing every decision until the whole team queues behind the lead.*

![Comic panel: Vera draws a tech-lead figure standing on blocks labeled build, align, and grow while Arlo takes notes.](assets/images/pragmatic-tech-lead/comic-04-accountable-and-hands-on.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: accountable for the project succeeding, hands-on enough to understand the system and set its quality bar.*

![Comic panel: Arlo runs a kickoff over a written scope page while a path of small milestone stepping stones stretches behind him.](assets/images/pragmatic-tech-lead/comic-05-kickoff-and-milestones.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: start with a real kickoff and written scope, then ship small milestones — estimates are forecasts that firm up as they land.*

![Comic panel: a stakeholder adds a scope box to a balance whose timeline and people pans tilt, while Arlo states the tradeoff.](assets/images/pragmatic-tech-lead/comic-06-the-physics-conversation.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: when scope grows, timeline, staffing, or quality moves — and the tech lead says so at that moment, out loud.*

![Comic panel: Arlo brings a rough prototype to another engineer's desk while an unused escalate lever stands behind him.](assets/images/pragmatic-tech-lead/comic-07-the-direct-conversation.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The discipline: risks are worked, not hoped away — prototypes for the unknowns, and a direct conversation before any escalation.*

![Comic panel: the team celebrates a finished project by a done board while Arlo watches from the back and Vera takes a note.](assets/images/pragmatic-tech-lead/comic-08-succeeds-without-him.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: the job is to make the team succeed — not to make the team dependent on the lead.*
