![Comic panel: a smiling manager hands Arlo an all-green status report and Arlo gives a thumbs up.](assets/images/managing-managers/comic-01-all-green.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: every report on the desk is green — and every one of them has been summarized on the way up.*

![Comic panel: Arlo shocked by a resignation letter while an unhappy team is visible through the window behind him.](assets/images/managing-managers/comic-02-the-resignation-surprise.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: rely on what filters up, and you learn about the failing team from the resignation letter.*

![Comic panel: Arlo sits by a wide-open door facing an empty hallway with a drifting paper tumbleweed.](assets/images/managing-managers/comic-03-the-open-door.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the open door — the people with the worst news are the least likely to walk through it.*

![Comic panel: Vera taps a whiteboard funnel that smooths a jagged line into a rosy one while Arlo leans in.](assets/images/managing-managers/comic-04-signals-arrive-filtered.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: filtering is what summarization does — so deliberate inspection has to replace ambient awareness.*

![Comic panel: Arlo listens over coffee to a junior engineer speaking freely, notebook open on the table.](assets/images/managing-managers/comic-05-the-skip-level.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: regular skip-levels with real questions — the ground-truth channel, framed to learn, not to police.*

![Comic panel: Arlo steadies but does not take a heavy TEAM PROBLEM box carried uphill by a manager.](assets/images/managing-managers/comic-06-coach-dont-absorb.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: coach the manager who carries the problem — absorb it, and team health quietly becomes your job.*

![Comic panel: Arlo studies an evidence wall of tickets, calendars, and incidents with a magnifying glass while Vera advises.](assets/images/managing-managers/comic-07-debugging-the-team.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: ground truth is work — hypothesis, data, observation, and asking why until the real issue surfaces.*

![Comic panel: Arlo presents an annotated wall of team cards with green and amber dots as his managers stand relaxed nearby.](assets/images/managing-managers/comic-08-i-know-why.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: the bar for the rung — for every team, being able to say whether it runs well, and why.*
