![Comic panel: a leader makes a casual hallway remark while behind him a team frantically starts a rewrite.](assets/images/senior-leadership/comic-01-the-hallway-echo.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: at the top, a casual hallway comment redirects a team's week.*

![Comic panel: echo rings spread from a small speech bubble across a floor of worried team figures.](assets/images/senior-leadership/comic-02-everything-carries-weight.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: everything a senior leader says carries extra weight — a vented anxiety becomes the organization's anxiety by Friday.*

![Comic panel: a leader in a paper crown labeled CTO strides confidently away from a signpost pointing the other way.](assets/images/senior-leadership/comic-03-the-title-is-not-the-job.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: assuming the title is the job — doing what it meant at the last company instead of what this company needs.*

![Comic panel: a whiteboard with four icons — gather, nudge, decide, model — presented as the whole job.](assets/images/senior-leadership/comic-04-four-tasks-one-job.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: the job reduces to four tasks — gather and synthesize, nudge rather than command, decide on incomplete inputs, model the values.*

![Comic panel: a leader raises one project flag while explicitly marking another lane stopped.](assets/images/senior-leadership/comic-05-change-priorities-cleanly.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: change priorities cleanly — confirm the shift, decide out loud what stops, and repeat the message until it lands.*

![Comic panel: a mentor hands over a compass labeled True North while team leads navigate forks with their own compasses.](assets/images/senior-leadership/comic-06-true-north.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: a written True North — explicit standards and risk analysis — lets leaders decide fast without escalating everything upward.*

![Comic panel: a leader steps away from a team gathering to personally deliver a difficult message.](assets/images/senior-leadership/comic-07-the-cost-of-detachment.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: bad news is delivered personally — never by broadcast, never a message you cannot stand behind.*

![Comic panel: an engineer freely reports bad news while the leader reacts with curiosity instead of anger.](assets/images/senior-leadership/comic-08-trust-not-fear.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: build trust, not fear — when people can safely tell you the truth, the four tasks finally work.*
