## Verdict

A well-built record with the journal's best framing device — the week-away
test — used consistently as the bar in highlight, table, Scope, checklist §5,
and the comic's closing panel. All links resolve and all images exist. One

before calling it done.

### Minor

- **[index.md · Statement, bullet 5 "I say no like a leader"]** The spec's
  saying-no criterion names five moves; the article carries four ("yes, and",
  tradeoffs, policies, quick clear no's) but omits **"help me say yes"** —
  it appears only in checklist.md §7. The criterion is met across modalities
  but partial in the article, and it is the chapter's most distinctive
  saying-no move. *Add it to the bullet's litany.*
- **[index.md · Statement]** Culture-building without "us vs. them" is named
  in the spec's Intent as one of the rung's mechanics and gets a full
  Rationale paragraph, an anti-pattern, and checklist §10 — but no Statement
  commitment. Of the spec's mechanics, it is the only one missing from the
  record's normative core. *Add a culture commitment bullet (peers as first
  team, alignment to company purpose).*

### Nits

- **[comics.md · Panel 4 caption]** "…a system of work that runs without
  you" — the only second-person address in the four posts' comics, against
  the article's strict first person. Narrator captions tolerate it, but "runs
  without the manager" would match the register.
- **[index.md · Statement b.2 vs. Rationale ¶2 vs. Practice ¶]** "Prune
  low-value meetings / calendar review / prune the calendar" appears three
  times in close variants; the third occurrence (Practice) is doing
  checklist-§13's job and could lean on it more lightly.
