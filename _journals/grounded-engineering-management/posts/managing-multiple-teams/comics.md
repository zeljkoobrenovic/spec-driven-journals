![Comic panel: Arlo reaches for a sprint ticket in front of three team boards while Vera raises an eyebrow.](assets/images/managing-multiple-teams/comic-01-still-taking-tickets.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: three teams now — and the strongest pull is back toward the comfortable old job.*

![Comic panel: Arlo buried under envelopes and notifications while dusty folders sit untouched on a shelf.](assets/images/managing-multiple-teams/comic-02-everything-urgent.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: urgent work invades on its own; the important work — hiring, health, leaders — never sends an invite.*

![Comic panel: a frazzled Arlo shoves an armful of loose papers at a startled tech lead.](assets/images/managing-multiple-teams/comic-03-delegation-by-drowning.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: delegation by mood — offloading when overwhelmed reduces workload but builds no capability.*

![Comic panel: Vera shows a diagram of three leaders growing out of Arlo's role while he watches thoughtfully.](assets/images/managing-multiple-teams/comic-04-your-output-is-leaders.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: the job's output is no longer code — it is leaders, priorities, and a system of work that runs without you.*

![Comic panel: Vera taps the highlighted top-right cell of a two-by-two whiteboard grid as Arlo places a task tile.](assets/images/managing-multiple-teams/comic-05-the-delegation-grid.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: two questions — how frequent, how complex — decide what to hand off, keep, or turn into coaching.*

![Comic panel: Arlo fences off a blue calendar block against bouncing urgent arrows while tearing off a meeting card.](assets/images/managing-multiple-teams/comic-06-the-defended-hour.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: important work survives only when it is structurally defended — reserved time, pruned meetings, quick clear no's.*

![Comic panel: Arlo wistfully stores his laptop in a drawer near a wall of team health gauges as Vera reassures him.](assets/images/managing-multiple-teams/comic-07-letting-go-of-shipping.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: giving up the comfort of shipping yourself — credibility becomes maintenance, and health gauges replace the editor.*

![Comic panel: Arlo returns with a travel bag to find three teams running smoothly with green status lights.](assets/images/managing-multiple-teams/comic-08-the-week-away-test.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: the bar is the week-away test — the teams keep operating well when the manager steps away.*
