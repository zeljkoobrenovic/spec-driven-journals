![Comic panel: Arlo stares up at a tall ladder vanishing into fog and asks what good management looks like, while Vera watches.](assets/images/introduction/comic-01-what-does-good-look-like.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: nobody working with me should have to guess what good management looks like at any rung.*

![Comic panel: Vera contrasts an executive's faded thought cloud with a team facing an empty chair at a 1:1 table.](assets/images/introduction/comic-02-org-experiences-behavior.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: an organization never experiences intentions — it experiences what managers at every rung actually do.*

![Comic panel: three managers each improvising the job differently while Arlo watches in dismay.](assets/images/introduction/comic-03-every-manager-improvises.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: leave the ladder unwritten and every manager improvises a different job.*

![Comic panel: Vera taps a ladder whose rungs are written record cards stretching upward.](assets/images/introduction/comic-04-write-the-ladder-down.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: the whole management ladder, written down as records — what each rung is for and what I hold it to.*

![Comic panel: Arlo holds a record card with an article tab and a checklist tab while Vera points at the checklist.](assets/images/introduction/comic-05-article-plus-checklist.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: every record states the principle in the article and ships the runnable checklist in its own tab.*

![Comic panel: Arlo stands on a bridge joining a manager ladder and an engineer ladder while Vera points.](assets/images/introduction/comic-06-two-ladders-meet.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: the management ladder and the engineer career ladder covered side by side — meeting at the tech lead rung and the manager–report contract.*

![Comic panel: Vera stamps a record card DRAFT with source books on a shelf behind her as Arlo looks surprised.](assets/images/introduction/comic-07-draft-until-worn-in.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: this is an operating manual, not a book report — records stay draft until practice wears them in, and each names when it gets revised.*

![Comic panel: Arlo climbs the ladder of records holding a checklist while Vera nods from below.](assets/images/introduction/comic-08-find-your-rung.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: find your rung, read what it commits to, and run its checklist against yourself.*
