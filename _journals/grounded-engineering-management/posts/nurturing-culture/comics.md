![Comic panel: Arlo dramatically unveils a glossy values poster to a politely watching team.](assets/images/nurturing-culture/comic-01-the-values-poster.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: culture work begins — with a poster.*

![Comic panel: three team members give three different answers to what the team values while the poster hangs ignored.](assets/images/nurturing-culture/comic-02-three-different-answers.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: ask random team members what the team values — and get three different answers.*

![Comic panel: Arlo preaches balance by day and sends midnight messages by night, a teammate's phone buzzing in bed.](assets/images/nurturing-culture/comic-03-the-midnight-contradiction.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: preach balance, send midnight messages — when behavior and values conflict, the team believes the behavior.*

![Comic panel: Vera presents four icons — megaphone, mirror, medal, pennant — while the poster leans on the floor.](assets/images/nurturing-culture/comic-04-repeat-model-reward-ritualize.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: culture is what you repeat, what you model, what you reward, and what you ritualize — not what you frame on a wall.*

![Comic panel: Arlo and the team lay out honest diagnosis cards, with five value badges each carrying a small shadow.](assets/images/nurturing-culture/comic-05-diagnose-then-choose.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: diagnose the culture you actually have — adjectives, proud moments, complaints — then choose five values with each one's shadow named.*

![Comic panel: Arlo swings a reward signpost away from a lone hero on a pedestal toward a group lifting a beam together.](assets/images/nurturing-culture/comic-06-fix-the-incentives.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: audit what actually gets rewarded — and when the incentive fights the values, change the incentive, not the poster.*

![Comic panel: montage of Arlo repeating the same value in a meeting, a one-on-one, a chat, and over his own admitted mistake.](assets/images/nurturing-culture/comic-07-repeating-yourself.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: repeating the message across every channel — mistakes included — long past the point it feels redundant; that is when it starts to land.*

![Comic panel: the team ceremonially passes a golden rubber duck trophy under pennant flags while Vera and Arlo watch.](assets/images/nurturing-culture/comic-08-a-ritual-of-our-own.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: a ritual that is unmistakably this team's — daily behavior finally telling the same story as the stated values.*
