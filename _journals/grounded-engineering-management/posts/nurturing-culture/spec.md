# Spec: Nurturing Culture

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I work on team culture deliberately instead of letting it happen to
me: diagnose the current culture honestly (three adjectives, proud moments,
top complaints, the consistency test — would random team members name the
same values?), choose five aspirational adjectives with the tradeoff of each
named, rate and describe the gap, then close it through the four mechanisms
the source gives: repetition across channels, visibly modeled behavior, an
audit of what actually gets rewarded (with the incentive traps named), and
rituals specific to the team. The load-bearing idea: culture is what I
repeat, model, reward, and ritualize — when daily behavior and stated values
tell different stories, the team believes the behavior.

## Audience

Managers in my organization (this is the culture work I expect from anyone
running a team); my own reports (so they can see the mechanism and call out
contradictions); peer executives comparing operating models. First-person
declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states that culture is what I
      repeat, model, reward, and ritualize, and that behavior beats stated
      values every time they conflict.
- [x] **The diagnosis survives** — three current adjectives, proud moments,
      what we do better than most, the random-team-member consistency test,
      comparison with the broader organization, top three complaints.
- [x] **Aspirations carry tradeoffs** — five chosen adjectives each with the
      downside of taking it too far; admired traits noted with the tradeoffs
      those teams accept; traits deliberately not copied.
- [x] **The four reinforcement mechanisms survive** — repetition in many
      forms, walking the walk (consistency, feedback symmetry, trust as
      fragile), the incentive audit with the four named traps, and
      team-specific traditions.
- [x] **The final reflection survives** — what my actions, rewards, and
      rituals are teaching the team, adjusted until daily behavior tells the
      same story as stated values.
- [x] **Credit is explicit** — References name Julie Zhuo's *The Making of a
      Manager* and the "Nurturing Culture" chapter.
