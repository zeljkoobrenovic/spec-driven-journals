# Spec: What Is Management

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State my definition of the management job itself, grounded in the opening of
Julie Zhuo's *The Making of a Manager*: management is getting better outcomes
from a group of people working together — a multiplier effect, not personal
output plus a title — worked through three levers (purpose, people, process),
judged by the team's present and future results rather than the manager's
activity, and gated by an honest self-check on whether I actually want the
day-to-day work of the role. The load-bearing idea: the job is the multiplier,
and a manager who measures themselves by activity or personal output is doing
a different job while the real one sits vacant.

## Audience

Managers in my organization (so they know the scorecard I apply to them and
to myself); engineers considering the move into management (the self-check);
peer executives comparing operating models. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight defines management as better
      outcomes from a group working together, names the multiplier effect,
      the three levers, results-not-activity, and the self-check gate.
- [x] **The three levers survive intact** — purpose (success defined, why it
      matters, people care, reinforced daily), people (skills, motivation,
      trust, strengths/weaknesses, career support, happiness), process
      (clear ownership, decision rules, effective meetings, learning from
      mistakes, planning ahead, healthy culture).
- [x] **The scorecard survives** — team results over activity, present
      outcomes plus future setup, and both of Zhuo's self-questions quoted.
- [x] **The self-check survives** — all five questions (day-to-day
      enjoyment, motivated by team outcomes, comfort talking with people,
      stability in emotional situations, willingness to adapt) appear in the
      article and checklist.
- [x] **Credit is explicit** — How to Read This and References name Julie
      Zhuo, *The Making of a Manager* (Portfolio/Penguin, 2019).
