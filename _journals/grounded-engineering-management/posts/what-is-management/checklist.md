*The working checklist behind this record. The Article tab carries the rationale and anti-patterns.*

## 1. Core job

- [ ] Focus on getting better outcomes from a group of people working together
- [ ] Remember that a team can achieve more than one person alone
- [ ] Aim to create a multiplier effect, not just add my own individual output
- [ ] Improve the team's purpose, people, and process

## 2. Purpose

- [ ] Make sure the team knows what success looks like
- [ ] Make sure the team understands why the work matters
- [ ] Check that people care about achieving the goal
- [ ] Reinforce purpose in meetings, goals, and day-to-day communication

## 3. People

- [ ] Build a team that works well together
- [ ] Support team members in reaching career goals
- [ ] Hire and develop people well
- [ ] Keep an eye on team happiness and satisfaction
- [ ] Make sure people have the right skills for the work
- [ ] Make sure people are motivated
- [ ] Build trusting relationships with team members
- [ ] Understand each person's strengths and weaknesses
- [ ] Give individuals what they need to do their best work

## 4. Process

- [ ] Create processes that help work move smoothly and efficiently
- [ ] Clarify who should do what
- [ ] Define how decisions should be made
- [ ] Run effective meetings
- [ ] Learn from past mistakes
- [ ] Plan for tomorrow, not just today
- [ ] Help create and maintain a healthy team culture

## 5. How I judge my management

- [ ] Evaluate myself by my team's results, not just my activity
- [ ] Look at my team's present outcomes
- [ ] Look at whether my team is set up for great future outcomes
- [ ] Ask: "Am I creating valuable, easy-to-use, well-crafted work through this team?"
- [ ] Ask: "Am I building a strong, satisfied team?"

## 6. Personal approach

- [ ] Spend less time doing the work myself and more time enabling others
- [ ] Avoid acting only as an individual contributor
- [ ] Adapt to what the team and organization need
- [ ] Accept that much of management work is unglamorous but necessary
- [ ] Inspire people to act, rather than just telling them what to do
- [ ] Build trust and respect so people want to follow me

## 7. Self-check before becoming or staying a manager

- [ ] Do I enjoy the day-to-day work of management?
- [ ] Am I motivated by helping a team achieve an outcome?
- [ ] Am I comfortable spending a lot of time talking with people?
- [ ] Can I provide stability in emotionally challenging situations?
- [ ] Am I willing to adapt into the leader my team needs?
