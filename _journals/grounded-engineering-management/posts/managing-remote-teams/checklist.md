*The working checklist behind this record. The Article tab carries the rationale and anti-patterns.*

## 1. Foundations

- [ ] Clarify team priorities for the week
- [ ] Make roles, responsibilities, and expectations explicit
- [ ] Define response-time norms for messages and updates
- [ ] Document how decisions, updates, and knowledge should be shared
- [ ] Default to asynchronous communication when possible

## 2. Build trust

- [ ] Hold regular 1:1 check-ins
- [ ] Ask open-ended questions about workload, morale, and concerns
- [ ] Create space for honest feedback
- [ ] Share uncertainty or humanity as a leader when appropriate
- [ ] Watch for signs of disengagement or isolation
- [ ] Avoid making negative assumptions about silence or tone

## 3. Strengthen communication

- [ ] Repeat important information in multiple places when needed
- [ ] Summarize decisions, owners, and next steps in writing
- [ ] Use dedicated threads/channels for project-specific communication
- [ ] Keep a searchable decision log or knowledge base
- [ ] Set clear meeting norms: purpose, format, and follow-up

## 4. Create communication rhythms

- [ ] Run daily or weekly stand-ups
- [ ] Include what was done, what's next, and blockers
- [ ] Schedule regular all-hands or team updates
- [ ] Record key updates for people who cannot attend
- [ ] Offer office hours or open Q&A time
- [ ] Review and refine communication rhythms regularly

## 5. Foster connection

- [ ] Build rituals for team bonding
- [ ] Celebrate small wins publicly
- [ ] Encourage peer recognition
- [ ] Create casual spaces for non-work conversation
- [ ] Experiment with fun remote traditions or activities
- [ ] Make time for connection, not just task updates

## 6. Use in-person time well

- [ ] Prioritize relationship-building over packed agendas
- [ ] Plan shared meals or informal conversations
- [ ] Include low-pressure group activities
- [ ] Leave unstructured time for natural connection

## 7. Manager habits

- [ ] Check for clarity before assuming alignment
- [ ] Overcommunicate rather than undercommunicate
- [ ] Notice who is participating and who is fading into the background
- [ ] Address misunderstandings quickly
- [ ] Reinforce trust, connection, and consistency every week

## 8. Quick self-review

- [ ] Does everyone know what matters most right now?
- [ ] Does everyone know where to ask questions and find updates?
- [ ] Are people comfortable being honest about problems?
- [ ] Are wins being noticed?
- [ ] Are quieter team members still connected and supported?
