![Comic panel: Arlo alone in an empty office hallway, his team reduced to small video tiles on a laptop.](assets/images/managing-remote-teams/comic-01-the-empty-hallway.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the hallway that used to carry priorities, context, and corrections is simply gone.*

![Comic panel: three video tiles nod at a decision while a fourth teammate from another time zone looks confused.](assets/images/managing-remote-teams/comic-02-never-heard-about-that.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: whatever is not written down explicitly does not exist for part of the team.*

![Comic panel: Arlo calls yet another sync meeting while a teammate attends at midnight, exhausted.](assets/images/managing-remote-teams/comic-03-office-habits-over-video.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: office habits over a video link — synchronous everything, someone's midnight in every meeting.*

![Comic panel: Vera taps a wall of written cards for priorities, norms, and the decision log while Arlo takes notes.](assets/images/managing-remote-teams/comic-04-write-it-down-repeat-it.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: a remote team runs on what is written down and repeated — structure replaces the hallway.*

![Comic panel: Arlo routes most messages into a wide async chute, keeping a narrow lane with two chairs for sync.](assets/images/managing-remote-teams/comic-05-async-by-default.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: async by default with response-time norms — synchronous time reserved for disagreement and connection.*

![Comic panel: Arlo imagines anger behind a terse message while Vera points him to a 1:1 instead.](assets/images/managing-remote-teams/comic-06-dont-read-malice.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: no negative assumptions about silence or tone — open-ended 1:1 questions surface what text hides.*

![Comic panel: a weary Arlo posts the same priority message into three different channels.](assets/images/managing-remote-teams/comic-07-again-yes-again.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: repeating what matters in multiple places feels redundant — on a remote team it is the delivery mechanism.*

![Comic panel: the team shares a relaxed meal together with a crossed-out agenda discarded on a chair.](assets/images/managing-remote-teams/comic-08-the-offsite-that-connects.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: when a remote team finally meets, spend it on the one thing distance cannot deliver — connection.*
