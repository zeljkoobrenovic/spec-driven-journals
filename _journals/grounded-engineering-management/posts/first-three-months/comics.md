![Comic panel: a new manager marches in on day one with a binder of big plans while the team watches warily.](assets/images/first-three-months/comic-01-day-one.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: day one arrives with the urge to prove the promotion was justified.*

![Comic panel: a new manager faces four labeled doors, each with its own warning sign, as a mentor points at them.](assets/images/first-three-months/comic-02-four-doors.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: there are four ways into the role — Apprentice, Pioneer, New Boss, Successor — and each path has its own traps.*

![Comic panel: an eager new manager topples a process board while a working machine crashes behind him.](assets/images/first-three-months/comic-03-the-mark-leaver.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the mark-leaver — reorganizing in week two to show impact, and breaking what the team silently depended on.*

![Comic panel: a mentor turns a signpost from a podium labeled prove toward a rising ramp labeled learn.](assets/images/first-three-months/comic-04-ramp-not-proving.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: the first three months are a ramp-up, not a proving period — and acting otherwise is the fastest way to fail them.*

![Comic panel: a new manager in a friendly 1:1 surrounded by five floating question cards with icons.](assets/images/first-three-months/comic-05-questions-are-free.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: ask every report the five relationship questions — support, feedback, recognition, past managers, the ideal relationship — while questions are still free.*

![Comic panel: a manager climbs three landings labeled 30, 60, and 90 with icons for listening, clarifying, and targeted change.](assets/images/first-three-months/comic-06-the-30-60-90-arc.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: the 30-60-90 arc — listen and learn, clarify and strengthen, then make a few targeted, evidence-based changes and review them.*

![Comic panel: a manager past the 90-day mark admits he is not comfortable yet, and his mentor calls it normal.](assets/images/first-three-months/comic-07-comfort-lags.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: comfort lags competence — feeling at home may take far longer than three months, and pretending otherwise corrodes trust.*

![Comic panel: a manager reviews a board of preserved and changed items with an engaged team at day 90.](assets/images/first-three-months/comic-08-day-90-review.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: at day 90 the ramp has a verdict — a written scorecard, a few targeted changes, the good things explicitly preserved, and a team that trusts you.*
