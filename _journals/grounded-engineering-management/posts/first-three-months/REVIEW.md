- **[checklist.md · §2 vs. index.md Statement]** The article (and the spec's Intent: "a scorecard (written 3/6-month success definition)") makes *written* the point — the "undefined ramp" anti-pattern hinges on it — but checklist §2 says only "Define what success looks like for my first 3 and 6 months," and manager alignment sits separately in §5. Someone running only the checklist loses the record's sharpening. *One word ("in writing") in §2 closes the gap.*

### Nits

- **[comics.md · Panel 5]** Caption says "past managers" (plural) where the question everywhere else is "previous manager."
- **[index.md · Statement, trust bullet]** "Trust is built through 1:1s and consistency, deliberately." — the trailing adverb reads awkwardly; "…is built deliberately, through 1:1s and consistency" flows better.
