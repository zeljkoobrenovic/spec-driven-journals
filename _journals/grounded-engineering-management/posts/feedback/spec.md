# Spec: The Art of Feedback

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State my operating model for feedback as a system that starts before the
work does and runs continuously, distilled from the feedback chapter of
Julie Zhuo's *The Making of a Manager*: set expectations about success up
front, give frequent feedback while work is fresh, make it specific with
concrete examples, include behavioral feedback grounded in patterns, gather
360-style perspectives, and check that the feedback actually landed — the
person can state their own takeaways and next steps. Critical feedback is
delivered directly and calmly, about the work rather than the person, and
never softened into fog. The load-bearing idea: feedback counts on the
receiving end, not the sending end — the measure is whether the person
heard it and knows what to do next.

## Audience

Managers in my organization (so they know the feedback bar I hold them to);
my reports (so they know what feedback to expect from me and why serious
issues will never wait for review time); peer executives comparing operating
models. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states that feedback is a system
      set up before work starts, and that its measure is whether it landed,
      not how it felt to give.
- [x] **The before-work setup survives** — what success looks like, strong
      versus weak results, how to start well, common pitfalls, expectations
      set early.
- [x] **The quality bar survives** — frequent and fresh, specific with
      concrete examples and the why, behavioral feedback anchored to
      patterns, 360-style input from collaborators.
- [x] **Landing and register are explicit** — safety to listen, asking for
      their takeaways and next steps, written follow-up, and the
      expectation-versus-suggestion distinction.
- [x] **Critical-feedback delivery and the common mistakes survive** —
      direct and calm, about work not character, never while upset; no
      review-time ambush, no compliment sandwich, no fake discussions, and
      the final test (clear, fair, timely, respectful, actionable).
- [x] **Credit is explicit** — References name Julie Zhuo's *The Making of
      a Manager*.
