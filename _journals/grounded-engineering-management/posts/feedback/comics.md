**Panel 1:** *The hook: the review-time ambush — months of unsaid feedback, delivered all at once.*

![Comic panel: Arlo returns a document with vague squiggles while the team member scratches their head, confused.](assets/images/feedback/comic-02-the-mood-not-the-message.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: 'this is confusing' is a mood, not feedback — nothing to repeat, nothing to change.*

![Comic panel: Arlo offers a giant sandwich with thick praise bread and a tiny warning filling; the team member takes only the bread.](assets/images/feedback/comic-03-the-compliment-sandwich.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the compliment sandwich — the person walks away remembering only the bread.*

![Comic panel: Vera taps the starting flag of a timeline on a whiteboard while Arlo takes notes.](assets/images/feedback/comic-04-starts-before-the-work.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: feedback is a system set up before the work starts — not a reaction after it ends.*

![Comic panel: Arlo points at a strong-result sketch next to a weak-result sketch while briefing a team member.](assets/images/feedback/comic-05-what-strong-looks-like.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: strong versus weak named before the work begins — the cheapest feedback there is.*

![Comic panel: the team member counts takeaways on their fingers while Arlo listens and asks for them.](assets/images/feedback/comic-06-the-landing-check.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: the landing check — their takeaways, their next steps, not your recitation.*

![Comic panel: Arlo calmly points at a document during a direct, serious conversation with a team member.](assets/images/feedback/comic-07-direct-and-calm.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: directness takes a breath and a steady voice — comfort-seeking is what feedback fails from.*

![Comic panel: a team member nods through their review unsurprised while Vera watches approvingly from the doorway.](assets/images/feedback/comic-08-no-surprises.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: the measure of the system — a review with nothing new in it.*
