**Panel 1:** *The hook: excellent work, done silently, waiting for the organization to notice.*

![Comic panel: Arlo holds a thin flat review sheet and blames politics in a thought bubble.](assets/images/own-your-career/comic-02-the-flat-review.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the flat review that follows — read as proof of politics, when it is proof of missing information.*

![Comic panel: Arlo slides a tangled yarn ball labeled my career across the table to Vera.](assets/images/own-your-career/comic-03-outsourcing-the-career.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: handing the whole tangle to the manager — a savior cannot drive a career any more than an adversary can.*

![Comic panel: Vera unrolls a seven-spoke habit wheel diagram in front of Arlo.](assets/images/own-your-career/comic-04-ownership-is-habits.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: ownership is a habit set — goals, finishing, evidence, feedback, alliance, pacing — not luck and not politics.*

![Comic panel: Arlo hands Vera a goal card while she matches it against open slots on a project board.](assets/images/own-your-career/comic-05-say-what-you-want.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: say what you want — a manager who has to guess your goals will guess wrong, and staff you by convenience.*

![Comic panel: Arlo writes a weekly line in a small work-log notebook with link icons, clock showing fifteen minutes.](assets/images/own-your-career/comic-06-the-work-log.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: fifteen minutes a week in the work log — review evidence, 1:1 backbone, and a tool for saying no, all in one notebook.*

![Comic panel: Arlo shows Vera a slipping commitment on a calendar long before the deadline while she calmly notes it.](assets/images/own-your-career/comic-07-the-early-update.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: telling your manager early when a commitment slips — the trust that advocacy runs on is built from exactly these moments.*

![Comic panel: Vera holds a door open as Arlo walks through carrying his notebook and goal flag.](assets/images/own-your-career/comic-08-doors-open-for-owners.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: I can open doors and advocate — but the engineers who practice these habits are the ones I can actually help.*
