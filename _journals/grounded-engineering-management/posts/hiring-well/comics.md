![Comic panel: Arlo holds a resignation letter and stares at an empty desk chair in a panic.](assets/images/hiring-well/comic-01-the-empty-chair.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: a resignation lands, and the reflex is to fill the hole — fast.*

![Comic panel: Arlo and Vera look at a lopsided patchwork team chart on the wall.](assets/images/hiring-well/comic-02-shape-by-attrition.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: hire reactively for long enough and the team's shape becomes an accident of attrition.*

![Comic panel: Arlo enthusiastically hires a candidate after a thirty-minute chat, clock on the wall.](assets/images/hiring-well/comic-03-the-gut-feel-hire.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: a gut-feel verdict from one short conversation — interviews are far too noisy for that.*

![Comic panel: Vera at a whiteboard with a year-end team blueprint showing dashed outlines for planned roles, Arlo taking notes.](assets/images/hiring-well/comic-04-build-the-future-team.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: hiring is building the future of the team — plan the year-end team and its gaps before the req opens.*

![Comic panel: Arlo writes the job description at his laptop while a recruiter shows a sourcing list beside him.](assets/images/hiring-well/comic-05-own-the-process.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: own it personally — write the job description yourself and shape a pipeline that feels personal, not generic.*

![Comic panel: interviewers hold sealed independent scorecards while one raises a hand as the passionate advocate, Arlo chairing.](assets/images/hiring-well/comic-06-the-advocate-test.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: same core questions, distinct dimensions, independent debriefs — and no offer without at least one passionate advocate.*

![Comic panel: Arlo declines a marginal candidate while the empty chair waits behind him, Vera reassuring him.](assets/images/hiring-well/comic-07-the-open-chair-holds.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: the bar means saying no to a "maybe" while the chair stays empty — because a weak hire compounds on the whole team.*

![Comic panel: a diverse thriving team a year later, the new hire high-fiving Arlo while Vera looks on.](assets/images/hiring-well/comic-08-the-hire-compounds.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: every hire is a bet on the team's long-term capability and its diversity of perspective — the strong ones compound for years.*
