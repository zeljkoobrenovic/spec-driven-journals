# Spec: Hiring Well

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I hire onto teams I manage, grounded in the hiring chapter of
Julie Zhuo's *The Making of a Manager*: hiring as deliberate team-building
rather than hole-filling. The record covers the whole arc — planning the
year-end team and its gaps before opening a role, owning the process
personally even with recruiters, building a sourcing pipeline, running
consistent interviews with independent debriefs, asking questions that
surface real work and self-awareness, deciding on evidence with a
no-weak-hire bar and a passionate advocate, hiring for diversity of
perspective, closing fast and warmly, scaling with systems, and slowing
down for leadership hires. The load-bearing idea: every hire is a bet on
the team's long-term capability, so hiring is designed, not reacted to.

## Audience

Managers in my organization (this is the hiring craft I hold them to);
recruiting partners (so they know where accountability sits); peer
executives comparing operating models. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states hiring as building the
      future of the team, never filling holes, with the no-weak-hire bar
      and the advocate test.
- [x] **The planning discipline survives** — year-end team map, gap
      analysis, diversity intent, and level/scope per role, before the req
      opens.
- [x] **The interview discipline survives** — prepared questions, a
      consistent core set, interviewers on distinct dimensions, independent
      debriefs before group discussion, and the better-questions list
      (real output, owned contributions, conflict, growth).
- [x] **The decision bar survives** — interviews are noisy, no gut feel,
      no weak hires, at least one passionate advocate, toxic behavior
      rejected regardless of skill, long-term capability favored.
- [x] **Scale and leadership hiring survive** — funnel metrics, trained
      interviewers, systems over heroics; extra research and values
      alignment for manager and senior hires; the long game of
      relationships and hiring culture.
- [x] **Credit is explicit** — References name Julie Zhuo's *The Making of
      a Manager* and the hiring chapter.

## Non-goals

- Not [[hiring]] (executive journal) — that record is the executive's
  hiring *system*: loops, leveling frameworks, compensation governance,
  monthly metrics. This one is the hiring manager's craft inside that
  system.
- Not [[interview-loop]] (people journal) — that record carries the
  structured loop mechanics; this one states the operating principle the
  loop serves.
- Not [[managing-a-growing-team]] — what happens after the hires land and
  the team scales; this record stops at the accepted offer and the culture
  that keeps the bar high.
