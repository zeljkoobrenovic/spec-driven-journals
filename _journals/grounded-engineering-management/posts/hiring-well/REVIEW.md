- **[index.md · Rationale, ¶4 ("A 'weak hire' is the most expensive decision…")]** Diversity of perspective — named in the spec's Intent as its own beat and given its own checklist section (§7) — is argued only as a bolted-on final clause of the weak-hire paragraph. *Give it its own sentence-level beat, or its own short paragraph.*

Met across modalities, but the article promises less than the spec lists. *One clause naming the self-awareness/conflict/growth probes would close it.*
- **[comics.md · Panels 5–8]** The comic's captions never carry the diversity or warm-close beats (panel 8's alt text shows "a diverse thriving team" but the caption doesn't say it). Acceptable compression for the form, but both are named in the spec's Intent. *Panel 8's caption could absorb the diversity note in a phrase.*

### Nits

- **[index.md · Scope and Revisiting]** "if a hire made without a passionate advocate recurs" — a hire doesn't "recur"; slightly tangled. Suggest "if I again make a hire without a passionate advocate."
- **[comics.md · Panel 7 vs index.md · Concretely ¶]** Quote-style inconsistency: panel 7 has 'maybe' in single quotes; the article uses "maybe" in double quotes.
