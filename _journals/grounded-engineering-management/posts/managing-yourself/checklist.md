*The working checklist behind this record. The Article tab carries the rationale and anti-patterns.*

## 1. Know yourself

- [ ] Identify my top strengths
- [ ] Identify my biggest weaknesses
- [ ] Write down 3 words people close to me would use to describe me
- [ ] List 3 qualities I am most proud of
- [ ] Name traits that helped me succeed in the past
- [ ] List the most common positive feedback I receive
- [ ] Notice my inner-critic patterns
- [ ] Identify situations that trigger self-doubt or overreaction
- [ ] Write down common feedback on how I could be more effective

## 2. Calibrate with reality

- [ ] Ask my manager where they see my biggest opportunities for growth
- [ ] Ask my manager what is holding me back from greater impact
- [ ] Ask how my skills compare with the ideal person in my role
- [ ] Ask 3–7 trusted coworkers for honest feedback
- [ ] Request specific, actionable feedback rather than general praise
- [ ] Ask for task-specific feedback after presentations, meetings, or projects
- [ ] Thank people for feedback without getting defensive

## 3. Shift out of imposter syndrome

- [ ] Notice when I am telling myself a worst-case story
- [ ] Pause and ask: "What evidence do I actually have?"
- [ ] Consider alternative explanations before assuming the worst
- [ ] Replace fixed-mindset thoughts with growth-mindset thoughts
- [ ] Treat discomfort as part of learning, not proof I am failing
- [ ] Remind myself that early confusion in management is normal

## 4. Understand my best conditions

- [ ] Identify the conditions in which I do my best work
- [ ] Notice what gives me energy and momentum
- [ ] Protect time for thinking and reflection
- [ ] Get enough sleep
- [ ] Create routines that support focus and recovery
- [ ] Structure my calendar in a way that helps me work well

## 5. Understand my triggers

- [ ] Notice which people or situations reliably set me off
- [ ] Separate normal frustration from strong emotional triggers
- [ ] Reflect on moments when I overreacted
- [ ] Identify recurring pet peeves and why they affect me
- [ ] Pause before responding when triggered
- [ ] Cool down before making decisions or replying

## 6. Build confidence when I'm struggling

- [ ] Stop punishing myself for feeling bad
- [ ] Name the worry directly instead of letting it swirl
- [ ] Question whether my interpretation is accurate
- [ ] Visualize handling hard situations successfully
- [ ] Remember the previous challenges I have handled well
- [ ] Ask trusted people for help when I am in a rut
- [ ] Keep a record of small wins
- [ ] Celebrate progress, not just big outcomes

## 7. Protect my wellbeing

- [ ] Set boundaries around work time
- [ ] Schedule short non-work breaks into my day
- [ ] Make time for exercise, hobbies, or rest
- [ ] Preserve time with family and friends
- [ ] Avoid letting work consume all mental space

## 8. Learn faster as a manager

- [ ] Ask for feedback often
- [ ] Use my manager as a coach, not just an evaluator
- [ ] Bring hard problems to my manager early
- [ ] Treat 1:1s as learning time
- [ ] Learn from peers and informal mentors
- [ ] Ask skilled coworkers how they do what they do
- [ ] Set aside regular time to reflect on what I learned
- [ ] Review my week and write down key takeaways
- [ ] Set goals for the next 6 months
- [ ] Revisit goals and evaluate what worked
- [ ] Take advantage of formal training when useful

## 9. Keep perspective

- [ ] Focus on becoming better, not perfect
- [ ] Play to my strengths instead of copying someone else's style
- [ ] Remember that good management is personal
- [ ] Stay true to my values while improving my skills
