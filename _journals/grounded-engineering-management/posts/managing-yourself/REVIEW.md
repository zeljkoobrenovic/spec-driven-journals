### Minor

- **[index.md · highlight vs Rationale ¶1]** The same load-bearing idea gets two competing metaphors: "inner critic wearing a lab coat" (highlight) and "inner critic with better vocabulary" (Rationale ¶1 opener). Each is good; having both dilutes quotability and reads as the author trying the line twice. *Standardize on one (the comic and spec use the plain version — that may be the tiebreaker).*
- **[index.md · Statement bullet 1]** The inventory bullet is a 45+-word comma chain of six list items — the densest sentence in the post and a hard first bullet. *Break after "biggest weaknesses" or point to the Checklist tab for the full inventory.*
- **[index.md · Rationale ¶4 "Wellbeing is what keeps my judgment available"]** The paragraph switches subject midway: it opens on wellbeing boundaries, then pivots at "When I am struggling" into the recovery moves (checklist §6) with no setup. The recovery discipline is real content from the source but arrives as a rider on a different argument. *Either give it its own bolded paragraph or trail the pivot ("and when the reserve runs out anyway…").*

### Nits

- **[index.md · Figure 2 caption / comics.md · Panel 6 caption]** Both end on the verbatim clause "it usually loses" — fine if deliberate, but worth knowing it repeats word-for-word across modalities.
- **[index.md · Statement bullet 1 vs checklist.md §1]** The article compresses the inventory and silently drops two source items ("3 qualities I am most proud of", "common feedback on how I could be more effective") that the checklist carries — acceptable division of labor, noted for awareness.
