![Comic panel: Vera draws a loop from Arlo's org-chart box back to himself.](assets/images/managing-yourself/comic-01-the-first-report.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: before the team, there is one report nobody assigned you — yourself.*

![Comic panel: a storm-cloud thought bubble with a mocking silhouette looms over Arlo at his desk.](assets/images/managing-yourself/comic-02-the-inner-critic.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the worst-case story sounds like perception — it is only an interpretation.*

![Comic panel: Arlo hides a problem folder under his desk and waves off an opportunity while a storm cloud hovers.](assets/images/managing-yourself/comic-03-obeying-the-story.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: obeying the story — hiding hard problems and declining chances the evidence never justified.*

![Comic panel: Vera hands Arlo a two-column self-inventory sheet linked by strings to coworker portraits.](assets/images/managing-yourself/comic-04-write-it-down-check-it.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: self-knowledge that never leaves your head is just the inner critic — calibration makes it real.*

![Comic panel: Arlo asks three coworkers for specific feedback right after a presentation, sheet in hand.](assets/images/managing-yourself/comic-05-asking-for-the-mirror.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: ask right after the task, ask for specifics — and say thank you, not 'yes but'.*

![Comic panel: Arlo weighs a deflated storm cloud against a heavier stack of evidence blocks on a scale.](assets/images/managing-yourself/comic-06-cross-examine-the-cloud.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: cross-examine the story — weighed against evidence, it usually loses.*

![Comic panel: Arlo closes his laptop on time and writes his weekly review beside a six-month goal calendar.](assets/images/managing-yourself/comic-07-the-boring-disciplines.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: none of it is glamorous — boundaries, breaks, a written weekly review, goals actually revisited.*

![Comic panel: Arlo and Vera walk up a milestone-flagged path, ignoring a faded perfection star in the sky.](assets/images/managing-yourself/comic-08-better-not-perfect.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: good management is personal — aim at better, not perfect.*
