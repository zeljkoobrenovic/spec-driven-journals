*The working checklist behind this record. The Article tab carries the rationale and anti-patterns.*

## 1. Set direction

- [ ] Define a concrete vision with a clear, measurable outcome
- [ ] Make sure the vision is easy to repeat and hard to misinterpret
- [ ] Test whether different people describe the goal the same way
- [ ] Write a believable plan for how the team will get there

## 2. Build the plan

- [ ] Base the plan on the team's real strengths and constraints
- [ ] Choose the few priorities that matter most
- [ ] Cut or defer lower-value work
- [ ] Break big goals into smaller milestones
- [ ] Add buffer for delays, surprises, and changing conditions

## 3. Organize execution

- [ ] Define who owns each decision, task, and follow-up
- [ ] Clarify what must be done, by whom, and by when
- [ ] Prioritize work from most important to least important
- [ ] Make decisions quickly when enough information is available
- [ ] Once a decision is made, commit and move forward
- [ ] Revisit plans promptly when important new information becomes available

## 4. Balance time horizons

- [ ] Define the long-term vision first, then work backward
- [ ] Connect short-term work to long-term goals
- [ ] Keep a portfolio of work: short-term improvements, medium-term projects, longer-term bets
- [ ] Avoid over-optimizing for only the immediate term
- [ ] Avoid locking into long-term plans that no longer fit reality

## 5. Improve process

- [ ] Run regular debriefs / retrospectives
- [ ] Focus debriefs on learning, not blame
- [ ] Capture lessons and share them with others
- [ ] Turn repeated work into playbooks or checklists
- [ ] Update processes as the team learns

## 6. Signs the team is executing well

- [ ] Top priorities get the most time and attention
- [ ] There is a trusted process for making decisions
- [ ] The team moves quickly, especially on reversible decisions
- [ ] People commit after decisions, even if they disagreed earlier
- [ ] New information leads to fast reassessment
- [ ] Every task has a clear owner and deadline
- [ ] The team keeps learning and gets stronger after mistakes
