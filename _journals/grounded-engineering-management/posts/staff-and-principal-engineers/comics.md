![Comic panel: Arlo stacks a tall tower of identical code blocks toward a shelf labeled staff.](assets/images/staff-and-principal-engineers/comic-01-more-of-the-same.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the obvious route to staff — more senior work, done harder — is the wrong one.*

![Comic panel: Arlo's block tower wobbles while pedestals labeled business, influence, and teams stand untouched and cobwebbed.](assets/images/staff-and-principal-engineers/comic-02-the-failed-transition.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the volume of personal output grows, but business understanding, influence, and cross-team scope stay untouched.*

![Comic panel: Arlo drops a giant architecture scroll from a tower to confused engineers below.](assets/images/staff-and-principal-engineers/comic-03-the-ivory-tower.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the ivory-tower architect — painfully precise, detached from the work, gone before implementation.*

![Comic panel: Vera draws a crossed-out vertical arrow and a five-pointed star of staff-plus dimensions around a hands-on core.](assets/images/staff-and-principal-engineers/comic-04-the-change-of-axis.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: beyond senior, the grade changes axis — five dimensions together, anchored by staying hands-on.*

![Comic panel: Arlo and a product manager trace a line from a code icon to a coin icon under a North Star chart.](assets/images/staff-and-principal-engineers/comic-05-know-the-business.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: know the North Star, the customers, and the money — so technical judgment picks problems worth solving.*

![Comic panel: Arlo walks peers through a proposal with tradeoffs while a listener holds a trust token and a megaphone sits crossed out.](assets/images/staff-and-principal-engineers/comic-06-trust-not-title.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: influence and execution across teams both run on trust capital — proposals with problem, options, tradeoffs — never bulldozed through a title.*

![Comic panel: Arlo with a pager and a monitoring dashboard pauses at a one-way vault door, checklist in hand.](assets/images/staff-and-principal-engineers/comic-07-the-pager-and-the-vault-door.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The discipline: reliability owned end to end, and homework scaled to blast radius — two-way doors move fast, one-way doors do not.*

![Comic panel: Arlo reviews code with a junior engineer, work log open, while Vera notes approvingly from the doorway.](assets/images/staff-and-principal-engineers/comic-08-grounded-at-the-table.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: stay hands-on enough that every decision is grounded in the actual work — the anchor that keeps all five dimensions honest.*
