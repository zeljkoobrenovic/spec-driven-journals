# Spec: Amazing Meetings

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State my operating model for meetings, distilled from the meetings chapter
of Julie Zhuo's *The Making of a Manager*: every meeting has one primary
purpose out of five (making a decision, sharing information, providing
feedback, generating ideas, strengthening relationships) with success
stateable in advance; format, attendee list, and preparation are chosen to
serve that purpose; participation is made safe and airtime actively managed
so quieter voices contribute; every meeting closes with summarized outcomes
and action items that have owners and timelines; and each purpose carries
its own specific checks. Above all sits the gut-check — is this a good use
of people's time, does the meeting truly need to happen, do I personally
need to be there — with "no" treated as an instruction to cancel,
restructure, or decline. The load-bearing idea: one primary purpose is the
design constraint that makes every other meeting decision decidable.

## Audience

Managers in my organization (the bar for any meeting they run); everyone
who attends or is invited to my meetings (what they can expect: purpose,
prep, airtime, owners); peer executives comparing operating models.
First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states that a meeting earns its
      calendar slot with one primary purpose everything else serves, and
      ends with the gut-check on whether it needs to happen at all.
- [x] **The five purposes survive** — decision, information-sharing,
      feedback, idea-generation, relationship-building, with success
      stateable by the end and one meeting not doing too many things.
- [x] **Design choices follow purpose** — format fits the goal; everyone
      necessary and no one extraneous invited, affected people represented;
      agenda and pre-reads in advance; follow-up capture planned before the
      meeting.
- [x] **Facilitation is active** — safety for dissent and honest input,
      formats for quieter people, watching interruptions, directed
      questions, limiting overtalking politely but firmly.
- [x] **The close and the per-purpose checks survive** — summary before
      ending, owners and timelines on every action item, and the five
      purpose-specific check blocks (decision-maker designated, live-worthy
      information, improve-not-judge feedback, quiet thinking time, trust
      and connection).
- [x] **The gut-check survives** — good use of time, truly needs to happen,
      I personally need to be there.
- [x] **Credit is explicit** — References name Julie Zhuo's *The Making of
      a Manager*.
