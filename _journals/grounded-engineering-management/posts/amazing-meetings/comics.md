**Panel 2:** *The problem: the everything meeting — three purposes at once, none of them served.*

![Comic panel: a huge invite list, then a packed room of passive observers watching two people talk.](assets/images/amazing-meetings/comic-03-the-audience-meeting.jpeg)

![Comic panel: Vera circles one of five purpose chips on a whiteboard while Arlo watches.](assets/images/amazing-meetings/comic-04-one-primary-purpose.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: one primary purpose, stateable as an outcome — the constraint that makes every other choice decidable.*

![Comic panel: Arlo trims an attendee list while pre-read paper planes fly from his laptop to recipients.](assets/images/amazing-meetings/comic-05-designed-backward.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: the meeting is designed before it opens — necessary people only, context sent ahead.*

![Comic panel: Arlo pauses an overtalker with one hand and invites a quiet attendee with the other.](assets/images/amazing-meetings/comic-06-managing-the-airtime.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: facilitation is active work — the loudest voices are not a representative sample.*

![Comic panel: Arlo hovers over the delete button of a cobwebbed recurring meeting entry while Vera nods.](assets/images/amazing-meetings/comic-07-killing-your-own-meeting.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: the gut-check spares no one — including the meeting you created.*

![Comic panel: a closing summary board with names and dates on every action, next to a calendar with open white space.](assets/images/amazing-meetings/comic-08-owners-and-negative-space.jpeg)
