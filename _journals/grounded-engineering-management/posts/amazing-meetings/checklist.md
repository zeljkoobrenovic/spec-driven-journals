*The working checklist behind this record. The Article tab carries the rationale and anti-patterns.*

## 1. Define the outcome

- [ ] I know the primary purpose of this meeting
- [ ] The meeting is mainly for one of these: making a decision, sharing information, providing feedback, generating ideas, or strengthening relationships
- [ ] I can clearly state what success looks like by the end

## 2. Match the format to the purpose

- [ ] The structure of the meeting fits its goal
- [ ] I am not trying to make one meeting do too many things
- [ ] I will redirect off-topic discussion and park unrelated issues

## 3. Invite the right people

- [ ] Everyone necessary is invited
- [ ] No one extraneous is invited
- [ ] The people affected by the decision or discussion are represented
- [ ] The attendee list supports useful discussion, not passive observation

## 4. Help people come prepared

- [ ] The agenda is sent in advance
- [ ] Pre-reads or slides are shared in advance when needed
- [ ] Participants have enough context to contribute thoughtfully
- [ ] I have planned how follow-ups and next steps will be captured

## 5. Make participation safe and easy

- [ ] I will explicitly encourage questions, dissent, and honest input
- [ ] I will set norms for respectful discussion
- [ ] I will use a format that helps quieter people contribute
- [ ] I will avoid letting only the loudest voices dominate

## 6. Manage airtime

- [ ] I will watch for interruptions
- [ ] I will make space for people who have not spoken
- [ ] I will use directed questions when helpful
- [ ] I will limit overtalking politely but firmly

## 7. Close with clarity

- [ ] We will summarize decisions, feedback, or ideas before ending
- [ ] We will agree on next steps
- [ ] Each action item has an owner
- [ ] Each action item has a timeline or next check-in

## 8. Purpose-specific checks

*Run the block that matches this meeting's primary purpose.*

**Decision meeting**

- [ ] A clear decision-maker is designated
- [ ] Credible options are presented objectively
- [ ] Relevant background information is included
- [ ] The team recommendation is clear, if there is one
- [ ] Dissenting views will get fair airtime
- [ ] People affected by the decision will feel the process was fair

**Information-sharing meeting**

- [ ] The information is worth sharing live rather than by email/chat
- [ ] The content is clear and memorable
- [ ] The presentation is engaging
- [ ] The audience should leave having learned something valuable

**Feedback meeting**

- [ ] The purpose is to improve work in progress, not to judge it
- [ ] Success criteria are clear
- [ ] Current status, concerns, and changes are presented honestly
- [ ] Specific questions are framed to get useful feedback
- [ ] Next steps and future milestones are clear

**Idea-generation meeting**

- [ ] People have quiet time to think before or during discussion
- [ ] The format collects ideas from everyone, not just the loudest
- [ ] The discussion helps ideas build on each other
- [ ] The meeting ends with a path to action

**Relationship-building meeting**

- [ ] The meeting is designed to build trust and understanding
- [ ] It encourages openness and authenticity
- [ ] People should leave feeling cared for and more connected

## 9. Final gut-check

- [ ] This meeting is a good use of people's time
- [ ] Attendees will likely leave with new knowledge, clearer next steps, a sense of engagement, and a feeling of being welcomed
- [ ] This meeting truly needs to happen
- [ ] I personally need to be there
