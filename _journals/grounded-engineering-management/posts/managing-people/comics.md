![Comic panel: Arlo stares anxiously at an org chart with three reports while Vera calmly replies.](assets/images/managing-people/comic-01-not-a-natural.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: people management looks like a talent — it is actually a set of learnable mechanics.*

![Comic panel: a shocked engineer holds a review document across the table from an uncomfortable Arlo.](assets/images/managing-people/comic-02-the-blindsided-review.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: when feedback waits for the formal moment, the formal moment becomes an ambush.*

![Comic panel: Arlo deletes a 1:1 from his calendar while stuffing feedback notes into an overflowing drawer.](assets/images/managing-people/comic-03-hoarded-feedback.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: cancel the 1:1 for 'real work', hoard the observations, deliver them all at once later.*

![Comic panel: Vera at a whiteboard with NO SURPRISES circled and spokes to small icons, Arlo taking notes.](assets/images/managing-people/comic-04-no-surprises.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: every mechanic — 1:1s, feedback, reviews, plans — exists so nothing important lands as a surprise.*

![Comic panel: Arlo and a new hire share coffee over a 30/60/90 plan sheet.](assets/images/managing-people/comic-05-the-deliberate-start.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: every new report gets a deliberate start — preference questions and a joint 30/60/90 plan.*

![Comic panel: Arlo drags a 1:1 calendar block to a new day beneath a timeline of small feedback bubbles.](assets/images/managing-people/comic-06-moved-never-skipped.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: 1:1s are weekly by default and rescheduled rather than skipped; feedback flows continuously in small doses.*

![Comic panel: Arlo holds a calm documented conversation with an engineer while Vera taps her notebook.](assets/images/managing-people/comic-07-say-it-early.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: the rule demands the uncomfortable conversation early and in writing — not deferred until it is a crisis.*

![Comic panel: a relaxed engineer reads a review and nods while Arlo smiles and Vera approves from the doorway.](assets/images/managing-people/comic-08-the-boring-review.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: when the mechanics run all year, the review is boring — and boring is the win.*
