![Comic panel: Arlo stares at a cost-cuts memo at night with a storm cloud over his head.](assets/images/managing-downturn/comic-01-the-memo-lands.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the downturn lands on the manager first — as guilt, anxiety, and isolation.*

![Comic panel: Arlo's strained face transmits visible anxiety waves to his worried team.](assets/images/managing-downturn/comic-02-the-team-reads-his-face.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: a manager who has not processed his own state transmits it.*

![Comic panel: Arlo delivers forced cheer while rumor scribbles grow on the wall behind unconvinced teammates.](assets/images/managing-downturn/comic-03-fake-optimism.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: fake optimism — spending credibility exactly when it is needed most, while the rumor gap fills itself.*

![Comic panel: Vera shows Arlo a notebook with two crossed-out stories and one circled replacement.](assets/images/managing-downturn/comic-04-own-head-first.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: drop 'this is happening to me' and 'I should have been perfect' — keep 'I can influence how we navigate this.'*

![Comic panel: Arlo briefs his team at a whiteboard with know, don't-know, and next-update columns.](assets/images/managing-downturn/comic-05-the-cadence-board.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: what I know, what I don't, and when you'll hear more — on a cadence the team can trust.*

![Comic panel: Arlo flags one top goal and crosses out other workstreams while Vera approves.](assets/images/managing-downturn/comic-06-one-goal-stop-the-rest.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: scarcity mode — one top goal, an explicit stop-doing list, and permission to say no.*

![Comic panel: Arlo delivers hard news face to face, uncomfortable but honest, with the reason attached.](assets/images/managing-downturn/comic-07-hard-news-honestly.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: hard news delivered fast and honestly feels bad even when led well — discomfort is not failure.*

![Comic panel: a steadier team around Arlo with a bridge on the whiteboard as Vera nods from the doorway.](assets/images/managing-downturn/comic-08-trust-deepens.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: trust deepens when you show up honestly in hard times — that is the asset a downturn can build.*
