![Comic panel: Arlo celebrates his tech lead badge while turning straight back to his laptop, as Vera watches knowingly.](assets/images/tech-lead/comic-01-now-i-can-code-more.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the title arrives — and the first instinct is to keep doing the old job, harder.*

![Comic panel: Arlo codes intensely while teammates stand blocked and the project board drifts off the table behind him.](assets/images/tech-lead/comic-02-project-drifts.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: his output has never been higher — and the project has never been more adrift.*

![Comic panel: a split scene of Arlo as a bottleneck with a queue versus a ghostly absent figurehead at an empty desk.](assets/images/tech-lead/comic-03-bottleneck-or-ghost.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way, twice: own every decision and become the queue — or own none and leave the team leaderless.*

![Comic panel: Vera's whiteboard contrasts one person lifting alone with a lead guiding four teammates carrying one block, a crown crossed out in the corner.](assets/images/tech-lead/comic-04-team-outcomes-no-authority.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: the job stops being 'write code' and becomes 'help the team deliver' — held on influence, not authority.*

![Comic panel: Arlo sorts decision sticky notes into make, delegate, and escalate columns as a teammate takes one.](assets/images/tech-lead/comic-05-make-delegate-escalate.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: every technical decision gets sorted — made where his knowledge is load-bearing, delegated to grow people, escalated when above the project.*

![Comic panel: Arlo runs a premortem over a sequenced plan with parallel lanes, starred critical tasks, and launch and rollback flags.](assets/images/tech-lead/comic-06-plan-premortem-rollback.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: convert ambiguity into sequence — critical path marked, premortem before, launch plan during, rollback plan in case.*

![Comic panel: Arlo accepts a smaller personal code stack while the team's delivery arrow grows, with a crossed-out hero cape on the wall.](assets/images/tech-lead/comic-07-slower-for-me.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: slower personal technical progress and no heroic saves — sustainable team leverage is the trade.*

![Comic panel: the team celebrates a shipped release while Vera shows her notebook of sorted decisions to a relaxed Arlo.](assets/images/tech-lead/comic-08-that-is-leading.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: the team ships, and the tech lead can say without preparation which decisions they made, delegated, and escalated — that is leading.*
