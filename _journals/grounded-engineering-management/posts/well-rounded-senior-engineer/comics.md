![Comic panel: Arlo stands proudly atop a tower of commits while Vera looks on unimpressed.](assets/images/well-rounded-senior-engineer/comic-01-i-ship-the-most.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: more output, presented as seniority — raw effort is a junior metric.*

![Comic panel: a pager wakes an on-call engineer while a long road of operations, bugs, and migrations stretches ahead.](assets/images/well-rounded-senior-engineer/comic-02-the-system-lives-on.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the developer's job ends when the code works — but the system must be operated, debugged, and migrated for years.*

![Comic panel: Arlo silently holds a huge iceberg of hidden complexity underwater while stakeholders call him slow.](assets/images/well-rounded-senior-engineer/comic-03-the-quiet-hero.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the quiet hero — absorbing hidden complexity silently, then being read as slow.*

![Comic panel: Vera contrasts a short arrow ending at 'works' with a long arrow spanning the system's life.](assets/images/well-rounded-senior-engineer/comic-04-senior-is-a-shift.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: senior is the developer-to-engineer shift — thinking in system lifetimes, not bigger piles of the same job.*

![Comic panel: Arlo lifts the iceberg into view and offers stakeholders three options on a card.](assets/images/well-rounded-senior-engineer/comic-05-surface-it-with-options.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it runs: make complexity visible early — roadblocks arrive with options attached, not as silent heroics.*

![Comic panel: Arlo reviews and pairs while teammates around him each gain a small upward arrow.](assets/images/well-rounded-senior-engineer/comic-06-the-collaboration-surface.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The mechanic: the collaboration surface — reviews, pairing, mentoring, feedback — is where senior impact multiplies.*

![Comic panel: Arlo presents a cost curve for tech debt to a stakeholder, a crumpled complaint discarded.](assets/images/well-rounded-senior-engineer/comic-07-argue-the-economics.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The cost: 'the code is bad' is not an argument — debt and testing are economics, quantified and tied to business impact.*

![Comic panel: Vera shows Arlo three framed surfaces of senior impact while the old commit tower sits forgotten.](assets/images/well-rounded-senior-engineer/comic-08-impact-not-effort.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: the measure is impact — a sustainable system, a surfaced complexity, and a person made better. All three.*
