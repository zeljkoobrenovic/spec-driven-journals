The book starts from one source, the October 2026 *Pragmatic Engineer* episode with Uday Kiran Medisetty, and widens it with three kinds of evidence: **Uber's own written record** (engineering blog posts, papers, talks and open-source repositories), **reporting about Uber** (The Pragmatic Engineer's earlier articles and the press), and **industry and research context** (other companies' engineering accounts, studies, vendor guidance, standards and product documentation). This page lists every source cited in a chapter's "Sources and Evidence" section once, under the kind of evidence it is, so a reader can see at a glance **how much of a claim rests on Uber's account and how much on independent sources**.

The grouping follows the book's evidence labels ([[introduction]] explains them). A statement backed only by the episode is Uber's own account, given from memory. A statement backed by an entry in "Uber's engineering record" is documented by Uber in writing, with a date; older posts describe systems that may have changed since. Entries under "Industry and research" support comparisons and general claims, never facts about Uber's internals.
