> **IN THIS PAGE, YOU WILL:** Look up any Uber system named in the book, see in one row what it does, when it appeared, how strong the public evidence for it is, which foundation it depends on and which chapter explains it, and then see the AI-era systems grouped by the pre-AI foundation each one reuses.

<br>
The October 2026 *Pragmatic Engineer* episode moves through close to two dozen internal systems in about an hour and a half, and the chapters add dozens more from Uber's written record: the platforms the AI tools stand on, their documented predecessors and the components Uber has described in its 2026 posts. This page is the index to all of them. **Each row answers four questions at once: what the system does, when it appeared, how much of it is on the public record, and what it stands on.** The last column links to the chapter that explains the system in depth, with its sources.

The evidence column matters more than it looks. **Many AI-era names are known only from the episode**, which is Uber's own account given from memory; other systems have dated Uber blog posts, papers or talks behind them. A row marked "podcast only" is not doubtful, but nobody outside Uber has documented it, and the chapter treats its details as Uber's claim. Dates are the documented ones where they exist; where the episode and the written record disagree, the [[timeline]] reconciles them once, and this page follows it.

## How to Read the Catalog

| Column | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **System** | Uber's name as Uber uses it. Where written sources use another name for the same system, the row says so and an alias row points to it. |
| **What it does** | One or two sentences on purpose and mechanism, as far as the public record shows. Figures from the episode are Uber's own account. |
| **Era** | When the system appeared or was first described. "→" means still in use as of October 2026, as far as the record shows. |
| **Evidence** | **Podcast only**: named or described only in the October 2026 episode or its show notes. **Documented (year)**: described in a dated Uber blog post, paper, talk or open-source repository, and not discussed in the episode. **Both**: in the episode and in a dated written source; the year in brackets is the first or main written source. |
| **Depends on** | The foundation the system reuses, as the chapters describe it. "Foundation" means the system is itself one of the pre-AI layers; "Not described" means the book has no source for its dependencies. |
| **Chapter** | The chapter that owns the system, or for systems mentioned only in passing, the page where the book discusses it. |

Names follow the fact ledger's corrected spellings. The planning tool the auto-generated transcript renders as "U-Plan" appears here as the **design-doc hub**, because its real name is unconfirmed. Industry systems named for comparison (Stripe's Minions and Toolshed, Spotify's Honk and Fleet Management, Google's Rosie, Meta's RADAR) are not Uber systems and are not listed; their sources are in the [[bibliography]].
