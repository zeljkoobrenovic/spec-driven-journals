## The group

- **Northmere Group** — a multinational, technology-driven, multi-brand digital commerce group. Listed in New York (SEC registrant, US GAAP, SOX 404 applies). Headquarters in Amsterdam; major hubs in London, Dublin, Singapore, Austin and São Paulo.
- **Reporting currency:** US dollar. Most European entities have euro functional currency.
- **2026 figures (fiscal year = calendar year):** gross transaction value (GTV) about **$64 billion**; revenue about **$8.2 billion**; about **24,000 employees**; **71 legal entities in 38 countries**; customers pay in **46 currencies**; Northmere settles with partners in **31 currencies**.
- **Statutory reporting:** local GAAP or IFRS per entity; group reports under US GAAP.
- **Northmere is not a bank.** Its licensed payments entity is an e-money institution (see Northmere Pay).

## The 2027 situation — problems Northmere starts with

1. **Close takes 9 working days** (group). Target: **5** by end of 2028, **3** by 2030 (continuous close aspiration).
2. **Payments-to-ledger gap:** at working day 3 of each close, ~**1.9%** of PSP settlement lines (by count) for Stayloft APMs are unmatched; unreconciled balances in PSP clearing/suspense accounts averaged **€14 million** at month-end in 2026, with items older than 90 days ~€2.1 million.
3. **Three charts of accounts** (SAP group CoA, Oracle Wayfare CoA, Workday Tablekin CoA) mapped only in OneStream; mappings maintained in spreadsheets.
4. **Duplicate capabilities:** two partner-payout engines (SFP and a Wayfare engine for ground-transport suppliers), three billing approaches (SFP commission billing, FI-CA invoicing, Stripe Billing), reconciliations performed in BlackLine *and* in a OneStream module bought in 2022 (rarely used) *and* in Oracle Fusion for Wayfare intercompany.
5. **Audit finding (FY2026):** a **significant deficiency** (not a material weakness) related to change management and access on the SFP payout engine — developers had production access, and payout rule changes lacked documented approval. Remediation in progress.
6. **Custom code in S/4:** ~2,300 custom objects carried from early design decisions; clean-core score low; upgrades take 7 months.
7. **Regulatory stressors on the horizon:** EU ViDA (e-invoicing/digital reporting; platform deemed-supplier rules for short-term accommodation), Pillar Two (GloBE information return), national e-invoicing mandates (e.g. Poland, Belgium, France, Germany), PSD3/PSR, DORA ongoing, Brazil market launch.

## Program Keystone

Northmere's multi-year finance transformation, approved by the board in **December 2026**, sponsored by Tomás, design authority chaired by Dana with Leila.

- **Year 1 (2027) — Govern and stabilise:** one architecture decision record and review board; mandatory standards; payments-to-ledger completeness for cards and APMs; publish the financial-systems target state; portfolio ROI baseline; remediate the SFP significant deficiency.
- **Year 3 (2029) — One finance core:** converge brand ledgers onto a single group finance core on S/4HANA (decision on Central Finance vs accounting-hub pattern pending in 2027 — chapters may discuss it as an open decision); canonical financial events and a brand adapter kit; one group chart of accounts; tax determination and e-invoicing as shared services; retire duplicate payout engine.
- **Year 5 (2031) — Continuous, real-time finance:** continuous close (3 days), AI-assisted reconciliation and controls with auditable evidence, real-time cash and liability views, new brands or markets onboarded as configuration.
- **Budget:** about **$310 million** over five years (capex + opex), with a target of **$95 million** a year of run-rate savings by 2030 from retired systems, reduced manual effort and lower PSP and bank costs.

## Recurring worked examples (keep amounts consistent)

- **The Lisbon booking (merchant model):** a traveler from Germany books a 3-night stay in a Lisbon apartment on Stayloft, total **€600.00** incl. Commission rate **15%** → commission **€90.00** (Stayloft's revenue under net presentation as agent for the accommodation; VAT treatment of the commission handled per jurisdiction — chapters may simplify, but must state the simplification). Adyen processing fee **€1.74** (illustrative). Partner payout **€510.00** instructed after check-out on **Thursday 22 April 2027**, settled to the partner's bank **Monday 26 April 2027**. Adyen books the capture into its settlement batch on **Sunday 14 March 2027** and the batch payout credits Northmere Payments B.V.'s bank on **Tuesday 16 March 2027** (illustrative). (Corrected 2026-10-18: earlier text said settled 14 March and payout settled 24 April, which fall on a weekend.)
