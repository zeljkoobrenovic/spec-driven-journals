# Review: Working Small

**Reviewed:** 2026-08-14 · **Reviewer:** post-review skill (AI-mediated)
**Files reviewed:** spec.md, index.md, checklist.md, comics.md

## Verdict

The strongest-argued of the five Operating Takes records: the teachability
asymmetry is a genuine thesis, and cycles/estimation/weekly-progress all hang
off it cleanly. One real defect blocks polish sign-off: the quotable highlight
says progress should "be **sensible** daily and weekly" where every other
surface (excerpt, spec, Statement, checklist) says **sensed** — a wrong word in
the most-quoted line of the record. Fix that and it is publish-ready.

## Findings by severity

**Counts:** blocker 0 · major 1 · minor 2 · nit 2

### Major
- **[index.md · Status/Principle highlight]** "…expect real progress — even on
  big, hard things — to be **sensible** daily and weekly." Wrong word:
  "sensible" means *reasonable*; the claim (per the spec, the excerpt, and the
  Statement) is that progress can be **sensed**. The highlight is the record's
  most-quoted surface and currently contradicts its own excerpt. *Change to
  "to be sensed daily and weekly" (or "expect to sense real progress…").*

### Minor
- **[checklist.md · Estimate on the Ladder]** The two nested items under "For
  each 1–3-quarter (or larger) bet, name its shape" are rendered as checkboxes
  but are mutually exclusive classifications, not two checks to satisfy.
  *Convert the two to plain `-` note bullets under the one real check.*
- **[index.md · Rationale, "Coarse estimates" ¶]** The paragraph runs seven
  long sentences carrying three distinct ideas (atomic-level sizing, the two
  shapes, the weekly meeting coda); the two-shapes contrast deserves its own
  breath. *A paragraph break before "A different 1–3-quarter bet" would do it.*

### Nits
- **[index.md · after Figures 1–3]** Double blank lines left by the figure
  generator (cosmetic).
- **[index.md · "Every cadence" ¶]** "life — and learning — is only happening
  *now*" — the parenthetical dodges the agreement question ("life and learning
  are"); fine as rhetoric, worth a glance.
