# Spec: Product Vision and Principles

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I use a product vision and product principles as the organization's
shared north star. The post turns the *EMPOWERED* "Product Vision and
Principles" checklist into an operating principle: one shared, customer-centric
vision of the future — 3–10 years out — that supports the company's
objectives, informs product strategy, architecture, and team topology, and is
inspiring enough to recruit with; paired with explicit product principles that
encode what the products stand for and decide the countless small trade-offs
teams face without escalation, including the ethical ones. The vision is
validated for demand, communicated relentlessly (visiontype, prototype,
storyboard, video), owned by the head of product with design and technology
leadership shaping it and the CEO genuinely behind it — and it is not a
feature roadmap.

## Audience

My product leaders and product teams (so they know what the vision is for and
what the principles are allowed to decide); peer executives and engineering
leadership (so topology and architecture conversations start from the vision);
candidates I want to recruit. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states "one shared vision, 3–10
      years out, inspiring enough to recruit with" and "principles decide the
      small stuff so the vision can decide the big stuff" in one paragraph.
- [ ] **The north-star role is explicit** — the vision informs strategy,
      architecture, and team topology; each team sees how it contributes to
      the larger whole.
- [ ] **The horizon survives** — 3–10 years, end state not implementation
      plan, vision is not a roadmap, no per-team visions.
- [ ] **Principles carry weight** — explicit enough to help with trade-offs,
      reasoning explained, priority in conflicts clear, ethics addressed
      explicitly.
- [ ] **Validation is honest** — demand can be validated; the final solution
      cannot; pursuing the vision is a reasoned leap of faith.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — all eleven PDF sections reproduced
      in the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`), numbers preserved.
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — References name Cagan & Jones's *EMPOWERED*
      and note the svpg.com essay origins.
