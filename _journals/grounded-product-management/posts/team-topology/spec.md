# Spec: Team Topology

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I split product work across teams: team topology is an
empowerment decision, not an org-chart by-product. The post turns the
*EMPOWERED* "Team Topology" checklist into an operating principle: start
from strategic context (mission, vision, strategy — not reporting-line
convenience), optimize for ownership, autonomy, and alignment, give every
team something meaningful to own end to end, distinguish platform teams
(leverage, reduced cognitive load) from experience teams (customer/user
outcomes, aligned by the most natural customer dimension), align boundaries
with customers, business outcomes, and architecture, avoid the structural
traps (shipping the org chart, skill silos, design as an internal service),
and keep the topology stable but deliberately evolving — moving
responsibilities between intact teams rather than shuffling people.

## Audience

My product leadership bench and peer executives (so topology decisions are
legible as empowerment moves, not turf allocation); product and engineering
leaders comparing operating models. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states "topology is an
      empowerment decision" and "every team owns something meaningful"
      in one paragraph.
- [ ] **The three optimization targets are explicit** — ownership,
      autonomy, and alignment each appear and each carries weight in the
      body, not just the list.
- [ ] **Platform vs experience teams is argued** — platform teams provide
      leverage and reduce cognitive load; experience teams own end-to-end
      customer outcomes; internal platforms managed like products.
- [ ] **The alignment lenses survive** — customers/business outcomes,
      product vision, and architecture as overlapping lenses, including
      the customer dimensions (persona, segment, journey stage, channel,
      KPI, geography).
- [ ] **Stability with evolution is included** — revisit triggers
      (painful dependencies, teams unable to ship simple things, ownership
      too narrow) and the rule to move responsibilities between intact
      teams, not people between teams.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — all thirteen PDF sections plus
      the quick scorecard reproduced in the Checklist tab
      (`checklist.md`).
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — References name Cagan & Jones's
      *EMPOWERED* and note the svpg.com essay origins.

## Non-goals

- Not a reorg playbook or a specific topology for any company.
- Not [[empowered-product-strategy]] — that record covers what the teams
  work on; this one covers how the teams are drawn.
- Not a restatement of Skelton & Pais's *Team Topologies* — the
  engineering journal's [[four-fundamental-team-topologies]] covers the
  flow-oriented model; this record is Cagan's empowerment-oriented cut,
  cross-linked rather than merged.
- No claim that any particular topology is permanent — the record treats
  topology as reviewed and evolving by design.
