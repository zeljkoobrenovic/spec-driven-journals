# Spec: Product Dysfunctions

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I diagnose and repair product decision-making: I treat recurring
product dysfunctions as diagnosable patterns, not personality problems. The
post turns the *Build What Matters* "Product Dysfunctions" checklist into an
operating principle: run an honest self-assessment against the known symptom
list (with its 0 / 1–2 / 3–4 / 5+ scoring), name the pattern in play —
sales-led, visionary-led / HiPPO, consensus-driven, spreadsheet-led, feature
factory — and fix the operating system rather than the people, by installing
the vision-led chain: target customer segment, key customer outcome, customer
journey vision, strategy with measurable milestones, roadmap where every item
links to a customer outcome. Feedback is filtered (act / investigate / defer /
decline), no is said out loud, and progress is reviewed against outcomes, not
output.

## Audience

Product leaders and my own leadership bench (so roadmap pushback and declined
requests are legible as system moves, not obstruction); founders and
executives who recognize their company in one of the dysfunctions;
engineering leaders reading the product-side counterpart of my
engineering-executive journal. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states "name the dysfunction
      first and fix the operating system, not the people" in one paragraph.
- [ ] **The dysfunctions are named** — sales-led, visionary-led / HiPPO,
      consensus-driven, spreadsheet-led, and the feature factory each appear
      as a recognizable pattern, not a vague vice.
- [ ] **The scoring survives** — the self-assessment thresholds (0 strong,
      1–2 warning signs, 3–4 meaningful dysfunction, 5+ urgent leadership
      problem) are preserved.
- [ ] **The cure is the vision-led chain** — target customer → key outcome →
      journey vision → strategy → roadmap is argued as the systemic fix, with
      the act / investigate / defer / decline feedback filter.
- [ ] **Systems over blame** — the record argues explicitly that swapping
      people while keeping the system reproduces the dysfunction.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — all seven PDF sections reproduced
      in the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`), scoring and decision guide
      preserved.
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — References name Foster & Nerlikar's *Build
      What Matters*.

## Non-goals

- Not a full vision-writing guide — [[customer-journey-vision]] covers the
  journey vision itself; this record only names it as the cure.
- Not the outcomes framework — [[outcomes]] covers how key customer outcomes
  are defined and measured.
- Not a process manual — [[right-processes]] covers the operating processes
  that keep the fix in place.
- No claim that the named dysfunctions are exhaustive, or that founders'
  vision is worthless — the visionary enters through the framework, not
  around it.
