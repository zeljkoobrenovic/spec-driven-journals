# Spec: Product Strategy

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I approach product strategy: as the bridge from vision to work. A
vision without a strategy is a poster; a backlog without a strategy is a job
queue. The post turns the *Build What Matters* "Product Strategy" checklist
into an operating principle: define the destination (Point A → Point B, the
end-state customer journey), work backward from the vision to today, run a
gap analysis between the ideal journey and the current one, sequence the
work under real constraints, evaluate the strategy factors (financials,
competitive advantage, market segments, outcome delivery, technology and
data dependencies, strategic leverage), establish milestones with business
and customer KPIs, review progress and adapt without letting feedback
replace the vision, and run a final strategy check before committing teams.

## Audience

My product leaders and peer executives (so roadmap and phasing decisions are
legible as strategic sequencing, not taste); engineering leaders who want to
see what their strategy has to connect to. First-person declarative.

- [x] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states that strategy is the
      bridge between the vision and the roadmap, built by working backward
      from Point B, in one paragraph.
- [x] **Point A / Point B survive** — the destination framing (current
      experience vs end-state customer journey) is explicit.
- [x] **Working backward is argued** — dependencies, "what must be true
      before this step", and dead-end detection appear as method, not
      slogan.
- [x] **The strategy factors are enumerated** — financial situation,
      competitive advantage (must-haves / performance features /
      delighters), market segments, outcome delivery, technology and data
      dependencies, strategic leverage all appear.
- [x] **Milestones carry KPIs** — each milestone proves something and
      carries both business and customer KPIs.
- [x] **Adaptation is bounded** — feedback improves the vision rather than
      replacing it; the strategy changes on evidence, not on every signal.
- [x] **The checklist survives intact** — all eight PDF sections reproduced
      in the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`), sub-groups preserved.
- [x] **Credit is explicit** — References name Foster & Nerlikar's *Build
      What Matters*.
