![Mila proudly shows a vision poster and a huge backlog screen while Vera eyes the empty wall between them.](assets/images/product-strategy/comic-01-poster-and-backlog.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: a vision on the wall, a backlog in the tool — and nothing in between.*

![A whiteboard of four quarters with arrows pointing in scattered directions, none toward the lighthouse icon.](assets/images/product-strategy/comic-02-plausible-quarters.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: every roadmap is defensible, and none of them add up.*

![Vera draws a bridge labeled strategy connecting a road labeled roadmap to a lighthouse labeled vision.](assets/images/product-strategy/comic-03-strategy-is-the-bridge.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The principle: strategy bridges the vision to the work.*

![A wall map with a path from A to B, arrows drawn backward from B, and a dead-end side path marked with an X.](assets/images/product-strategy/comic-04-work-backward.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *Phasing the vision: work backward from Point B, name the gaps between today's journey and the ideal one — and find the dead ends before paying for them.*

![Mila steps onto the first of four flagged stepping stones leading across water to a lighthouse island.](assets/images/product-strategy/comic-05-phases-stepping-stones.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Milestones are claims, not dates: each phase proves something, with business and customer KPIs.*

![Mila plants a flag on one fork of a three-way path while the other two forks are roped off.](assets/images/product-strategy/comic-06-deliberate-fork.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Trade-offs are forks: one path deliberately taken, the others consciously declined.*

![The wall now shows vision, bridge diagram, and an ordered backlog connected by flowing arrows, Mila beaming.](assets/images/product-strategy/comic-07-roadmap-derives.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The payoff: the roadmap derives from the strategy, and the strategy derives from the vision.*

![Vera and Mila look toward a distant lighthouse, Vera annotating a small sketch of it in her notebook.](assets/images/product-strategy/comic-08-improve-not-replace.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: change the strategy on evidence — feedback improves the vision, it does not replace it.*
