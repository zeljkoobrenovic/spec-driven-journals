**Panel 1:** *The hook: a roadmap where everything is urgent — and everything is reactive.*

![Vera points at a dusty vision card stuck in a 'next quarter' tray while Mila looks embarrassed.](assets/images/balanced-roadmap/comic-02-vision-never-scheduled.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the vision is always 'next quarter' — which means never.*

![Thought bubble of a PM on an ivory tower with a vision scroll while customers and a smoking server are ignored below.](assets/images/balanced-roadmap/comic-03-ivory-tower.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The mirror failure: all vision and no iteration or operation is just as unbalanced.*

![Vera draws three columns labeled innovation, iteration, and operation while Mila sorts roadmap cards into them.](assets/images/balanced-roadmap/comic-04-three-categories.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: classify everything — vision work, customer improvements, and keep-the-lights-on work each get their own column.*

![Vera and Mila adjust sliders on a three-segment allocation bar above still-empty ranked lists.](assets/images/balanced-roadmap/comic-05-set-the-allocation.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Allocate before you rank: the split is the real decision, and items compete only within their own slice.*

![Mila calmly holds up an intake form to a shouting stakeholder with a giant request card while Vera nods.](assets/images/balanced-roadmap/comic-06-defending-the-split.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Defending the split: the loud request gets a process and a fair fight inside its slice — not the whole roadmap.*

![Vera and Mila view a balanced three-lane roadmap leading to milestone flags climbing toward a mountain-peak vision.](assets/images/balanced-roadmap/comic-07-roadmap-advances-journey.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The payoff: a roadmap that serves today's customers and systems while still planting the next milestone flag.*

![Portrait close-up of Vera holding her notebook with a small three-segment allocation bar, Mila smiling beside her.](assets/images/balanced-roadmap/comic-08-balance-is-the-strategy.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: the allocation is the strategy, made visible.*
