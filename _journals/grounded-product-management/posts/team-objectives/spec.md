# Spec: Team Objectives

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I run team objectives the empowered way: I assign teams problems
to solve, not features to ship. The post turns the *EMPOWERED* "Team
Objectives" checklist into an operating principle: leadership provides
strategic context and decides which teams attack which problems; teams
propose the key results; ambition levels (roof shot vs moon shot) are set
explicitly as portfolio risk management; high-integrity commitments are
rare, investigated, tracked separately, and kept; capacity for
keep-the-lights-on work is priced in; progress is actively managed weekly;
and accountability is proportional to ambition — postmortems improve the
system, not just the team. The enemy throughout is OKR theater: objectives
dictated as feature lists, quietly ignored by week three.

## Audience

My product leadership bench and peer executives (so objective-setting reads
as an empowerment mechanism, not paperwork); product managers and
engineering leaders comparing operating models. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states "problems to solve, not
      features to ship" and "key results are proposed by the team" in one
      paragraph.
- [ ] **The two-way flow is explicit** — strategic context and objectives
      flow down; key results and solution ideas flow up.
- [ ] **The numbers survive** — 2 to 4 key results per objective, one
      primary success measure plus guardrails, weekly check-ins, quarterly
      refresh.
- [ ] **Ambition is a dial, not a mood** — roof shot vs moon shot named,
      framed as portfolio risk management, and accountability made
      proportional to the level set.
- [ ] **High-integrity commitments are the exception** — investigated
      before promised, tracked separately, risks raised early, and kept.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — all thirteen PDF sections plus
      the quick quality check reproduced in the Checklist tab
      (`checklist.md`).
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — References name Cagan & Jones's *EMPOWERED*
      and note the svpg.com essay origins.

## Non-goals

- Not an OKR tooling or template guide — no specific software, forms, or
  scoring schemes.
- Not [[outcomes]] — that Build-What-Matters sibling covers what outcomes
  are and why they beat output; this record covers how teams are assigned
  problems and commit to results against them.
- Not [[empowered-product-strategy]] — that record covers choosing which
  problems matter; this one starts where strategy hands a problem to a
  team.
- No claim that OKRs are mandatory — the mechanism matters less than the
  empowerment; OKRs without empowered teams are theater regardless of
  format.
