*The working checklist behind this principle — derived from John Cutler's "20 Unfiltered Operating Takes". The Article tab carries the rationale and anti-patterns.*

## Run the Shape Exercise

- [ ] The team has mapped its shapes of work — expect 5–8, be suspicious of fewer.
- **For each shape, its profile is named:**
  - [ ] Risk profile — what kind of failure is possible, and how reversible.
  - [ ] Experiment-friendliness — does this work tolerate iteration and pivots?
  - [ ] Size — where it sits on the [[working-small]] estimation ladder.
  - [ ] Blast radius — who and what it disrupts internally.
- [ ] Shapes inform coordination and review; they are not a bureaucratic filing scheme.

## Read the Thing

- [ ] Each substantial initiative gets read and written about explicitly — not just filed under a label.
- [ ] The people close to the work get to raise the nuances before categorization locks in.
- [ ] Differences that really matter are surfaced in writing, not flattened for the deck.
- [ ] Slide-deck summaries are produced when needed — and treated as views, not reality.
- [ ] "We need to keep it simple" is challenged when it would erase a load-bearing distinction.

## Tier Launches on Two Axes

- [ ] External launch tier (marketing volume) and internal coordination tier (blast radius) are assessed separately.
- [ ] Internally disruptive but externally silent work gets full coordination attention.
- [ ] Deliberately quiet or secret launches are planned for confidentiality, not treated as low-effort.
- [ ] Marketing's tiering scheme is used for marketing decisions only.

## Review Team Durability

- [ ] Every new team carries an explicit durability assumption: project-scoped or durable, and why.
- [ ] Project-scoped teams have a named sunset condition from day one.
- [ ] "We'll always need that" claims are tested against evidence, not accepted as irrefutable.
- [ ] Hierarchy (directors, GMs) is added only after the product underneath has proven real.
- [ ] Team durability is revisited on a schedule — while reassignment is still cheap.
- [ ] Teams past their sunset condition are unwound by reassignment now, not deferred to a future restructuring.
