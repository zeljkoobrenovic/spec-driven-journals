## Verdict

A coherent pairing — the spec's bet that "one template" and "eternal team" are
the same flattening instinct pays off, and the [[team-topology]] reconciliation

record's best coinage, "silent earthquake," lives only in a figure caption and

remember is never actually introduced by the article.

## Findings by severity

**Counts:** blocker 0 · major 0 · minor 3 · nit 1

### Minor
- **[index.md · "One scheme" ¶ vs Figure 1 / comics Panel 3]** "Silent
  earthquake" — the record's most memorable label — appears only in the
  Figure 1 caption and the comic; the Rationale describes the phenomenon in a
  clause chain but never names it. *One naming sentence in the launch-tier
  paragraph would let the visuals inherit the term instead of inventing it.*
- **[index.md · "One scheme cannot carry two meanings" ¶]** The paragraph
  carries the example, the distinction, the confusion claim, and the
  "keep it simple" voice in one unbroken run — the load-bearing distinction
  (blast radius vs launch demands) is easy to lose mid-sentence. *A break
  before "That one small distinction" would foreground it.*
- **[checklist.md · Review Team Durability]** "Unwinding a team is done by
  reassigning people early, not by layoff physics later" is an outcome
  statement, not an action a reader can check this quarter. *Rephrase as the
  check it implies, e.g. "Teams past their sunset condition are unwound by
  reassignment now, not deferred."*

### Nits
- **[index.md · after Figures 1–3]** Double blank lines left by the figure
  generator (cosmetic).
