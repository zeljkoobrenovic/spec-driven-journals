# Review: Routines

**Reviewed:** 2026-08-14 · **Reviewer:** post-review skill (AI-mediated)
**Files reviewed:** spec.md, index.md, checklist.md, comics.md

## Verdict

A vivid, well-paced record — the week-four material is the emotional core and
lands in all three modalities. Publish-ready. The one alignment defect worth
fixing: the article and the checklist give different thresholds for the
silent-death signal (four weeks running vs "more than twice running") — pick
one number and propagate it.

## Findings by severity

**Counts:** blocker 0 · major 0 · minor 3 · nit 1

### Minor
- **[checklist.md · Watch for the Week-Four Collapse vs index.md · Anti-Patterns]**
  Threshold mismatch: the article's "silent cancellation" anti-pattern says the
  ritual "'didn't make sense this week' **four weeks running**"; the checklist
  says watch for "**more than twice** running." Same signal, two numbers.
  *Align both (the checklist's earlier trigger is arguably the better
  operational value — watch at two, call it dead at four — but then say so).*
- **[comics.md · Panel 8]** The closer carries two beats at once — the AI
  turbocharger *and* boring product — so the record's actual closing idea
  ("you can hear when it is working") exists only as half a caption clause.
  The other seven panels are one-beat. *If ever regenerated, an eight-panel
  arc ending on listening-to-the-team would match the article's close better.*
- **[index.md · Rationale, "Week four" ¶]** The longest paragraph in the five
  new records (~9 sentences, four distinct movements: the collapse, the fear
  diagnosis, the avoidance workstreams, the push-through). It works read
  aloud, but a break before "This is not laziness" would halve the load.

### Nits
- **[index.md · after Figures 1–3]** Double blank lines left by the figure
  generator (cosmetic).
