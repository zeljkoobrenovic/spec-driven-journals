*The working checklist behind this principle — derived from John Cutler's "20 Unfiltered Operating Takes". The Article tab carries the rationale and anti-patterns.*

## Bootstrap a New Team (V1)

- [ ] A V1 way of working exists before real work starts — written in one session or delegated to one person.
- [ ] The route is legitimate: collective working agreements, or an explicit "hammer out V1" assignment.
- [ ] V1 is written by and for the people doing the work — not deck-ified for observers.
- [ ] V1 covers just enough to start: cadence, decision habits, how work is picked up and reviewed.
- [ ] Theorizing about the perfect operating model is explicitly deferred.
- [ ] The team knows V1 is theirs to adapt as they go.

## Keep the Heartbeat

- [ ] A regular ritual gets the team in a room (virtual or otherwise) to connect on the here and now.
- [ ] The agenda can vary; the connection does not lapse.
- [ ] Most of the energy goes to what was done and learned, not only what is planned.
- [ ] Risks and elephants get named in the room, not in back channels.
- [ ] Hard calls are made when due — not postponed "one more week".

## Watch for the Week-Four Collapse

- [ ] The ritual is watched for zombie signs: attendance without engagement, status without conversation.
- [ ] The ritual is watched for silent death: "didn't make sense this week" twice running is the warning; four running is the collapse.
- [ ] Leaders spend more time connecting on current work than planning far-off things in other rooms.
- [ ] Accumulated elephants trigger an explicit reset of the routine, not its abandonment.
- [ ] "Going back to the drawing board for alignment" is challenged when it is avoidance of doing.

## Maintain Anchor Artifacts

- [ ] A small set of anchor artifacts exists — documents the team writes and returns to.
- [ ] Anchor artifacts are revisited on a rhythm: do they still make sense?
- [ ] New initiatives reuse the anchors instead of inventing a new doc format.
- [ ] Anchors evolve deliberately; format changes are decisions, not defaults.

## Point AI at the Habits

- [ ] AI leverage starts from things the team already knew it should do (research library, analytics instrumentation, documentation).
- [ ] New AI-enabled practices are judged by people who know what good looks like.
- [ ] Newly cheap capabilities (e.g. synthetic personas) get explicit "for what, within what boundaries?" scrutiny.
- [ ] Practice changes are expected and revisited — expertise, not novelty, sets the bar.

## Listen for the Motion

- [ ] You can hear the team's motion: shipping, learning, small connected to big.
- [ ] Progress is discussed daily and weekly, undramatically.
- [ ] When the team sounds like a movie that never finds its footing, that instinct is acted on.
