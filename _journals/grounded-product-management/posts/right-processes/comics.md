![Vera points at a hamster wheel built from calendar icons spinning in place as Mila watches.](assets/images/right-processes/comic-02-process-serving-itself.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: process running for its own sake — motion without progress.*

![Vera draws a gear connected by an arrow to a customer figure and a rising outcome chart while Mila takes notes.](assets/images/right-processes/comic-03-processes-serve-outcomes.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The principle: processes exist to serve outcomes — anything else comes off the calendar.*

![Mila interviews a customer while sketching a discovery loop, as Vera holds a card reading four hours per week.](assets/images/right-processes/comic-04-discovery-loop.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The loop: continuous discovery and validation — research before commitment, every single week.*

![A funnel filters a cloud of requests into a short ranked list held by Mila while Vera waves off a giant megaphone.](assets/images/right-processes/comic-05-prioritization-from-strategy.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Prioritization: an idea bank and one consistent framework — the roadmap is not a volume contest.*

![Vera and Mila stand before three interlocking gears labeled sprint, quarterly, and yearly vision.](assets/images/right-processes/comic-06-cadence-sized-to-company.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Cadence: reassessment sized to the horizon — sprints spin fast, the vision turns once a year.*

![Mila shows Vera a dashboard where a metric line rises past a launch flag while engineers look on.](assets/images/right-processes/comic-07-evidence-after-launch.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The learning loop closes: not 'we shipped it' but 'it moved the outcome' — with evidence.*

![Vera and Mila stand before a mostly clear wall calendar with an open slot marked by a customer icon.](assets/images/right-processes/comic-08-lean-calendar.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: fewer ceremonies, every one earning its place — and room for the customer again.*
