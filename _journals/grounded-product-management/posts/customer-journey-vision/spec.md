# Spec: Customer Journey Vision

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I anchor product direction: in a **customer journey vision** — a
concrete, written story of how a specific customer's life works roughly three
years from now, not a slogan about our product. The post turns the *Build What
Matters* "Customer Journey Vision" checklist into an operating principle,
covering the foundation, the full trigger-to-retention journey, competitive
differentiation and the Kano model, communication artifacts, supporting
components, and final validation. The Success criteria below carry the
specifics.

## Audience

My product leaders and product managers (so roadmap conversations start from
the customer's future journey, not a feature list); peer executives and
engineering leaders who need to see why I ask "how does the customer's life
change?" before approving direction. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states that the vision describes
      the customer's future journey, not our features, in one paragraph.
- [ ] **The journey stages are explicit** — trigger, discovery, evaluation,
      trial, engagement, retention each appear with what they must answer.
- [ ] **The numbers survive** — the 10x customer outcome and the roughly
      three-year horizon are stated, with the caveat that market or business
      model can shorten or lengthen it.
- [ ] **Artifacts are argued** — comic strips, vision mock-ups, and customer
      diary entries appear as communication vehicles, with mock-ups framed as
      concept direction, not engineering requirements.
- [ ] **Kano is included** — must-haves, performance features, delighters,
      and the decay of delighters into expectations.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — all nine PDF sections reproduced in
      the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`), numbers preserved.
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — References name Foster & Nerlikar's *Build
      What Matters*.

## Non-goals

- Not a vision *statement* workshop — this record is against slogan visions,
  not a template for writing one.
- Not [[product-strategy]] — that record covers how strategy sequences the
  path toward the vision; this one covers the destination itself.
- Not a persona or UX-research methodology guide — personas appear only as
  optional supporting components.
- No claim that three years is a law — it is the default horizon and shifts
  with market and business model.
