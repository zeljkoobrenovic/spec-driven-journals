# Spec: Coaching

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I approach coaching as a product leader: developing people is the
first responsibility of every product leader, including me. The post turns
the *EMPOWERED* coaching checklist into an operating principle: the coaching
mindset (developing people as job #1, judging my success by their growth,
empowerment over task control), an honest skills assessment (expectations vs.
current capability by skill area, top 3 gaps first), coaching across product
knowledge, process, and people skills, a written coaching plan reviewed
weekly, the weekly 1:1 as protected development time, written narratives as
the stronger coaching tool, teaching the six strategic-context elements,
coaching ownership and outcome orientation, and the weekly manager
self-audit that keeps me honest about all of it.

## Audience

Product leaders in my organization (so they know coaching is the standard I
hold them to, not an optional extra); product managers who report to them
(so they know what to expect from an assessment, a coaching plan, and a
1:1); peer executives comparing operating models. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states "coaching is the first
      job of every product leader" and "the 1:1 develops the person rather
      than collecting status" in one paragraph.
- [ ] **The loop is explicit** — honest assessment → written coaching plan →
      weekly 1:1 → visible growth, with periodic reassessment.
- [ ] **The numbers survive** — top 3 gaps first, 1:1s at least weekly, the
      six strategic-context elements, the four product risks plus the fifth
      question ("should we build it?"), several meaningful customer
      conversations each week.
- [ ] **Ownership is the endgame** — coaching aims at ownership of outcomes,
      not dependency on the coach.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — all 61 numbered PDF sections
      reproduced in the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`), numbers preserved.
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — References name Cagan and Jones's
      *EMPOWERED* and note the authors' svpg.com essay origins.

## Non-goals

- Not [[staffing]] — that record covers hiring and onboarding; this one
  covers developing people once they are in the seat.
- Not [[right-team]] — that record covers what an empowered product team is;
  this one covers growing the people inside it.
- Not a performance-management or compensation process — coaching feeds
  those, but this record does not define them.
- No claim that coaching replaces standards — the bar itself is
  [[calibrating-your-standards]]; coaching is how people reach it.
