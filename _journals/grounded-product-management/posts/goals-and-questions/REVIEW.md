## Verdict

The record successfully does what its spec promised — the practice-side
companion to [[outcomes]] rather than a restatement of it — and the
"price list" framing keeps the anti-theory stance from becoming a rant.
Publish-ready. Most useful polish: the record covers five loosely-coupled
takes, and the seams show mildly in two places — the "answers expire" epigram
that the same paragraph then contradicts, and a checklist section whose title
covers only half its items.

## Findings by severity

**Counts:** blocker 0 · major 0 · minor 3 · nit 1

### Minor
- **[index.md · "Questions compound; answers expire" ¶]** The epigram says
  answers *expire*, but the paragraph's actual claim is that answers are the
  fuel — "answers are what sharpen the next question." The two halves of the
  bolded lead pull in opposite directions. *Either "Questions compound;
  answers feed them" or drop the second clause.*
- **[checklist.md · Hold the Ambition Bar]** The section title covers the
  first two items (imperfect measurement), but the last two — multi-horizon
  thinking, power ICs — are a different topic sharing the section because
  they had nowhere else to go. *Either retitle ("Hold the Bar, Spread the
  Horizons") or split a two-item section out.*
- **[index.md · "Horizons belong to everyone" ¶]** "There should be power ICs
  who love showing people how to do the thing" arrives as the record's last
  Rationale sentence with no development — the only place in the five records
  where a closing sentence introduces a new noun ("power ICs") rather than
  landing an argued one. *Half a sentence of connection (they are how
  horizons reach the front line) would finish it.*

### Nits
- **[index.md · after Figures 1–3]** Double blank lines left by the figure
  generator (cosmetic).
