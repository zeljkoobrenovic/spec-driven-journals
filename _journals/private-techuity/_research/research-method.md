# Research Method and Evidence Limits

Research and draft date: September 12, 2026. This was a multi-pass, question-led investigation, not a systematic review with a preregistered exhaustive search strategy. No claim is made to have reviewed every available source.

## Work performed

1. **Repository and input pass.** Read the applicable repository instructions and relevant research, article, summary, comic, and specification skills. Inspect the actual inputs and existing journal structure before developing the book architecture. The current `journal` workspace was empty; the user explicitly selected `spec-driven-journals` alongside the located inputs as the destination.
2. **Foundations pass.** Consult SEC and ILPA material, original academic overviews and practitioner-survey evidence. Establish separate fund, firm, company, cash, return, and governance concepts. Use worked fictional examples where transparent arithmetic is more useful than an unsourced market average.
3. **Operating and role pass.** Connect the private role brief and Productscapes hypotheses to official technology frameworks and original research: DORA, SPACE, FinOps, NIST, and AI productivity experiments. Avoid treating a framework as demonstrated financial impact.
4. **Case pass.** Examine company filings, transaction announcements, contemporaneous financial disclosures, a sponsor realization account, and credible reporting for contrasting cases. Follow initial situation, ownership, financing, interventions, outcomes, stakeholder scope, and alternative explanations separately.
5. **Contradiction and temporal pass.** Seek heterogeneous buyout outcomes, financial-crisis support evidence, healthcare harms and differing quality findings, post-sale financial evidence, and later AI research. Correct the buyout-employment estimates to the April 2024 paper revision and qualify the meaning of the February 2026 AI update.
6. **Reconciliation and editorial pass.** Check major financial definitions and example arithmetic; distinguish the contemporaneous Visma report from a later website presentation; retain access failures and causal gaps. Recheck selected primary-source passages and dates during final editing. Validate links, source IDs, modality structure, and input preservation separately from historical fact assessment.

Searches used public concepts and publicly documented cases. Private role text, private personal information, and sensitive input details were not supplied to external searches. No interviews were conducted, no messages were sent to third parties, and no proprietary company datasets were obtained.

## What a citation means

The cited source was actually consulted to the scope recorded in `sources.json`. It may support a definition, a reported statement, a financial observation, or a premise for the manuscript's analysis. It does not automatically support every possible causal interpretation. Sources are cited close to the relevant claims; the public bibliography supplies authors or organizations, titles, dates, links, and important limits.

A filing contains different forms of evidence. Financial tables, management descriptions, forward-looking ambitions, and risk disclosures should not be treated as equally verified outcomes. An investor or company announcement is an interested primary account. An empirical estimate is bounded by sample, identification, and measurement. A framework is a proposed organization of work, not an impact study.

## Generalization and selection

The cases lean toward mature and visible organizations because disclosure is better. US large transactions and healthcare empirical studies do not stand in for European lower mid-market software engagements. The role brief points to that latter setting, but permitted private operating evidence has not been supplied. Growth equity, pure venture, smaller software buyouts, and standalone carve-outs need additional documented cases.

Positive outcomes receive more retrospective investor coverage than routine or disappointing interventions. Search for discontinued initiatives and former participants, subject to permissions, rather than assembling only successful support stories. Comparison companies are difficult to select because both targets and interventions are chosen rather than assigned randomly.

## Temporal discipline

“Accessed September 12, 2026” is not a claim that every cited observation describes September 2026 conditions. Dates are explicit in the chapters and source register. Preserve report versions and distinguish publication date from event date and observation period. Financial ratios, software metrics, AI evidence, and ownership structures may change.

Do not update one headline in isolation. A changed source can require revising the chapter, TL;DR, comic caption, glossary, claim ledger, and any related example. A current data point does not automatically replace a deliberately historical case window.
