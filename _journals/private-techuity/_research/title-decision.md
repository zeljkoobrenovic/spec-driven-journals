# Accepted Working Title

On September 12, 2026, the author accepted **Private Techuity: Product and Engineering Leadership in Private Equity**. Use it consistently. It remains a working title for the living manuscript; a further title decision is not pending.

The alternatives below are retained as editorial history.

## Title Alternatives

| Provisional title | What it promises | Editorial judgment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Private Techuity: Product and Engineering Leadership in Private Equity** | Economic understanding and practical leadership through ownership | **Recommended.** Retains the distinctive name and describes the book's full scope beyond an introduction. |
| Private Techuity: Demystifying Private Equity for Product and Engineering Leaders | An accessible entry to an unfamiliar system | The original working title remains strong for discovery; “demystifying” understates the operating and research depth. |
| Building Value Under Private Equity: A Guide for Product and Engineering Leaders | A clear practical emphasis | Direct, but less distinctive and liable to suggest that value creation is assured. |
| Technology Under Private Equity: Ownership, Operations, and Durable Value | A broad analytical treatment | Clear and balanced, though less personal and memorable than Private Techuity. |

The recommended title remains provisional. Revisit it after the Principal and Productscapes chapters have been tested against experience and independent cases.
