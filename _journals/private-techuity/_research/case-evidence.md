## Highest-value additions to each case

**Hilton:** obtain a complete acquisition and refinancing chronology, the investor capital/distribution series on a defined basis, and longitudinal segment/customer/franchisee/employee evidence. Inspect OnQ investment and adoption timing before claiming its incremental return. The public filing is already enough to show a mechanism, not enough to identify a technology treatment effect.

**Skype:** reconstruct consortium ownership changes and cash flows, debt, further funding, and IP settlement economics. Compare contemporaneous product execution with plausible market and strategic-buyer explanations. Keep the 2025 retirement as later history; do not imply an unsupported causal chain spanning ownership regimes.

**Visma:** the 2024 and 2025 annual reports were obtained; the EUR 892.646m plus EUR 11.665m acquisition-cost bridge to EUR 904.311m adjusted EBITDA is resolved. Trace manager versus vehicle ownership and realized flows. Select one or two acquired software businesses with independently documented customer and operating histories, including a disappointing integration or retired product where evidence supports it.

**Toys R Us:** access the complete fiscal 2016 10-K; reconstruct original and subsequent debt, refinancing, distributions and fees using actual financing/court records. The September 2017 declaration now supplies selected debt, technology-plan and vendor-term evidence; Hasbro’s 2018 report supplies a separate supplier observation. Extend these into a complete dated transition and supplier-confidence timeline. Distinguish US entities and liquidation from international operations and brand reuse. Seek primary employee and supplier records and credible counteraccounts.

## Cases to select next, not references already reviewed

Prioritize one European lower mid-market software buyout with attributable operating evidence, one growth-equity engagement with sustained reinvestment, and one carve-out with disclosed transition-service and standalone-cost outcomes. A smaller documented case can be more useful than another famous exit. No particular candidate is asserted as a successful or failed case until its evidence is reviewed.

Healthcare papers in source folder 24-durable-value are research findings, not complete case histories of each hospital or nursing home. Do not inflate them into transaction-level explanations. If a dedicated healthcare narrative is added, it needs its own ownership, financing, operating, patient, and post-exit evidence.
