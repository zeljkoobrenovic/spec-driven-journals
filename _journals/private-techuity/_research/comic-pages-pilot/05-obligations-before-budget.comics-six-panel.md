**Comic.** A positive earnings figure does not establish the cash available for a new initiative. The panels follow one earnings figure through investment, tax, financing payments and the increase in unpaid customer invoices during the period, to the cash on hand, separate the payments that are obligations from the ones that are choices, and end with three priced options and a decision.

Morgan is an investor’s adviser in the fund scenarios; Alex leads technology, Priya leads product, Sam leads finance and Ines is the chief executive. All are fictional. Comparative scenarios use separate assumptions. Historical cases are discussed through documents; the scenes do not reenact real events. Terms used below: EBITDA is a profit measure that leaves out interest, taxes and the charges that spread asset costs over time; working capital is money tied up in unpaid customer invoices and inventory, goods held for sale or use, less bills owed to suppliers; net burn is the cash spent each month beyond what is collected.
