**Comic.** When one company buys another, or a business is separated from the larger business that owns it, the plan behind the purchase can assume savings or independence before anyone has checked that the work is possible in the time allowed. These panels follow what happens afterwards, when those assumptions become work somebody has to do: choosing how much to combine, deciding which promised work waits, and replacing a former parent’s services before they stop.

Two words recur. **Integration** is the work of making selected activities of two businesses operate together. A **carve-out** is the separation of a business from its **parent**, the larger business that owns it.

The people are all fictional. Morgan advises an investor; Alex leads technology, Priya leads product, and Sam leads finance at the company being bought or separated. The scenarios are invented and use their own figures. Real historical cases are discussed in the article through documents; these scenes do not reenact real events.
