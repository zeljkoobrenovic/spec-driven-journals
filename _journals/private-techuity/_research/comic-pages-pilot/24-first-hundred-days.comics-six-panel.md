**Comic.** Larkspur, a fictional company, sells scheduling software. Every new customer needs **onboarding**: Larkspur’s staff set the software up with that customer’s settings and data. An investor has just funded Larkspur’s growth. Its **investment thesis** is its reason for expecting the investment to succeed. At first the thesis was that Larkspur could onboard twice as many customers without more onboarding staff. Before investing, the investor ran **due diligence**, an investigation of the company, and cut that expectation to 50% more customers in the first year. The panels follow the first hundred days, in which one finding from that investigation becomes a funded plan and the day-100 review changes the plan. Every figure is fictional.

Ines is the chief executive; Priya leads product; Alex leads technology; Sam leads finance; Morgan is the investor’s technology adviser. All are fictional. The **board**, the directors who approve major spending, decides the plan. An **engineer-week** is one engineer’s working time for one week. Day numbers count from day 0, the board meeting that adopts the plan.

**Panel 6:** Day 100, the board review.

- **Restore:** the test failed at day 45. The board released €20,000 and two engineer-weeks from the reserve to fix it. The day-85 retest passed: dispatch, customers’ daily assignment of jobs, resumed within four hours, with under fifteen minutes of updates lost.
- **What the retest covers:** the loss of Larkspur’s own systems inside a working cloud region, one geographic group of the cloud provider’s rented computing facilities. An outage of the whole region was not tested.
- **Second engineer:** passed at day 60.
- **Pilot:** effort fell from 80 to 62 staff hours per customer, not the 50 an earlier planning model assumed for illustration. Customers waited just as long. At 62 hours the same staff can handle about 1.3 times as many customers, short of the 50% more now expected.
- **Decision:** the board funds a data-quality step from the reserve: checking and correcting each customer’s records before setup, for €40,000 and four engineer-weeks. Commitments reach €340,000 and all 24 engineer-weeks. The expansion decision is proposed for about day 190, and postponed if the next group of customers has not finished.
