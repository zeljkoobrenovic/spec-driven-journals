## What changed

The book now follows the product and engineering leader’s decisions under investors. The opening distinguishes owners, rights, financing and transaction context. Part I adds comparative ownership maps, a minority investment and dilution calculation, funding-round valuation and runway alongside the existing buyout treatment. Governance and incentives address several investors, conditional commitments, employee options and corporate rewards. Operating chapters connect investor expectations to actual product, systems, costs, risk, AI and team choices. Support chapters distinguish what different providers can supply and the dependence it can create.

Part V now covers funding rounds, changed ownership, delayed money, refinancing, corporate integration and continued ownership, alongside possible sales. Historical cases retain their original tables and source citations and distinguish transferable questions from evidence about other investor types. Productscapes remains an optional fund-side hypothesis with explicit adaptation needed elsewhere.

All 42 article specifications were changed before their articles. All 31 summaries and six-panel storyboards now include the revised ownership consequences. The 29 main chapters, two appendix essays, six parts and existing permalinks remain in place. Current title, thesis and next-session records supersede earlier positioning while retaining dated history. S58–S61 add consulted institutional guidance; earlier bibliography entries S54–S57 have been reconciled into the private registry without implying a fresh full-document review.
