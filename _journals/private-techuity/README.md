# Owned — Manuscript Workspace

**OWNED: Product & Engineering Leadership Under Investors**

A practical guide to making product and engineering decisions when investors shape the money, authority and expectations around your company.

Start with [the purpose and reading guide](posts/introduction/index.md) or [the book index](index.md). The book follows company leaders working with venture, growth, buyout and corporate investors. The actual ownership rights, financing, expectations and available support determine the advice. The historical evidence remains concentrated on private equity.

The manuscript contains **30 main chapters in six parts**. All 30 have an article and a 300–500-word summary; 29 have a six-panel illustrated comic (the handover chapter added in the September 2026 editorial revision still needs its comic, header logo and icon generated). The edition includes 39 header logos and 39 navigation icons, alongside 174 comic panels and a dedicated Owned journal logo. Six part introductions and five guide/reference pages bring the published total to **41 pages**, each with a companion specification.

[PLAN.md](PLAN.md) records the substantive Owned rewrite and its chapter contracts. [STRUCTURE.md](STRUCTURE.md) records the resulting positioning and reading structure. The September 2026 editorial review lives in `posts/REVIEW.md` and the per-post `REVIEW.md` files (kept unchanged as the baseline); `posts/REVISION_LOG.md` records how each recommendation was handled. Source directory names remain stable; public page names use unprefixed permalinks. Chapter numbers refer to reading positions. Use the book’s part and chapter order for navigation; omit article tags from front matter.

Use [the public bibliography](posts/bibliography/index.md) for evidence. `_research/` preserves private provenance, dated decisions and validation notes; earlier counts and titles describe their dated revisions. `_input/` remains private and is excluded from the site.
