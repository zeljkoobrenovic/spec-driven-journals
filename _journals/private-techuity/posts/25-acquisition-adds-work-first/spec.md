# Spec: An Acquisition Adds Work Before It Adds Value

## Intent

Treat combining businesses and separating one as two processes under one principle: a new boundary and a continuing customer promise. Show the transaction plan assuming savings or independence before the operating teams have validated the work, complete one capacity decision for an add-on acquisition and one dated carve-out bridge with an independence test, and distinguish a financial sponsor’s consolidation, a strategic acquisition and a separation funded by any suitable owner.

## Audience

Product and engineering leaders inside companies working under investors, including leaders who inherit an ownership arrangement. Assume no specialist finance background; explain necessary terms before use. Investor-side readers are secondary.

- Make the company leader’s decision, authority and funding assumptions explicit. Distinguish the stated ownership arrangement from a claim about every investor.
- Keep historical findings within their source scope and label new comparative scenarios as fictional.
- Introduce necessary terms in ordinary language before using them in explanations, examples, tables, or diagrams; keep opening key points readable without prior study.

- Connect staffing synergies priced into a transaction to the transition cost, notice timing and knowledge-transfer checks in [[anatomy-of-a-layoff]] and [[fix-decisions-before-hiring]] in one sentence each, without developing a reduction case here.
- Carry the group-versus-local standardization boundary point (stated problem, cost of compliance, exceptions process, named funder of the transition) in the integration-depth section; use responsibility language rather than “owns”; link to the authority chapter instead of re-establishing minority versus controlling rights.
- Distinguish sourced observations, the manuscript's analysis, and explicitly fictional examples.
- Include a meaningful trade-off or counterargument and identify the evidence that would change the judgment.
- Link related chapters and cite substantive external factual claims close to the text.
- Keep the TL;DR and the comic page script consistent with the full article.
- Cost the carve-out fallback completely and release it by one rule: state reconnection pricing and a corrective-work allowance separately from the extension fee, give the notice deadline for exercising the extension, give the latest feasible replacement and test dates, and say which decision is required when those dates cannot be met.
- State the extension as one coherent set of contract terms: when the reservation becomes a binding purchase, whether the price covers all bundles and standby, whether individual months can be declined, and what each slipped go-live therefore costs. Every scenario in the chapter must settle against those terms.
- Distinguish spare contractual months from a complete repair-and-retest cycle, and label each test month a first test or a retest according to the scenario.
- Treat an outside supplier as a conditional route that requires a provider, rights, funded migration and a dated handover secured in advance, with its own decision deadline; state the interruption the plan cannot prevent when neither route is in place.
- Separate reserving the fallback from spending it: require the written extension reservation by its notice deadline regardless of the forecast, since reservation is free if unused, and state separately who authorizes reconnection and repair money when a later failure occurs, with the activation and cancellation conditions. Make any dependence on the former parent beyond the agreement explicitly conditional on a new agreement the parent may refuse, and name what the board does if it does.
- Distinguish the preferred latest go-live date that preserves a recovery buffer from the absolute contractual limit, and show the go-live-to-test mapping month by month rather than asserting a single cutoff.
- Name the funded team for the launch work that follows an integration milestone, and reconcile support and leave allocations with the quarterly project allocations so no person is double-booked. Scope the sixteen-engineer-week overrun trigger to the specialists' initial April–June work, distinct from the two planned November release-support weeks, in every format.
- Describe deeper integration designs — a combined data store, a joined product, replacement of every former-parent service — as possible implementations chosen for a named benefit, never as requirements of a unified customer experience, cross-selling or separation.
- Define organic growth as growth in sales from the same businesses over comparable periods, excluding the increase that arrives merely through acquisitions, in every format including the comic.
- Keep the TL;DR inside its 300–500-word target: carry the central distinction, one capacity example, the recurring-versus-one-time cost bridge and the requirement to reserve a funded fallback before its deadline, leaving the month-by-month reconciliation to the article and preserving conditions rather than flattening them into promises.
- Keep the comic cast visually consistent between strips and pages; a character's face, hair and clothing should not change between appearances.
- Describe a lower transition-services total as parent charges avoided against a full-term comparison, never as money that funds the parallel runs; keep parent charges, replacement running costs and one-time work as separate lines.
- Anchor the capacity example to the closing date and a named planning period, show which work leaves that period and when the delayed diligence fits, and use “engineer-weeks” for effort and dates for milestones.
- Introduce each named decision-maker with their role at first appearance, and explain the purchase rationale, the expected combined benefit and the existing delivery plan in ordinary language before the terms for them are used.
- Present each Larkspur decision as a short outcome paragraph followed by labelled lines for alternatives, funding, scarce capacity, authority and the evidence that would change it; keep the scenario setup separate from its arithmetic and calendar.
- Keep figure labels within the vocabulary the argument needs, and carry the figure’s meaningful labels and relationships in the adjacent text so the explanation survives without the image.
