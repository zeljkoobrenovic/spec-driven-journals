**Comic.** Larkspur is a fictional company that rents its computing from a cloud supplier. Its directors approved a plan: keep cloud spending flat while customer transactions grow. Last quarter the bill rose 20% instead, and Morgan, the technology adviser working for Larkspur’s investor, has asked why.

Two questions hide inside that one. Did each completed customer transaction get cheaper? And was the approved plan met? Seven pages keep them apart, put every figure on one basis, test a commitment against a stated demand range, and end with the two-part answer Alex gives.

The cast, all fictional: Morgan advises the investor. Alex is the technology leader. Sam is the finance leader. Ines is the chief executive. Priya leads product. Every amount and date is invented for the example; examples that change the assumptions say so where they start.
