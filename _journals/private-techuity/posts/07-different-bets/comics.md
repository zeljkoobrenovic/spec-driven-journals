**Comic.** When a fund (a pool of investors’ money run by a fund manager) buys into a company, the company’s leaders are often given shares, and the fund manager has its own pay: a share of the fund’s profits called carried interest, or carry. Eight pages first read what a share percentage can actually pay. They then follow one fictional proposal, closing a second support office, through four bets: the executives’ shares, the fund manager’s profit share, the employees’ jobs and the customers’ contract renewals.

Morgan advises the investor; Alex leads technology, Priya leads product, Sam leads finance and Ines is the chief executive of Larkspur, a software company. All are fictional, and so is every figure; the examples use separate assumptions and say so where they start.

**Page 1: A percentage is not a payment.** Every figure in this comic is fictional. Shares are units of ownership in a company, also called equity; management equity is the part held by, or promised to, the company’s leaders. A threshold is the amount that must be exceeded before a plan pays anything. The rights are usually earned over time, which is called vesting; new shares issued later shrink the percentage, which is called dilution; and the plan says what happens if the holder leaves. Ask for worked outcomes for a weak sale, a good sale, new borrowing, a purchase paid for with new shares, and your own departure. Alex leads technology at Larkspur and Sam leads its finances.

**Page 3: Two payout systems, not one.** A fund is a pool of investors’ money run by a fund manager, a firm that chooses which companies to buy into and when to sell. When a company is sold, the company’s own rules divide the sale money among its kinds of shares, some of which are paid first; ordinary shares, the basic kind that leaders usually hold, come after. Separately, the fund’s agreement divides the fund’s profits between the fund’s investors and its manager, whose share is called carried interest, or carry. Many agreements work carry out on the whole fund, so a successful company can earn the manager no carry. Morgan is a technology adviser at the investment firm.
