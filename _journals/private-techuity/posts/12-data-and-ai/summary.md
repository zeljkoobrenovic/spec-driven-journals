Treat AI as an investment hypothesis with several possible mechanisms: a better customer product, a more effective internal workflow, or a response to competitive disruption. Each requires different evidence. Adoption of an AI tool does not establish any of them.

For a customer product, investigate a specific need, willingness to use or pay, quality requirements, and the full cost of delivery. For internal work, measure completed useful outcomes including review and correction. For strategic exposure, identify which customer tasks or sources of differentiation may change. Avoid adding these categories into one unsupported benefit total.

**Developer productivity evidence is context-dependent and changing.** A 2023 controlled GitHub Copilot study found faster completion of a bounded programming task. METR's early-2025 experiment found experienced developers working on familiar open-source repositories took longer with the studied tools. [S19: Peng et al., Copilot experiment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06590) [S20: METR early-2025 experiment](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/) These findings concern different settings and do not justify a universal staffing assumption.

METR's February 24, 2026 update explains why changing participation and task selection made a later estimate difficult to interpret. [S21: METR 2026 update](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/) This is a reason to update local evidence, not to choose whichever historical result supports a preferred budget. The chapter's evidence is dated; it is not a verdict on every tool available today.

A local experiment should define the workflow, comparison, quality threshold, exposure, and decision in advance. Include learning time, review, integration, errors, monitoring, and vendor costs. Report uncertainty and the tasks excluded from the experiment. A capacity gain becomes a cash saving only through an additional operating decision and a feasible change in spending.

Data rights, security, evaluation, and accountability belong in the investment case. NIST's generative AI profile provides a useful risk structure. [S18: NIST generative AI profile](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf) Capability to use a model is different from permission to send it particular data or confidence in its output.

The Technology Principal's useful contribution is to make experiments comparable enough to learn across companies while preserving local context. Scale evidence of useful performance; retire or revise offerings that do not pass their own outcome tests.
