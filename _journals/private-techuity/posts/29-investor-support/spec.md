# Spec: Find the Help That Changes What Your Team Can Do

## Intent

Help a company leader map available investor people, peer companies and connections to a specific capability gap. Cover starting a capability, accelerating delivery, hiring and development without assuming support is available or effective.

## Audience

Product and technology leaders learning investment and ownership from scratch. The primary setting is private equity; explain what depends on the actual ownership and support arrangement.

## Success criteria

- Define necessary terms before using them, and begin with a recognizable company decision.
- Develop one connected explanation from the question through options and example to an actionable conclusion.
- Label fictional scenarios and proposed working methods; do not imply validated performance or guaranteed access to resources.
- Include a useful trade-off and conditions for changing the decision.
- Connect the preceding and following chapters in the configured reading order.
- Provide an independently readable 300–500-word summary and six fictional comic panels with consistent visible and structured text; artwork remains pending.

## Non-goals

Investment advice, universal legal rights, provider endorsements, new claims about historical company returns, or confidential input disclosure.

## Open questions

The methods would benefit from future observed company engagements. Current examples illustrate reasoning and are not evidence of effectiveness.
