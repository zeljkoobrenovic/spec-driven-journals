**Comic.** Larkspur is a fictional software company. An investor has put money into it, and now offers to lend Larkspur an **outside specialist** for a few weeks to make customer setup repeatable — setting a new customer up on the software depends on one Larkspur employee, its implementation specialist. The outside specialist is paid from money the board has already set aside for a customer-setup trial, so the offer is not free help. Eight pages follow that one engagement from an undefined offer through a written agreement, a change of direction in week three and a tested handover in week six. Each page is three strips; the amounts, dates and labels are drawn into the artwork, and a transcript follows every page.

Two clocks run. Days are numbered from day 0, the meeting at which Larkspur’s **board** — the directors who approve the company’s major spending — adopted its plan. The specialist starts on day 21, so engagement week three falls around day 40 and week six around day 63. The board reads the result at day 100.

The people are fictional. Alex is Larkspur’s technology leader, Priya its product leader and the person accountable for the trial, Sam its finance leader and Ines its chief executive. Morgan is the investor’s technology adviser, who made the introduction. The **investor-side sponsor** is the senior person at the investment firm who backs the engagement and secures the specialist’s days; the sponsor is described but not drawn, and the outside specialist appears only as an unnamed figure in the handover strip.
