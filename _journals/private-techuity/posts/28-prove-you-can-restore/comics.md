**Comic.** In the fictional Larkspur scenario, backups had run for three years and nobody had restored the service. A **backup** is a saved copy of the company’s data; a **restore** puts that copy back into use so the service runs again. Seven pages follow one restore from a recovery objective stated in a dispatcher’s words, through a failed test and corrective work funded from money the board had held back, to a retest and a record of what remains exposed and who accepted it. The decisions are the company’s, taken by the Larkspur board, the directors who oversee the company, with its chief executive and its technology lead, under the plan the board approved. The investor’s adviser helps obtain specialist judgment but does not decide what Larkspur funds.

Morgan advises an investor on technology; Alex leads technology; Priya leads product; Sam leads finance; Ines is the chief executive. All are fictional, and so is every figure.
