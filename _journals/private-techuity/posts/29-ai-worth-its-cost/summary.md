**Artificial intelligence (AI)** spending is a cloud bill metered in **tokens** — the units AI services use to count the text a model reads and writes — so a feature’s cost moves with how it is built as well as how much it is used. In the fictional Larkspur scenario, September 2027, forty customers pay €300 a month for a feature that sorts inspection documents. The bill has outgrown revenue for four months, and Morgan, the investor’s technology adviser, asks for “the AI ROI”. The answer needs a unit, a period and a condition.

**Figure 1:** *Measure AI per useful task, separate why the bill moved, and commit only at the low end of a funded range.*

**Choose the company’s unit.** Vendors bill per token or per **model call** — one request to the model and its answer. Larkspur’s unit is **a document sorted and accepted** without correction. Tokens per document rose about 80% from April to August as prompts, retrieved context and retries grew.

**Put two months on one basis.** April: 20 customers, €6,000 revenue, €2,600 cash running cost, €750 left after engineers’ and support time. August: 40 customers, €12,000 revenue, €7,440 cash running cost, **a €1,140 loss** after time. Cash cost per accepted document held at €0.30; per customer it rose from €130 to €186 against a €300 price.

**Separate why the bill changed.** Model charges rose from €1,400 to €5,840: **usage** (twice the customers, more documents each) added €2,656, **construction** (longer prompts, more context, retries) added €3,245, and a 20% vendor price cut removed €1,460, hiding most of the construction rise. Alex, the technology leader, proposes construction work to cut tokens per document by 35%.

**Return has a period and a condition.** **Return on investment (ROI)** is benefit net of cost, divided by cost, for a stated period. April to September 2027: €57,000 revenue, about €30,000 running cost, about €27,000 of cash — roughly 90%, on the condition that accuracy stayed above 95% and review effort below 1.5 hours a week. The one-off trial and release costs remain about €18,000 short in cash. The support assistant returned about 300% in 2027 on a deferred hire, a benefit of that year only.

**Commit at the low end, after the construction work.** The vendor offers **committed spend** for 2028: a fixed monthly amount for twelve months at 25% less per token. The board forecasts 2028 model charges between €5,000 and €14,000 a month, with a €3,000 stress case. Committing €3,750 to cover €5,000 of usage (Option C) saves €1,250 a month across the range and costs €750 more in the stress case. Larkspur chooses C, €45,000 over 2028, signed by Ines, the chief executive, under her one-year permission.

**Set the stop rules first.** Running cost per customer above €150 for two months: fix construction, then reprice. Accuracy below 95% or a safety document missed: review-aid mode that day. A 30% vendor price rise: decide within sixty days. Fewer than 80% renewing at €300: a reprice test. A loss of up to €1,000 a month is tolerated until 31 March 2028 only if the feature lifts main-product renewals by ten points.
