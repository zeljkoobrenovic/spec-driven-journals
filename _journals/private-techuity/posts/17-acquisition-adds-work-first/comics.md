**Comic.** When one company buys another, or a business is separated from the larger business that owns it, the plan behind the purchase can assume savings or independence before anyone has checked that the work is possible in the time allowed. Eight pages follow what happens afterwards, when those assumptions become work somebody has to do: choosing how much to combine, deciding which promised work waits, and replacing a former parent’s services before they stop.

Two words recur. **Integration** is the work of making selected activities of two businesses operate together. A **carve-out** is the separation of a business from its **parent**, the larger business that owns it.

The people are all fictional. Ines is Larkspur’s chief executive; Alex leads technology, Priya leads product and Sam leads finance; Morgan advises an investor. The two scenarios are invented, use their own figures and dates, and say where each one starts.
