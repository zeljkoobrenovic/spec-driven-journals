> **This appendix in one line:** explore how an investment firm could design support that companies find useful and can hold accountable.

<br>
Part IV explained how a company leader can obtain and use investor support. This appendix considers the provider’s task: how could a firm make useful help available consistently across its investments?

**Product thinking** starts with the people using an offering, the work they need help with and evidence of whether the offering helps. **Productscapes** is the author’s proposed application of that approach to investment partnerships and technology support. It is a hypothesis to test, rather than an established model whose effectiveness has been demonstrated here.

## The Learning Path

- [[pt-investment-firm-as-product]] considers the investment firm’s wider offering, including the different needs and powers of investors, company leaders, employees and customers.
- [[pt-technology-capability-as-product]] narrows the proposal to reusable technology support, with defined services, costs, responsibilities and tests of usefulness.

The appendix is optional. Company leaders can use the practical approaches in [[pt-investor-support]] and [[pt-making-support-work]] without adopting Productscapes or designing an investment firm’s support organization.

Read these essays if you are building such an organization, evaluating its design or exploring the author’s proposed extension of the book’s argument. Ask whether the model improves actual decisions after the effort and cost of providing and receiving support are included.
