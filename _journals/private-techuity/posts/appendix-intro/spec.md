# Spec: Productscapes: Designing Investor Support as a Product

## Intent

Introduce Productscapes as the author’s proposed model for designing investor support, addressed to readers working on the provider’s side. Explain the two appendix essays and their relationship to the practical company-side material in Part IV.

## Audience

Readers interested in designing investment and technology-support services after learning the main book’s concepts.

## Success criteria

- Keep the introduction under 400 words and define Productscapes plainly.
- Make the appendix optional for the main reader journey.
- Keep the hypothesis and its evidence limits explicit.
- Link both essays and the relevant main chapters.

## Non-goals

A proven industry standard, a repeat of the main narrative, or an endorsement of one support organization.

## Open questions

The proposed model still needs evidence from actual use.
