# Spec: TeamSystem: Each New Owner Inherits Progress and Unfinished Work

## Intent

Use successive ownership transactions to show that each ownership change creates a new financial starting point while product work carries on across it. Separate reporting periods, adjusted earnings and statutory results with the company’s own reconciliation, distinguish announced transaction values from cash received, and end on what the group accumulates: products, knowledge, dependencies and continuing work.

## Audience

Product and engineering leaders inside companies working under investors, including leaders who inherit an ownership arrangement. Assume no specialist finance background; explain necessary terms before use. Investor-side readers are secondary.

## Non-goals

A current company valuation, complete sponsor cash-flow reconstruction, a claim that all ownership periods had the same strategy or outcome, or validation of Productscapes through resemblance alone. Do not treat a source's undefined profitability measure as EBITDA.

## Open questions

Independent customer and employee histories; full investor cash flows; matched product/cohort evidence; integration outcomes; later ownership-period evidence. The early regional-company setting improves relevance but does not make the entire history a lower mid-market case.
