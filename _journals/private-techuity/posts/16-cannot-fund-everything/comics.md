**Comic.** When an investor owns part of a company, the choice of what to fund has new conditions: establish the actual money, team time and authority before committing, and name the result the funding was agreed on. Eight pages follow a fictional company, Larkspur, through its plan for the first hundred days after the investment is completed: from four requests to a combination it can deliver, and then to the review that revised it. Team time is counted in engineer-weeks; one engineer-week is one person’s working time for one week.

Ines is Larkspur’s chief executive officer. She proposes the plan to the board, the directors who oversee the company and approve its major decisions. Alex leads technology, Priya leads product, and Sam leads finance. All are fictional, and so is every figure.
