# Editorial review: Bibliography and Evidence Guide

Reassessed 15 September 2026 from the full register, previous review, revision log, current source annotations and selective independent checks of consequential claims. This is not a claim to have read every linked source in full.

## Current assessment

The topic index, chapter backlinks and consolidated reading instructions improve this page substantially. Stable identifiers survive the moves to the new reference and handover pages, while source interests and actual consultation limits remain visible. Removing editorial chronology from the opening was appropriate. P02 is now clearly provenance rather than an unexplained method the reader must learn.

The remaining weakness is the claim that optional annotations have been checked for scope. The unicorn annotation now has the right population but reverses the percentage comparison; other primary material contains dates and cash disclosures the chapters call unavailable. Separating optional reading from registered evidence is useful, but it cannot exempt factual descriptions from verification. Preserve genuine access and causal limits while correcting claims that more specific evidence resolves.

## Active recommendations

- **High — Finish verification of consequential annotations and carry corrections into this register where applicable.** The unicorn working paper says reported post-money values average 50% above its modeled fair values, not that fair values are 50% below reported values. The distinction is independently checked in the [valuation review](../02-valuation-is-an-estimate/REVIEW.md). Optional status does not make the incorrect number harmless. The revised ESMA, Davis and healthcare-study boundaries are improvements and should be preserved rather than reopened without new evidence.
- **High — Update the evidence limits used in the historical cases.** S27 dates Qik's acquisition and premium group video calling to January 2011 and the Citrix announcement to March; S49's Note 10 discloses finance costs paid, including note interest. Those are confirmed source findings, not missing evidence. Add the exact pages or sections to the relevant consultation notes after manuscript revision. See the [Hilton/Skype](../26-hilton-and-skype/REVIEW.md) and [TeamSystem](../29-teamsystem/REVIEW.md) reviews for primary links and the distinction between chronology, accounting charges and cash.
- **Medium — Register newly relied-on evidence without inventing earlier consultation.** Hilton's earlier filings establish OnQ before the 2007 acquisition. HgCapital Trust's 2024 annual report records TeamSystem among reported realizations and discloses gross proceeds, which must not be silently substituted for the different July announcement figure or net distributions. If those findings enter the manuscript, add records with the actual new consultation scope and date. This does not require moving every optional reading into the register.
- **Medium — Reconcile the opening date and topic map with S62–S63.** “Sources were consulted on September 12–13” excludes the two entries explicitly accessed on September 14. State the actual range or let each entry carry its date. Add S62 to the Visma topic row and S63 to the AI/definitions route. Topic identifiers are still plain text; linking them to explicit entry anchors would make the new index more useful, using the same approach as the glossary.
- **Low — Reduce the repeated method disclaimer.** The key points, “Proposed Methods and Empirical Evidence” and closing limits all repeat that sources do not validate the author's tools. Preserve one clear statement and source-specific limits. “Every chapter ends” should mean the main chapters; the guide, introductions and reference pages do not all have further-reading lists.
