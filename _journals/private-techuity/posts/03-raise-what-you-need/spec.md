# Spec: Match the Funding to the Work

## Intent

Size the work a company must do next (base work, transition, uncertainty allowance, decision runway), then compare which funding arrangement can support it. Show one fictional Larkspur comparison of two feasible arrangements for the same need and the choice made under stated assumptions, and give leaders who inherit the arrangement an action within their authority. Separate investor type, control, financing and transaction context; a carve-out and a turnaround are not categories of investor. Detailed control and partnership questions belong to Part II and are linked, not repeated.

## Audience

Product and engineering leaders inside companies working under investors, including leaders who inherit an ownership arrangement. Assume no specialist finance background; explain necessary terms before use. Investor-side readers are secondary.

## Non-goals

Universal prescriptions, invented evidence, promises of investment performance, or disclosure of confidential inputs. The chapter is not a legal or tax opinion.
