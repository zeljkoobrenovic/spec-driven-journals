**Comic.** Larkspur is a fictional company that sells scheduling software, and an investor owns a large stake in it. That investor expects fast growth, and the expectation has reached the technology team as a request to make the software “more flexible”, with the reasoning left behind. Eight pages show Larkspur working out what was actually being asked for, comparing three ways to do it against the same customer need, the same date and the same spending limit, and choosing one, with a cheaper standby plan ready if the chosen one fails.

The people: Morgan advises the investor, Alex leads technology, Priya leads product, Sam leads finance, and Ines is the chief executive, who can approve spending inside the limit the board has set. All are fictional, and every amount and date is invented for the example.
