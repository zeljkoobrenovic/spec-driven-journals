**Comic.** Artificial intelligence (AI), software that predicts or generates content from data, is offered to a team as a reason to buy tools and, in the same breath, as a reason to hire fewer people. Eight pages follow Larkspur, a fictional company that sells scheduling software, as it treats that claim the way the hiring chapter treated a headcount claim: trace the work (record each stage it passes through and each wait between stages), find which stage a tool can touch, measure the work that completes, count the cost before payback (the point at which a spend has been repaid by what it saved or earned), and record a decision with a dated gate, a fixed day on which the result is judged by rules written in advance. The second team deferred in that chapter stays deferred on its three conditions throughout.

At Larkspur, Ines is the chief executive and leads the company, Alex leads technology, Priya leads product and Sam leads finance. Morgan advises Larkspur’s investor on technology and does not make the company’s decisions. All are fictional, and so is every figure.
