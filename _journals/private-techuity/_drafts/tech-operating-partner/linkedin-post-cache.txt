Want to become a tech operating partner at a PE fund?

Here's the path almost nobody talks about.

Most people think you need a direct invitation.
That's one way... but there's another route that's more accessible.

Start as an interim, fractional, or advisory tech leader.

Here's how it works:

• First, understand your value in the PE world. Have you worked in a PE-backed business before? What have you achieved in your career of note? What scale of business / industries are most relevant for you?
• Next, get introduced to a fund through a specialised recruiter (like me)
• Take on a portfolio company engagement (e.g. tech DD, interim CTO or CIO, fractional tech advisor)
• Deliver exceptional results on that project
• Build trust with the fund's value creation and investment teams
• Repeat with other portfolio companies, and then other funds
• Demonstrate your value across multiple situations

What funds care about:

• Tech background - you can identify and diagnose tech issues quickly
• Versatility - you can handle different company stages, scales and challenges
• Comms - you have an agreeable bedside manner with founders, exec teams and investors
• Achievements - you have a consistent record driving results in portfolio companies

Over time, you're not just another candidate... you're a proven performer in the PE world.

When a permanent operating partner role opens up (and they do get advertised), you're in a great spot. You've built credibility through real work, not just interviews. You have PE people who will reference you.

This is exactly how I've helped tech leaders transition into PE operating roles. The interim/fractional path builds your track record whilst keeping your options open.
