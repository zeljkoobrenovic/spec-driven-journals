# Spec: Operating Principle: Getting the Job

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I approach finding and accepting an engineering executive role: I
treat the search as a "one-of-one" process — unstructured, variable, and not
generalizable from past interviews — start from an honest answer to *why* I
want the role, work the search through network and recruiters without relying
on either alone, negotiate the full contract (equity, acceleration, severance,
support) without over-negotiating trust away, and hold the freedom to walk
away: the goal is not *an* executive job but the *right* one. The post turns
the "Job Search" checklist into a durable operating principle: deliberate
diligence at every stage, from deciding to pursue the role to handling
rejection.

## Audience

Engineering leaders (including future me) considering their first or next
executive role; recruiters, CEOs, and boards who want to know how I evaluate
an opportunity before saying yes. Declarative first-person register — a
vision statement, not a job-search tutorial.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — the highlight blockquote states the
      "right role over any role" stance in one paragraph.
- [ ] **Rationale is argued, not asserted** — explains why executive
      searches are one-of-one, why motivation must be pressure-tested before
      searching, and why walking away is a feature of the process.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — every section of the source PDF
      (deciding whether to pursue, one-of-one search, finding roles
      internal/external, interview process, negotiating the contract,
      deciding whether to accept, if you don't get the job) appears in
      the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`), regrouped as bullets.
- [ ] **Anti-patterns are concrete** — at least three (e.g. chasing the
      title for status, over-generalizing from past interview loops,
      over-negotiating until trust is damaged).
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — Authoritative References names Larson's *The
      Engineering Executive's Primer* and the lethain.com companion post.

## Non-goals

- Not an onboarding post — everything after the signed offer belongs to
  [[first-90-days]].
- Not a networking guide — sustained relationship-building as an ongoing
  practice is [[your-network]]'s territory; here the network appears only as
  a search channel.
- No compensation numbers, named companies, or personal war stories — the
  record states the operating stance, not a memoir.
