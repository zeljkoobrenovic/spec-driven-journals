**Panel 1:** *The hook: choose how to decide before you decide.*

![Comic panel: Leo swings the same hammer at a screw, a plant, and a cracking window while Vera notes every style fails outside its zone.](assets/images/leadership-styles/comic-02-one-hammer-everywhere.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the comfortable style gets applied where it does not fit.*

![Comic panel: a divided meeting room with a spinning clock and circular whiteboard arrows while Vera says the decision needs a decider, not more input.](assets/images/leadership-styles/comic-03-consensus-as-avoidance.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *Anti-pattern: consensus as conflict avoidance produces an expensive stalemate.*

![Comic panel: Vera presents a board with three tools labeled Recurs, Scattered, and Stalled, saying the situation picks the style.](assets/images/leadership-styles/comic-04-three-triggers.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: policy when it recurs, consensus when context is scattered, conviction when it is stalled.*

![Comic panel: Vera pins a one-page policy and walks away while three teams independently make the same consistent choice.](assets/images/leadership-styles/comic-05-policy-scales.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Policy: codify the recurring decision so the org moves without you in the room.*

![Comic panel: stakeholders add puzzle pieces to a shared framing document while an appointed closer holds a deadline flag and Vera lists the rules.](assets/images/leadership-styles/comic-06-consensus-with-a-closer.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Consensus: everyone is heard — with a framing doc, an appointed closer, and a deadline.*

![Comic panel: Vera writes her reasoning beside a small mirror with a question mark while a stalled cart waits on a track behind her.](assets/images/leadership-styles/comic-07-conviction-with-a-guard.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Conviction: run the micromanagement self-check, write the reasoning, then decide.*

![Comic panel: Vera hands Leo the least-worn tool from her toolbox in front of a calendar with one circled date, urging monthly practice of the weakest style.](assets/images/leadership-styles/comic-08-adaptability-not-purity.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *Closer: adaptability, not purity — deliberately practice the style you underuse.*
