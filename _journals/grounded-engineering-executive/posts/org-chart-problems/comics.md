![Leo proudly presents a large, perfectly symmetrical org chart while Vera looks on with a skeptical smile.](assets/images/org-chart-problems/comic-01-beautiful-chart.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the reorg is 'done' because the chart looks clean.*

![Vera peels back the org chart to reveal a tangled web of real communication lines behind it, surprising Leo.](assets/images/org-chart-problems/comic-02-hidden-web.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: beneath the chart, work flows through a web of communication the boxes never show.*

![Vera sketches three stacked layers — hierarchy, social network, and work flow — while Leo takes notes.](assets/images/org-chart-problems/comic-03-three-layers.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The model: formal, informal, and value-creation structures — the chart draws only the first.*

![A mirror reflects four tangled team communication lines as an equally tangled software architecture diagram.](assets/images/org-chart-problems/comic-04-conways-mirror.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: systems mirror communication paths — design architecture and team interactions together.*

![Leo draws a horizontal flow from idea through a small team to a running system with a feedback loop, as Vera nods.](assets/images/org-chart-problems/comic-05-design-for-flow.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *The shift: design for the flow of change and fast feedback — not for tidy boxes.*

![A decision paper passed up a ladder of managers loses sticky notes of context at every rung, arriving blank at the top.](assets/images/org-chart-problems/comic-06-escalation-ladder.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The failure mode: decisions decay as they climb — the top has authority but no context.*

![Vera hands a decision back to a small team at their screens, telling them they have the context to decide.](assets/images/org-chart-problems/comic-07-decide-in-context.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The practice: push decisions to the people with the context, with the trust made explicit.*

![Vera files the printed org chart into a drawer while a board behind her shows teams linked by clean flow lines.](assets/images/org-chart-problems/comic-08-chart-in-drawer.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: the chart is a filing system — the organization runs on flow.*
