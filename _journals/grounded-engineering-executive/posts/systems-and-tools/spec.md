# Spec: Management Systems and Tools

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I manage through systems and tools rather than heroics: I model
management problems as stocks and flows and find the real constraint; I run
internal work as a product (discovery, selection, validation); I align through
explicit leadership controls with agreed degrees of alignment instead of
standing meetings; and I protect my time by sizing work backward and
delegating work into systems I then trust. The post turns the "Engineering
Management Systems & Tools" checklist — fifteen tools from *An Elegant
Puzzle* — into one executive operating principle. The article organizes the
tools into a small number of themes; the Checklist tab carries the full
fifteen-section inventory.

## Audience

My direct reports and leadership bench (so they know which tool I will reach
for and why); peer executives; engineering leaders comparing operating
models. First-person declarative.

## Non-goals

- Not a summary of the whole book — only the systems-and-tools checklist,
  read through an executive lens.
- Not [[engineering-strategy]] or [[measuring-engineering-organizations]] —
  those Primer-grounded records own strategy and metrics in depth; this
  record places both inside the wider toolbox.
- Not an organizational-design record — sizing teams and states of teams
  live in the appendix sibling [[organizational-design]].
- No claim that tools replace judgment — the tools are how judgment scales,
  not a substitute for it.
