**Panel 5:** *The identity shift: peers as the first team — collaboration at the top instead of a zero-sum contest.*

![Vera pauses a broken machine and guides the exhausted hero away to rest while Leo lowers a small megaphone reading work harder.](assets/images/engineering-culture/comic-06-kill-your-heroes.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Kill your heroes: stop doing it harder — pause the broken system and fix the design.*

![The full team redesigns the machine over blueprints while Vera and Leo watch a wall chart trend upward.](assets/images/engineering-culture/comic-07-system-reset.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The reset pays off: a redesigned system the whole team can carry — improving one sprint at a time.*
