# Spec: Engineering Culture

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I build engineering culture as an executive: through mechanics, not
slogans. Inclusion is defined operationally — access to opportunity and access
to membership — and both are measured. Important people decisions (project
leads, senior roles, promotions) run on rubrics and structured processes so
opportunity does not pool around a small group. My peers are my first team.
Company freedoms (positive and negative) are managed deliberately rather than
imported blindly. And hero culture is treated as a system-design failure to
reset, not a work ethic to reward. The post turns the *Elegant Puzzle* culture
checklist into an executive operating principle.

## Audience

My direct reports and leadership bench (so they know how opportunity is
allocated and why heroes don't get parades here); peer executives; engineering
leaders comparing operating models. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states culture-as-mechanics in one
      paragraph: opportunity + membership, both measured; rubrics and
      structured processes; peers as first team; hero culture as system
      failure.
- [ ] **Inclusion is operational** — opportunity and membership each get a
      definition, concrete improvement moves, and named measurements
      (retention by cohort, usage rate, level distribution, time at level;
      referral rate, attendance, coffee-chat completion).
- [ ] **Fair project-lead selection is concrete** — the structured pipeline
      appears (scope doc, broad announcement, private applications, 3+ working
      days, nudging non-self-selectors, criteria-based choice, sponsor,
      feedback to rejected applicants, public record).
- [ ] **Hero culture is reframed** — effort issue vs system design issue;
      projects fail one sprint at a time; stop covering bad process with
      bursts of effort.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — all six PDF sections (opportunity
      and membership, project leads, peers as first team, senior-role
      processes, company freedoms, kill your heroes) reproduced in the
      Checklist tab (`checklist.md`), with sub-groups and concrete numbers
      preserved.
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — References name Larson's *An Elegant Puzzle*
      and note the lethain.com essay origins.
