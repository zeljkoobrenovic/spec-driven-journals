![Comic panel: Vera arrives at a new office while Leo eagerly asks what she will change first.](assets/images/first-90-days/comic-01-day-one.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *Day one: everyone expects the new executive to start changing things.*

![Comic panel: Vera at a desk with thought bubbles of the CEO, the team, and herself, all with clock icons.](assets/images/first-90-days/comic-02-pressure-to-shine.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: pressure from the CEO, the team, and yourself to demonstrate impact quickly.*

![Comic panel: Leo presents a week-three reorg chart that is cracking apart behind him.](assets/images/first-90-days/comic-03-quick-win-theater.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The trap: quick-win theater — a visible change that later has to be unwound costs more trust than it bought.*

![Comic panel: Vera takes notes at the back of a team meeting while engineers discuss at a whiteboard.](assets/images/first-90-days/comic-04-learning-quarter.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: treat the organization as a system you do not yet understand — and refuse to modify what you do not understand.*

![Comic panel: Vera takes a support call with a deploy running on a terminal beside her while Leo watches.](assets/images/first-90-days/comic-05-primary-sources.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it plays out: tickets, incidents, on-call, and a hands-on deploy tell you more than any report.*

![Comic panel: Vera drops small coins into a clear jar labeled Trust.](assets/images/first-90-days/comic-06-trust-deposits.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Build trust before conflict: small, consistent deposits compound into the reservoir hard calls will need.*

![Comic panel: Vera picks two sticky notes from a wall full of them while Leo gestures at the rest.](assets/images/first-90-days/comic-07-one-or-two-changes.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Then act — but small: one or two high-leverage improvements that will still look good in two years.*

![Comic panel: Vera faces the reader with a long winding path behind her outreaching a short sprint line.](assets/images/first-90-days/comic-08-slower-is-faster.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: move slower than your instincts — it is the fastest route to durable impact.*
