*The working checklist behind this principle, in the order I use it: audit myself first, diagnose the gap, then choose the response. The Article tab carries the rationale, implications, and anti-patterns.*

### Self-Awareness: Audit Your Standards

- [ ] Am I consistently operating at a higher standard than my organization supports?
- [ ] Am I overworking to maintain standards that others don't share?
- [ ] Is my frustration about performance actually frustration about misaligned expectations?
- [ ] Are my standards helping me achieve long-term impact — or burning me out?

### Diagnose Misalignment

- [ ] Does leadership knowingly tolerate a lower standard in this area?
- [ ] Is this a deliberate tradeoff (speed, cost, focus) rather than incompetence?
- [ ] Am I escalating a performance issue that leadership sees as acceptable?
- [ ] Am I unintentionally trying to hold the CEO accountable for a known but deprioritized issue?

### Escalate Carefully

- [ ] Have I framed the issue as impact-driven rather than personal criticism?
- [ ] Have I considered that this disagreement reflects a difference in strategy?
- [ ] Am I escalating out of ethical obligation — or frustration?
- [ ] Do I understand that pushing too hard may label me as "difficult"?
- [ ] Am I prepared for slow change rather than immediate resolution?

### Avoid the Burnout Trap

- [ ] Am I compensating for a peer's underperformance that management is unwilling to address?
- [ ] Am I grinding against constraints that realistically won't change?
- [ ] Is the penalty for lowering my standard here actually acceptable?
- [ ] Am I choosing battles that matter most?

### Use Role Modeling Instead of Force

**Model**

- [ ] Have I clearly identified the area I want to improve?
- [ ] Am I demonstrating a high-standard approach visibly and consistently?
- [ ] Have I tested and refined the approach until it works well?

**Document**

- [ ] Have I written a clear, practical guide to the approach?
- [ ] Does it explain both *how* and *why*?
- [ ] Does it evaluate outcomes rather than assume superiority?

**Share**

- [ ] Have I shared the documented approach with interested teams?
- [ ] Am I engaging those who opt in?
- [ ] Am I letting go of teams that don't engage?

### Adapt When Necessary

- [ ] What matters most at this moment — excellence, speed, stability, relationships?
- [ ] Is this a season where flexibility is more valuable than rigidity?
- [ ] Am I holding onto a "lesson not worth learning"?
- [ ] Are some penalties not worth paying?

### Final Alignment Check

- [ ] Are my standards aligned with the organization's tolerances?
- [ ] Are my standards serving my long-term goals?
- [ ] Am I improving outcomes — or just winning arguments?
- [ ] Am I preserving relationships while maintaining integrity?
