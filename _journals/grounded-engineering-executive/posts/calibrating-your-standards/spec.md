# Spec: Operating Principle: Calibrating Your Standards

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I keep my personal standards calibrated against what the
organization actually supports: I audit whether I am operating at a higher
standard than the company sustains, diagnose whether a gap is a deliberate
leadership tradeoff rather than incompetence, escalate carefully and only
when it matters, refuse to burn out compensating for constraints that won't
change, and raise standards through role modeling (model, document, share)
rather than force. The post turns the "Calibrating Your Standards" checklist
into a durable operating principle: misaligned standards are an alignment
problem to diagnose, not a performance problem to win.

## Audience

Engineering leaders (including future me) navigating the gap between their
own bar and the organization's tolerances; my own leadership team, so they
understand how I choose battles and why I don't fight every one. Declarative
first-person register — a vision statement, not a how-to tutorial.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — the highlight blockquote states the
      calibrate-then-model stance in one paragraph.
- [ ] **Rationale is argued, not asserted** — explains why frustration about
      performance is often frustration about misaligned expectations, and why
      role modeling outperforms escalation and force.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — every section of the source PDF
      (self-awareness audit, diagnose misalignment, escalate carefully, avoid
      the burnout trap, role modeling instead of force, adapt when necessary,
      final alignment check) appears in the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`), regrouped as bullets.
- [ ] **Anti-patterns are concrete** — at least three (e.g. overworking to
      hold a standard nobody else shares, escalating a known and deliberately
      deprioritized tradeoff to the CEO, winning arguments instead of
      improving outcomes).
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — Authoritative References names Larson's *The
      Engineering Executive's Primer* chapter this checklist distills.

## Non-goals

- Not a trust-verification post — [[inspected-trust]] covers how I verify the
  organization's work; this post covers calibrating my own bar against the
  organization's tolerances.
- Not a values-definition post — [[organizational-values]] covers the values
  the organization commits to; this post covers my personal standards where
  they exceed or diverge from those.
- Not a performance-management or PIP guide — underperformance handling is
  out of scope.
