# Spec: Operating Principle: Organizational Values

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I handle organizational values: values exist to guide real
decisions, not to decorate walls — so I test every candidate value for being
reversible (a real strategic choice), applicable (usable in concrete
trade-offs), and honest (describing how we actually behave today, changing
behavior before documenting it). I prefer extending existing company values
over inventing new ones, roll values out slowly with buy-in rather than
top-down announcement, and integrate them into hiring, onboarding,
promotions, and major decisions so they are used, not framed. The post turns
the "Organizational Values" checklist into a durable operating principle:
values as working decision tools, maintained and evolved like any other
system.

## Audience

Engineering leaders and my own leadership team, who will be held accountable
for modeling the values; peer executives and HR partners who co-own
company-wide values. Declarative first-person register — a vision statement,
not a values-workshop facilitation guide.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — the highlight blockquote states the
      "values are decision tools, not decoration" stance in one paragraph.
- [ ] **Rationale is argued, not asserted** — explains why reversibility,
      applicability, and honesty are the quality bar, and why behavior
      change must precede documentation.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — every section of the source PDF
      (clarify the purpose, decide the scope, evaluate each value for
      quality, avoid common traps, plan the rollout, integrate into daily
      work, align with strategy, maintain and evolve) appears in
      the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`), regrouped as bullets.
- [ ] **Anti-patterns are concrete** — at least three (e.g. identity values
      about how we want to see ourselves, vague prioritization slogans,
      announcing values top-down before behavior exists).
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — Authoritative References names Larson's *The
      Engineering Executive's Primer* and the lethain.com companion post.

## Non-goals

- Not a strategy post — values reinforce and clarify strategy, but the
  strategy itself is [[engineering-strategy]]'s territory.
- Not a standards post — engineering quality bars and how I calibrate them
  live in [[calibrating-your-standards]]; values are the decision-guiding
  layer above them.
- No proposed value list for any specific organization — the record states
  how I evaluate and roll out values, not which values to pick.
