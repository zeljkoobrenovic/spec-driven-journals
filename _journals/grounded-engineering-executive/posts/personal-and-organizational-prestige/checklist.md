*The working checklist behind this principle, ordered as the practice runs — from deciding whether to invest at all, through one artifact's lifecycle, to the organizational half. The Article tab carries the rationale, implications, and anti-patterns.*

### Decide If You Even Need to Invest

- [ ] Can I hire senior candidates successfully?
- [ ] Do people seek my advice beyond my direct reports?
- [ ] Can I start interview processes for the roles I want?
- [ ] Do executive recruiters bring me interesting roles?
- [ ] Is my network naturally expanding to more senior people?

- If mostly "yes" → minimal investment needed.
- If mostly "no" → build prestige deliberately.

### Understand the Goal

- [ ] I am optimizing for prestige (passive positive awareness), not audience size.
- [ ] I understand prestige ≠ brand ≠ followers.
- [ ] I will avoid over-investing in content volume.

### Choose a Timeless, High-Leverage Topic

- [ ] I have a meaningful, non-obvious perspective.
- [ ] The topic will remain relevant for years to come.
- [ ] The insight adds depth, not controversy.
- [ ] It reflects real experience and accomplishment.

### Pick the Right Format

- [ ] Blog post or conference talk (preferred).
- [ ] Small enough to iterate quickly.
- [ ] Creates a permanent, discoverable artifact.
- [ ] Not a book or podcast (unless intentional and strategic).

### Create High-Quality Content

- [ ] I expect to discard 2–3 drafts.
- [ ] I've gotten feedback from experienced reviewers.
- [ ] Readers can clearly articulate my key insight.
- [ ] Some thoughtful disagreement is possible (a good sign).

### Distribute Simply

- [ ] I asked 5–10 peers to share it simultaneously.
- [ ] I shared it thoughtfully in relevant communities.
- [ ] I avoided controversy-driven promotion.

### Make It Discoverable

- [ ] It lives on a personal website.
- [ ] It's linked from LinkedIn or professional profiles.
- [ ] It's easy to find via search.
- [ ] Future content is collected in one place.

### Repeat (Lightly)

- [ ] I'll repeat this 2–3 times over several years.
- [ ] I will prioritize quality over frequency.
- [ ] I won't build a heavy content machine.

### Avoid Bad Metrics

**Do not optimize for:**

- [ ] Pageviews.
- [ ] Social media followers.
- [ ] Content volume.
- [ ] Sales (if prestige is the goal).

**Instead:**

- [ ] Timebox effort.
- [ ] Notice whether content comes up in interviews or hiring conversations.
- [ ] Observe whether opportunities expand naturally.

### For Organizations

- [ ] Focus on meaningful, attractive engineering problems.
- [ ] Highlight prestigious employees or work.
- [ ] Produce a small amount of thoughtful, high-quality content.
- [ ] Avoid high-frequency publishing strategies.

### The Core Principle

- Prestige = ambient, positive, easily discoverable familiarity.

- [ ] Small amount.
- [ ] High quality.
- [ ] Timeless insight.
- [ ] Easily discoverable.
- [ ] Repeated occasionally.
