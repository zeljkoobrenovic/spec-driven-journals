# Spec: Operating Principle: Engineering Strategy

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I approach engineering strategy: I write it personally, ground it in
an honest diagnosis, and only publish policies I am willing to enforce.
Strategy is diagnosis → guiding policies → coherent actions (Rumelt's kernel);
anything else is a vision statement wearing a strategy costume. The post turns
the "Engineering Strategy" checklist into an operating principle covering how
I diagnose, draft, socialize, launch, and review a strategy — and what I do
when the company strategy above it is undocumented.

## Audience

Engineering leaders and peer executives who want to know what a strategy
process run by me looks like; staff+ engineers who will be asked to pressure-
test a diagnosis. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states the write-it-personally,
      enforce-what-you-publish stance in one paragraph.
- [ ] **Kernel structure is explicit** — diagnosis / guiding policies /
      coherent actions named and each given its quality bar (applicable,
      enforced, leverage-creating).
- [ ] **Process is visible** — working group, skeptics, socialize-before-
      announce, short feedback window, review cadence all appear.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — all PDF sections (before you start,
      structure, diagnosis, guiding policies, quality check, coherent actions,
      writing process, launch, missing company strategy, ongoing review)
      regrouped as bullets in the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`).
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — References name Larson's *Primer* and the
      lethain.com strategy essay.

## Non-goals

- Not the strategy document itself — this is how I produce one.
- Not annual planning mechanics — [[planning]] covers that; strategy and
  planning are deliberately separate records.
- No repo-specific or company-specific strategy content.
