![Comic panel: Leo unveils a glossy rocket poster reading world-class engineering while Vera looks unimpressed.](assets/images/engineering-strategy/comic-01-vision-poster.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: a shiny vision statement, presented as a strategy.*

![Comic panel: Vera points at the poster now wearing a cape and mask while Leo scratches his head.](assets/images/engineering-strategy/comic-02-strategy-costume.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: it sounds good and changes nothing — a vision statement wearing a strategy costume.*

![Comic panel: Vera draws three whiteboard boxes labeled Diagnosis, Policies, and Actions.](assets/images/engineering-strategy/comic-03-the-kernel.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The principle: a strategy has exactly three parts — diagnosis, guiding policies, coherent actions.*

![Comic panel: a cobwebbed strategy binder sits on a shelf above a shrugging committee as Leo whispers that nobody follows it.](assets/images/engineering-strategy/comic-04-committee-shelfware.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The wrong way: strategy by committee — sponsored, not owned, and dead on the shelf.*

![Comic panel: Vera writes a strategy draft by hand at her desk with pages spread around her.](assets/images/engineering-strategy/comic-05-write-it-yourself.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *My move: I write the strategy personally — visible commitment is what makes enforcement credible.*

![Comic panel: Vera passes a policy card through three gates labeled Applicable, Enforced, and Leverage, with a rejected card in a bin.](assets/images/engineering-strategy/comic-06-policy-quality-bar.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The quality bar: every policy must be applicable, enforced, and leverage-creating — or it goes back.*

![Comic panel: Vera takes notes across from a stern, arms-crossed engineer while Leo peeks in from the doorway.](assets/images/engineering-strategy/comic-07-meet-the-skeptics.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *How it survives: seek out the skeptics before launch — the people who could kill it shape it first.*

![Comic panel: Vera faces the reader holding a single thin signed document with Leo nodding beside her.](assets/images/engineering-strategy/comic-08-enforce-what-you-publish.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: short, honest, and enforced — only publish what you are willing to enforce.*
