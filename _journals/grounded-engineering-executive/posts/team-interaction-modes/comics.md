![Leo overwhelmed by calendar invites while teams in the background wait on each other in a circle.](assets/images/team-interaction-modes/comic-02-gridlock.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: all-to-all collaboration means gridlock — everything blocks on everything.*

![Vera draws three diagrams on a whiteboard showing an overlap, a socket joint, and a dotted arc between team shapes.](assets/images/team-interaction-modes/comic-03-three-modes.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The principle: three modes — collaboration, X-as-a-Service, facilitating — chosen deliberately per team pair.*

![Two teams working closely in a shared zone full of sketches while Vera points at a large hourglass.](assets/images/team-interaction-modes/comic-04-collaboration-timebox.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *Collaboration: high-bandwidth discovery — worth its high cost only when time-boxed with an exit.*

![A team plugs into a clean, well-documented socket provided by another team while Vera comments.](assets/images/team-interaction-modes/comic-05-service-socket.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *X-as-a-Service: a clean socket — clear ownership, strong docs, minimal day-to-day coordination.*

![A coaching figure helps a team along a dotted arc, then walks away as the team continues on its own.](assets/images/team-interaction-modes/comic-06-facilitate-then-leave.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Facilitating: grow the capability, agree what self-sufficient means, then step away.*

![Vera listens to a sparking connection between two teams with a stethoscope while pointing at the boundary line.](assets/images/team-interaction-modes/comic-07-friction-sensing.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Friction as sensing: an awkward interaction points at a wrong boundary or mode, not at bad people.*

![Vera and Leo stand before a tidy chart of a few deliberate team connections, Vera closing her notebook.](assets/images/team-interaction-modes/comic-08-designed-flow.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: interaction by design — an explicit mode for every important pair, and room to flow.*
