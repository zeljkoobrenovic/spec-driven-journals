![Three small team rockets launch independently from separate pads while an old shared release train rusts nearby.](assets/images/team-first-boundaries/comic-07-independent-releases.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The payoff: independent releases — the validation gate is test, deploy, and operate alone.*
