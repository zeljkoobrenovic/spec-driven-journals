![Comic panel: an exasperated Leo holds messy sprint boards and asks Vera to hire a TPM.](assets/images/run-engineering-processes/comic-01-hire-a-tpm.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: a struggling process, and the reflex answer — hire a specialist.*

![Comic panel: Vera holds a balance scale weighing a gem labeled Quality against weights labeled Overhead.](assets/images/run-engineering-processes/comic-02-quality-vs-overhead.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The frame: every process trades quality against overhead — and must be worth its cost.*

![Comic panel: Vera points at a five-rung ladder chart of process patterns with a pin marking Baseline.](assets/images/run-engineering-processes/comic-03-name-the-pattern.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The first move: name the pattern you are actually in before proposing a new one.*

![Comic panel: Leo sketches a giant corporate process org over their tiny office while Vera warns about overhead without scale.](assets/images/run-engineering-processes/comic-04-cargo-cult.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The wrong way: cargo-culting a company ten times your size.*

![Comic panel: Vera inspects the sprint board with a magnifying glass, revealing tags for no owner, no rotation, no recognition.](assets/images/run-engineering-processes/comic-05-under-execution.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *The diagnosis: is the model truly failing, or are we under-executing within it? Usually the latter.*

![Comic panel: engineers and managers each hold their own process clipboard, Leo among them, as Vera states they run their own processes.](assets/images/run-engineering-processes/comic-06-baseline-default.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The principle: default to baseline — engineers and managers run engineering-scoped processes themselves.*

![Comic panel: Leo relay-passes his process clipboard to a teammate while Vera applauds and pins a star on him.](assets/images/run-engineering-processes/comic-07-valued-ownership.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *What makes it durable: rotations, overlapping handoffs, and process work that visibly counts.*

![Comic panel: Vera plants a flag labeled Our model while faded trend banners blow past behind her.](assets/images/run-engineering-processes/comic-08-hold-the-model.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: ground the model in beliefs, commit for three to four years, and change intentionally — never fashionably.*
