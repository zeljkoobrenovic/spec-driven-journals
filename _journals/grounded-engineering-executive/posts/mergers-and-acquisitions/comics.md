**Panel 1:** *The hook: before excitement, a written thesis — or no evaluation at all.*

![Comic panel: a snowball labeled Deal rolls downhill collecting papers and dollar signs as Vera warns that deals generate their own momentum.](assets/images/mergers-and-acquisitions/comic-02-deal-momentum.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: without an anchor, diligence becomes a search for reasons to say yes.*

![Comic panel: Vera lifts a cloth to reveal a hidden anchor labeled Integration chained to a shiny rocket being priced on its pedestal.](assets/images/mergers-and-acquisitions/comic-03-upside-only-pricing.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *Anti-pattern: upside-only valuation — the pitch is presented, the integration is lived.*

![Comic panel: Vera pins a thesis sheet with a bullseye icon above three checkbox sticky notes, saying write what must be true.](assets/images/mergers-and-acquisitions/comic-04-the-written-thesis.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: a written thesis turns diligence into testing assumptions, not collecting impressions.*

![Comic panel: Vera opens a sports car hood to reveal a hamster wheel and tangled cables, asking if it is a real engine or a wrapper.](assets/images/mergers-and-acquisitions/comic-05-under-the-hood.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Diligence is about substance: is the implementation real, owned, secure, and scalable?*

![Comic panel: Vera holds back a contract signing while pointing at a map of two roads merging into one, insisting on the integration plan first.](assets/images/mergers-and-acquisitions/comic-06-plan-before-signing.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Integration is planned before closing — never discovered after.*

![Comic panel: outvoted at the executive table, Vera files a dissent note into a flagged folder and rolls up her sleeves to support the decision.](assets/images/mergers-and-acquisitions/comic-07-documented-dissent.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Dissent is a company act: document it professionally, then commit.*

![Comic panel: Vera holds the deal folder before a mirror where her reflection wears a hard hat, asking if she would run the integration herself.](assets/images/mergers-and-acquisitions/comic-08-the-sanity-check.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *Closer: approve only the deal whose integration you would personally run.*
