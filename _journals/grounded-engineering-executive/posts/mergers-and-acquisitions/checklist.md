*The working checklist behind this principle, ordered the way a deal unfolds — strategy, thesis, evaluation, integration, judgment. The Article tab carries the rationale, implications, and anti-patterns.*

### Establish Business Strategy (Before Evaluating Any Deal)

**Clarify how M&A supports the company's strategy**

- [ ] Are our business lines clearly defined?
- [ ] What are revenue and cash-flow expectations per line?
- [ ] What role should M&A play?
  - [ ] Accelerate growth.
  - [ ] Enter new markets.
  - [ ] Acquihire.
  - [ ] Reduce competition.
  - [ ] Other.
- [ ] Are we pursuing:
  - [ ] Acquihires (talent).
  - [ ] Product acquisitions.
  - [ ] Full business acquisitions.
- [ ] What deal sizes/stages are acceptable?
- [ ] Is this written down and aligned with the executive team?

### Define the Acquisition Thesis (Deal-Specific)

**What must be true for this deal to make sense?**

- [ ] How exactly does this acquisition fit our strategy?
- [ ] Is this about time-to-market acceleration?
- [ ] Is this a permanent product pillar or an interim solution?
- [ ] What assumptions must hold true for the deal to succeed?
- [ ] Have we documented those assumptions explicitly?
- [ ] Does the valuation reflect integration cost and risk (not just upside)?

### Engineering Evaluation

**A. Product Implementation**

- [ ] Is the implementation real and production-grade?
- [ ] Is it first-party IP, or is it primarily built on third-party APIs?
- [ ] Have we reviewed the actual source code?
- [ ] Is architecture scalable and maintainable?
- [ ] Are there hidden technical shortcuts or fragile systems?

**B. Intellectual Property**

- [ ] Do claimed patents/IP actually exist?
- [ ] Does the company truly own its core IP?
- [ ] Are there licensing risks?
- [ ] Have engineers validated the technical substance behind the IP?

**C. Security**

- [ ] Who owns security?
- [ ] Are regular penetration tests conducted?
- [ ] When was the last test? What were the findings?
- [ ] Is there employee security training?
- [ ] Have there been breaches?
- [ ] How would they detect a breach?
- [ ] Is sensitive data handled properly?

**D. Compliance**

- [ ] What certifications exist? (SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, HITRUST, etc.)
- [ ] Are audit reports available for the last 2–3 years?
- [ ] Are controls actually operational?
- [ ] Are there compliance gaps that affect valuation?

**E. Integration Mismatches**

- [ ] What is their cloud vendor? (AWS, GCP, Azure, on-prem)
- [ ] What is their core tech stack?
- [ ] How different is it from ours?
- [ ] Are there long-term vendor contracts?
- [ ] How painful would stack consolidation be?
- [ ] Would separate stacks create long-term subcultures?

**F. Costs & Scalability**

- [ ] What are the total Engineering costs over time?
- [ ] What does it cost to generate each dollar of revenue?
- [ ] Are there economies or dysfunctions of scale?
- [ ] How scalable is infrastructure?
- [ ] Can the product scale without a rewrite?
- [ ] Have Finance + Engineering modeled long-term cost structure?

**G. Engineering Culture**

- [ ] How are technical decisions made?
- [ ] How are teams structured?
- [ ] Is decision-making centralized or distributed?
- [ ] Who are the real influencers?
- [ ] How compatible are operating models?
- [ ] Is friction manageable?

### Process Discipline

- [ ] Do we use a standard Engineering evaluation template?
- [ ] Did we customize it to this deal's thesis?
- [ ] Did we request documentation in advance?
- [ ] Did we limit meetings to high-value sessions?
- [ ] Did we validate or invalidate key assumptions?
- [ ] Did we sync clearly with Finance and Legal before the recommendation?

### Integration Plan (Before Closing)

**Baseline Integration Approach**

- [ ] Will we run the acquired stack "as-is" initially? (e.g., 6 months)
- [ ] Do we have a consolidation timeline?
- [ ] Are integration risks documented?

**Technology Integration**

- [ ] Full rewrite?
- [ ] API integration?
- [ ] Temporary coexistence?
- [ ] Long-term multi-stack tolerance?
- [ ] Does this align with our Engineering strategy?

**Team Integration**

- [ ] Where does the acquired Head of Engineering report?
- [ ] Are teams merged or kept separate?
- [ ] Is infrastructure/security/data centralized?
- [ ] Is role clarity communicated early?

**Leadership Integration**

- [ ] Are senior leaders' roles defined?
- [ ] Have we aligned expectations privately?
- [ ] Are we setting up new leaders for success?
- [ ] Are we protecting the morale of existing teams?

### Executive Judgment & Dissent

- [ ] Have I clearly articulated risks from a company perspective (not just Engineering)?
- [ ] Have I separated emotional resistance from real risk?
- [ ] If overruled, did I document concerns professionally?
- [ ] Am I confident we're not falling into sunk-cost bias?

### If You Are Being Acquired

- [ ] Do we understand the acquirer's view of the deal? (business, product, acquihire)
- [ ] Have we protected team compensation packages?
- [ ] Have we clarified reporting lines?
- [ ] Is there a meaningful role for me?
- [ ] If required to stay, is compensation appropriate?
- [ ] Have we helped the team prepare for transition?

### Final Sanity Check

**Before recommending "yes":**

- [ ] Does this acquisition clearly advance our strategy?
- [ ] Are integration costs realistic?
- [ ] Are the biggest risks known and manageable?
- [ ] Would I still approve this deal if I had to personally run the integration?
