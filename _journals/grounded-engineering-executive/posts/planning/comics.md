**Panel 1:** *The hook: planning season — budget, allocation, and roadmap all fighting in one room.*

![Comic panel: three speech bubbles tangle into a knot above the table as Vera warns that mixing them ruins all three.](assets/images/planning/comic-02-three-ruined-debates.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: every roadmap disagreement reopens the budget, every allocation question becomes a pitch.*

![Comic panel: two colleagues exchange a folded roadmap page behind a pillar while Vera watches, frowning.](assets/images/planning/comic-03-side-deals.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: conflicts resolved in hallway side deals instead of open group discussion.*

![Comic panel: Vera draws three numbered whiteboard boxes labeled Budget, Allocation, and Roadmap in sequence.](assets/images/planning/comic-04-three-phases.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: sequence the decisions — budget sets the envelope, allocation divides it, the roadmap fills it.*

![Comic panel: Vera steadies a tray of large jars while Leo tries to shuffle beans between them.](assets/images/planning/comic-05-steady-allocation.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *How it plays out: steady allocation in big buckets beats reactive churn — compounding needs calm.*

![Comic panel: Leo holds a sign reading more headcount while Vera calmly tells him to model the org first.](assets/images/planning/comic-06-more-headcount.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The reflex to resist: 'we need more headcount' — asked before modeling the org or checking recruiting capacity.*

![Comic panel: Vera hands a team an open frame and they run freely inside it.](assets/images/planning/comic-07-plans-empower.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The closer: a plan is a shared constraint teams move fast inside — judged by whether it empowers them.*
