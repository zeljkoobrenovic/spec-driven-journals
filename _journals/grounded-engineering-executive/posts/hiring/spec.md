# Spec: Operating Principle: Hiring

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I operate in hiring as an executive: I own the hiring *system* —
documented loops, a written leveling framework, standardized compensation
governance, trained interviewers and hiring managers, and monthly metrics —
rather than personally driving individual hires; I resist over-optimizing a
process that is already fast and stable; I show up personally only where the
executive adds unique value (closing key candidates, out-of-band decisions);
and I follow the process by default while breaking it deliberately when the
business demands it. The post turns the "Hiring" checklist into a durable
operating principle: an intentional system, not accidental heroics.

## Audience

Engineering leaders and hiring managers who want to know what hiring system I
build and hold them accountable to; recruiting partners and peer executives
who want to know where I personally engage. Declarative first-person register
— a vision statement, not an interviewing handbook.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — the highlight blockquote states the
      system-over-heroics stance in one paragraph.
- [ ] **Rationale is argued, not asserted** — explains why the executive's
      leverage is the system (leveling, comp governance, metrics, training)
      and why over-optimizing a healthy process destroys speed.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — every section of the source PDF
      (establish the hiring process, avoid over-optimizing, monitor & debug,
      help close key candidates, level candidates effectively, standardize
      compensation decisions, manage hiring prioritization, train hiring
      managers, balance internal/external/network hiring, improve diversity
      responsibly, build engineering brand, consider hiring committees
      carefully, the system serves you, executive quick diagnostic) appears
      in the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`), regrouped as bullets.
- [ ] **Anti-patterns are concrete** — at least three (e.g. unicorn job
      requirements, unaccountable interviewer vetoes, systematic overpaying
      to "win" candidates, committees as responsibility diffusion).
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — Authoritative References names Larson's *The
      Engineering Executive's Primer* and the lethain.com companion post.

## Non-goals

- Not an onboarding post — [[engineering-onboarding]] covers what happens
  after the offer is accepted.
- Not a job-search post — [[getting-the-job]] covers my own path into a role,
  not how I hire others.
- Not an interviewing-craft handbook — question design and candidate
  assessment technique beyond the checklist's process level are out of scope.
- No company-specific compensation bands, headcount numbers, or vendor (ATS)
  commitments.
