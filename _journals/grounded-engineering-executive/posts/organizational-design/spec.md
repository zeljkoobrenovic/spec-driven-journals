# Spec: Organizational Design

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I approach organizational design as an executive: I treat the
organization as a system, and I fix systems, not symptoms. The post turns
the *Elegant Puzzle* "Organizational Design" checklist into an operating
principle: choosing the right lever (process vs culture vs org design),
sizing teams deliberately (6–8 engineers per manager, 4–6 managers per
manager-of-managers, grow to 8–10 then split into 4–5), diagnosing each
team's state (falling behind / treading water / repaying debt / innovating)
and applying the matching system fix, consolidating investment in one
constrained team at a time, refusing harmful global optimization (shift
scope, not people; respect re-gelling costs), managing hypergrowth and
entropy, owning organizational risk, and building succession plans that
make me replaceable.

## Audience

My leadership bench and peer executives (so staffing and reorg decisions are
legible as system moves, not favoritism); engineering leaders comparing
operating models. First-person declarative.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — highlight states "diagnose the team's
      state before prescribing the fix" and "shift scope, not people" in one
      paragraph.
- [ ] **The four states are explicit** — falling behind, treading water,
      repaying debt, innovating each appear with the matching system fix
      (add people / reduce WIP / add time / maintain slack).
- [ ] **The numbers survive** — 6–8 engineers per manager, 4–6 managers per
      manager-of-managers, ~8 for on-call, grow to 8–10 then split into
      4–5, a management layer per order of magnitude, ~3 interviews/week.
- [ ] **Consolidation over peanut-buttering** — one constrained team at a
      time is argued, not just listed.
- [ ] **Succession is included** — identify what I do, close the gaps,
      repeat yearly; making myself replaceable framed as the endgame.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — all nine PDF sections reproduced
      in the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`), numbers preserved.
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — References name Larson's *An Elegant Puzzle*
      and note the lethain.com essay origins.

## Non-goals

- Not a reorg playbook or a specific org chart for any company.
- Not [[hiring]] — that record covers how I hire; this one covers where the
  hires go and when.
- Not [[gelling-your-engineering-leadership-team]] — that record covers the
  executive team itself; this one covers the organization below it.
- No claim that the sizing numbers are laws — they are defaults that shift
  the burden of proof.
