![Comic panel: Leo excitedly shows Vera a messy org chart covered in arrows while Vera watches calmly from her desk.](assets/images/organizational-design/comic-01-hero-reorg.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: a struggling team, and the instinct to fix it with heroics and a midnight reorg.*

![Comic panel: a whiteboard shows one struggling team and a second healthy team cracking as a member is pulled away, while Vera warns Leo.](assets/images/organizational-design/comic-02-two-teams-sink.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the quick move breaks the donor team too — now two teams underperform.*

![Comic panel: Vera draws a staircase of four team states on a whiteboard while Leo takes notes.](assets/images/organizational-design/comic-03-diagnose-four-states.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The principle: diagnose first — falling behind, treading water, repaying debt, or innovating.*

![Comic panel: Vera points at the four-state staircase, now tagged with one fix per step, as Leo has a realization.](assets/images/organizational-design/comic-04-right-fix-per-state.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The fix follows the diagnosis: add people, reduce WIP, add time, or keep slack — one per state.*

![Comic panel: Vera moves a box of scope toward a happy intact team while a crossed-out arrow shows a person being pulled away.](assets/images/organizational-design/comic-05-shift-scope-not-people.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Don't raid gelled teams: shift scope to the team — re-gelling costs more than the seat you saved.*

![Comic panel: Leo spreads thin dabs across five teams while Vera waters one team that visibly grows.](assets/images/organizational-design/comic-06-consolidate-investment.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Consolidate: staff one team to the next healthier state, then move on — don't peanut-butter.*

![Comic panel: Leo draws a team of nine dots splitting into two smaller teams while Vera nods approvingly.](assets/images/organizational-design/comic-07-grow-and-split.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *New teams by mitosis: grow a gelled team to 8–10, split into 4–5 — never an empty box on a chart.*

![Comic panel: close-up of Vera handing her open notebook with a checklist to a smiling Leo.](assets/images/organizational-design/comic-08-make-yourself-replaceable.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: succession — a well-designed organization should not need its designer.*
