# Spec: The Next-Generation Operating Model

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I put the whole Team Topologies system together as one operating
model: four team types (stream-aligned, platform, enabling,
complicated-subsystem), three interaction modes (collaboration,
X-as-a-Service, facilitating), team-first boundaries sized to cognitive
load, Conway's law applied deliberately, and continuous organizational
sensing that keeps the topology dynamic rather than fixed. The post is the
**synthesis** of the Team Topologies appendix — the other records develop
the individual pieces; this one assembles them and gives the
getting-started sequence: start with the team, identify streams of change,
identify the thinnest viable platform, close capability gaps, and practice
sensing.

## Audience

My leadership bench and peer executives (so they can name the model we run
and reason about it, not just comply with it); engineering leaders looking
for a coherent alternative to org-chart-driven design. First-person
declarative.

## Non-goals

- Not a re-derivation of the individual pieces — [[four-fundamental-team-topologies]],
  [[team-interaction-modes]], and [[conways-law]] carry the detail; this
  record assembles them.
- Not a reorg announcement or a target org chart for any specific company.
- Not a platform-engineering build guide — the thinnest viable platform
  appears here as a starting move, not as a platform strategy.
- No claim that the topology, once designed, is finished — the model's
  defining property is that it keeps moving.
