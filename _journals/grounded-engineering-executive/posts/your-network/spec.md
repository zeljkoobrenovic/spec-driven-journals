# Spec: Operating Principle: Your Network

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I build and use an executive network: as a long-term exchange of
value, not extraction — I clarify one or two learning goals, prioritize
relationships aligned to what I am actively learning, favor working together
and precise near-network outreach over vague asks, prefer small curated peer
circles over large forums, and keep the whole practice at a sustainable,
modest rhythm (networking stays important, never my top priority). The post
turns the "Building Your Network" checklist into a durable operating
principle: why a curated network is executive infrastructure — for
benchmarking, sensitive advice, compensation signals, and backchannel
references — and how I maintain it without letting it become a job.

## Audience

Engineering leaders (including future me) deciding how much to invest in
networking and how; peers and near-network contacts who want to know what an
outreach or exchange with me looks like. Declarative first-person register —
a vision statement, not a how-to tutorial.

## Success criteria

- [ ] **Principle is quotable** — the highlight blockquote states the
      exchange-of-value, sustainable-rhythm stance in one paragraph.
- [ ] **Rationale is argued, not asserted** — explains why executives need an
      outside network at all (benchmarking, sensitive advice, backchannels)
      and why easier access means lower signal in large communities.
- [ ] **Outreach discipline is concrete** — the cold-outreach rules (near
      network, 2–4 sentences, one specific question, no vague "can we chat")
      appear as practices, not platitudes.
- [ ] **The checklist survives intact** — every section of the source PDF
      (right mindset, networking goals, leveraging effectively, building
      intentionally, community building, large communities, writing &
      speaking, common mistakes, other critical networks — founders, VCs,
      recruiters — and sustainable rhythm) appears in the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`),
      regrouped as bullets.
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — Authoritative References names Larson's *The
      Engineering Executive's Primer* and the lethain.com companion essay.

## Non-goals

- Not about public writing and speaking as a reputation strategy —
  [[personal-and-organizational-prestige]] covers prestige; here writing is
  explicitly a secondary networking channel.
- Not a job-search playbook — [[getting-the-job]] covers using the network
  to find a role; this post is about building and maintaining it.
- No name-dropping or inventory of my actual contacts — the principle, not
  the address book.
