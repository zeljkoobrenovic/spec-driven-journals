**Panel 1:** *The hook: the questions that matter most cannot be asked internally.*

![Comic panel: Vera points at an org chart where her top box has only empty dotted peer boxes beside it.](assets/images/your-network/comic-02-org-chart-ends.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: at the top, the org chart no longer provides a peer group.*

![Comic panel: a phone shows an unanswered message asking to grab time to chat while Vera says the ask earns its silence.](assets/images/your-network/comic-03-the-vague-ask.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *Anti-pattern: the vague ask makes the recipient do all the work.*

![Comic panel: Vera and a peer exchange small packages along a two-way loop of arrows as she says be useful first.](assets/images/your-network/comic-04-exchange-not-extraction.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: a network is a long-term exchange of value, not extraction.*

![Comic panel: Vera sends a short four-line message with one question and the recipient happily replies from their phone.](assets/images/your-network/comic-05-one-precise-question.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Outreach discipline: two to four sentences, one clear question, answerable in a minute.*

![Comic panel: a noisy stadium crowd contrasts with Vera's quiet five-person round table as she notes easier access means lower signal.](assets/images/your-network/comic-06-circle-beats-stadium.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Small and curated beats large and open for sensitive questions.*

![Comic panel: Vera marks one amber dot on a calendar that has a single dot per month and tells Leo one new person a month.](assets/images/your-network/comic-07-modest-rhythm.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The rhythm: consistency over intensity — about one new person a month.*

![Comic panel: Vera closes her notebook and returns to her desk, saying networking is important but never her top priority.](assets/images/your-network/comic-08-never-the-top-priority.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *Closer: the network stays important — and never becomes the job.*
