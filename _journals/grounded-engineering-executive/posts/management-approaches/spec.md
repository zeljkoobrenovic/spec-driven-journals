# Spec: Management Approaches

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State the durable management approaches I carry from the manager years into
the executive role: work the policy rather than the exceptions; say no with
data rather than apology; treat management as an ethical profession that puts
people impact alongside business outcomes; adapt style to growth plates versus
stable areas; avoid the classic ways managers get stuck; partner deliberately
with my own manager; measure scope by impact rather than headcount; set
direction when feedback goes scarce; and for every recurring problem choose
deliberately — close out, solve, or delegate. This is an appendix record: it
grounds in Will Larson's *An Elegant Puzzle* (his earlier, manager-focused
book), read through an executive lens, and complements the Primer-grounded
records in the main journal.

## Audience

My direct reports and leadership bench (these are the approaches I expect to
see, and the ones they can hold me to); peer executives; engineering managers
growing into larger scope. First-person declarative.
