![Comic panel: Leo buried under a pile of exception request slips, happily approving one while more people queue up.](assets/images/management-approaches/comic-01-buried-in-exceptions.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: every exception approved on arrival, and the queue keeps growing.*

![Comic panel: an exhausted Leo, laptop buried under slips, staring at a clock with no time left for real work.](assets/images/management-approaches/comic-02-no-time-left.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: one-off exceptions eat the calendar — and quietly erase the policy.*

![Comic panel: Vera collecting exception slips into a tray that feeds a policy document marked with a refresh loop.](assets/images/management-approaches/comic-03-work-the-policy.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The principle: exceptions are signals — batch them and let the batch redesign the policy.*

![Comic panel: Vera pointing at an overloaded kanban column while a stakeholder looks on, showing where work is stuck.](assets/images/management-approaches/comic-04-no-with-data.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *Saying no with data: the board shows whether the problem is velocity or priorities.*

![Comic panel: Vera drawing a three-way triage fork on a whiteboard, with the loop back to one's own desk crossed out.](assets/images/management-approaches/comic-05-triage-fork.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *The triage: close out, solve, or delegate — the crossed-out path is doing it all yourself.*

![Comic panel: Vera setting aside a tangled process flowchart to talk directly with a worried engineer.](assets/images/management-approaches/comic-06-people-over-process.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The ethics: when process is failing, choose the person — decisions land on lives, not just numbers.*

![Comic panel: Leo stacking three blocks labeled company, team, and self in order while Vera nods.](assets/images/management-approaches/comic-07-company-team-self.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *The hierarchy: decide in the order company, team, yourself — it is what keeps hard calls defensible.*

![Comic panel: Vera handing Leo her notebook full of revised principles, both smiling.](assets/images/management-approaches/comic-08-evolving-profession.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: management is a profession — the philosophy is yours, and it keeps evolving.*
