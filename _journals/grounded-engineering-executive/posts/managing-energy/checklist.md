*The working checklist behind this principle. The Article tab carries the rationale, implications, and anti-patterns.*

### Company → Team → Self (Baseline Alignment)

- [ ] Before making a decision, ask: does this serve the company first?
- [ ] Next ask: does this strengthen the team?
- [ ] Then ask: where do my personal preferences fit?
- [ ] Avoid prioritizing individual or team preferences over company priorities.
- [ ] Re-evaluate if you are following this hierarchy too rigidly.

### Balance Impact with Engagement

- [ ] Am I doing work that is impactful?
- [ ] Am I staying personally energized and engaged?
- [ ] If I am burning out, have I adjusted my work mix?
- [ ] Am I sacrificing long-term sustainability for short-term correctness?
- Reminder: a long-term career requires both impact *and* energy.

### Energy Management (Positive-Sum Thinking)

- [ ] Do I know what types of work energize me?
- [ ] Does each team member know what energizes them?
- [ ] Have I intentionally included some energizing work in the mix?
- [ ] Am I allowing others to do energizing work, even when it is not maximally important?
- [ ] Is energizing work avoiding negative spillover onto other teams?

### Strategic Rule-Breaking (with Guardrails)

- [ ] If I am deviating from strict priorities, is it to restore energy?
- [ ] Is the deviation modest and controlled?
- [ ] Will it create maintenance, execution, or coordination burdens for others?
- [ ] Is it orthogonal (neutral) to company goals — not opposed to them?
- OK: small orthogonal work. Not OK: work in opposition to the company's needs.

### Eventual Quid Pro Quo

- [ ] Generally prioritize company and team over personal work.
- [ ] If de-energized, intentionally increase energizing work until balance returns.
- [ ] If imbalance persists for about a year, seriously reassess the role or environment.
- [ ] Avoid short-term "what's in it for me?" thinking in leadership roles.
- [ ] Expect repayment and reward timing to be decoupled from immediate effort.

### Context Awareness (Mirrors of Misalignment)

- [ ] When something seems misaligned, ask: what context might I be missing?
- [ ] Operate from curiosity rather than judgment.
- [ ] Share strategic context more widely if you are senior; seek tactical context if you are senior and removed from execution.
- [ ] Recognize that seniority creates knowledge gaps as well as insight.

### Orthogonal, Not Opposed

- [ ] Limit outside or exploratory work to moderate levels.
- [ ] Avoid introducing tools or technologies purely for personal interest.
- [ ] Sanity-check gray-area decisions with a trusted peer.
- [ ] If uncertain whether something is aligned, treat that as a warning sign.

### Flexibility over Rigid Adherence

- [ ] Am I overvaluing "correctness" at the expense of progress?
- [ ] Do I equate lack of adherence with character flaws?
- [ ] Am I building relationships or alienating others through rigidity?
- [ ] Can I mute my desire for strict adherence when progress requires flexibility?

### Quick Weekly Reflection

- [ ] What energized me this week?
- [ ] What drained me?
- [ ] Did I prioritize correctly?
- [ ] Did I adapt intelligently?
- [ ] Is my current path sustainable for 12+ months?
