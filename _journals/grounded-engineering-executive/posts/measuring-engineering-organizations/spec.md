# Spec: Operating Principle: Measuring Engineering Organizations

> Working doc for the post in this folder. The spec drives the post; the post
> is the artifact.

## Intent

State how I measure an engineering organization: measurement is audience-
shaped — I measure differently for myself (to plan, operate, optimize, and
inspire) than for stakeholders (CEO/board, Finance, strategic and tactical
peer organizations), and I never present optimization metrics as proof of
business impact. I sequence measurement work deliberately — only measure
what feeds decisions, start with easy wins to build trust, one initiative at
a time — use metrics to improve the system rather than judge individuals,
and build confidence in the data through regular collaborative review. The
post turns the "Measuring Engineering Organizations" checklist into a
durable operating principle: measure something, for a specific audience,
and iterate.

## Audience

Engineering leaders who report metrics to me or with me; the CEO, board,
Finance, and peer executives who consume engineering measurement and should
know what I will and will not show them. Declarative first-person register —
a vision statement, not a metrics catalog.

      the unit of measurement.

      antipatterns; build confidence in data; ongoing mindset) appears in
      the Checklist tab (`checklist.md`), regrouped as bullets.
- [ ] **Anti-patterns are concrete** — at least three (e.g. substituting
      metrics for trust-building, waiting for perfect data, measuring
      individuals instead of teams).
- [ ] **Credit is explicit** — Authoritative References names Larson's *The
      Engineering Executive's Primer* and the lethain.com companion post.

## Non-goals

- Not a planning post — how impact data feeds the resourcing cycle belongs
  to [[planning]]; here measurement is the instrument, not the plan.
- Not a trust post — verifying delegated work through sampling is
  [[inspected-trust]]'s territory; this record is about organizational
  metrics, not inspection of individuals.
- No specific tooling or dashboard prescriptions — the record states what I
  measure and for whom, not which vendor renders it.
