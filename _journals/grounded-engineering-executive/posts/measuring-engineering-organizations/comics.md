![Comic panel: Vera hands different single sheets to figures labeled CEO, Finance, Peers, and Me.](assets/images/measuring-engineering-organizations/comic-04-audience-shapes-metric.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: the audience determines the metric — strategy progress for the CEO, budget fidelity for Finance, shared outcomes for peers.*

![Comic panel: Vera flips Leo's engineer leaderboard over and sketches a system of gears on the back.](assets/images/measuring-engineering-organizations/comic-05-teams-not-individuals.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *The line I hold: metrics improve the system — they are never per-person scorecards.*

![Comic panel: Vera lays a single numbered stepping stone while Leo wobbles holding five stones at once.](assets/images/measuring-engineering-organizations/comic-06-easy-wins-first.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *How it plays out: measure what feeds decisions, start easy to build trust, one initiative at a time.*

![Comic panel: Vera and Leo examine one chart together with a magnifying glass and sticky notes.](assets/images/measuring-engineering-organizations/comic-07-work-the-data.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Trust the data by working it: weekly collaborative review, hypotheses, and digging into surprises.*

![Comic panel: Vera confidently holds up a slightly wobbly hand-drawn chart with eraser marks.](assets/images/measuring-engineering-organizations/comic-08-imperfect-beats-nothing.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The closer: measuring something imperfect is strictly better than measuring nothing.*
