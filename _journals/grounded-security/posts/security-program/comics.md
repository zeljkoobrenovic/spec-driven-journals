![Comic panel: Nadia wheels a shopping cart full of boxed security tools while Vera asks which risk each one treats.](assets/images/security-program/comic-01-the-shopping-cart.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the shopping-cart program — tools bought first, risks named later, licenses looking for a strategy.*

![Comic panel: A wall of mismatched patches labeled after past incidents, with an empty cobwebbed gap labeled Recover.](assets/images/security-program/comic-02-program-of-accidents.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: without a shared map the program becomes the sediment of past incidents — and the gaps stay invisible.*

![Comic panel: During an incident Nadia pulls out a dusty folder labeled Known Risk, dated three years earlier, unsigned.](assets/images/security-program/comic-03-the-silent-acceptance.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: silent acceptance — the risk nobody remediated and nobody signed for, discovered mid-incident.*

![Comic panel: Vera unrolls a map with six regions named after the NIST CSF functions while Nadia places flags on it.](assets/images/security-program/comic-04-risk-before-tools.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: purpose and business objectives first, organized around a framework — every activity gets an address, every gap becomes visible.*

![Comic panel: Nadia writes Risk equals Likelihood times Impact on a whiteboard as a cloud of monsters becomes a neat numbered queue.](assets/images/security-program/comic-05-likelihood-times-impact.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Risk is calculated, not felt: likelihood 1–5, impact 1–5 — arithmetic honesty that converts anxiety into a queue.*

![Comic panel: Four doors labeled Avoid, Remediate, Transfer, Accept; the Accept door bears a signature line and an expiry tag.](assets/images/security-program/comic-06-accepted-with-a-name.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Every risk gets an explicit treatment — and acceptance needs a justification, a named approver, compensating controls, and an expiry date.*

![Comic panel: A four-step staircase from Quick Wins to Long Term; Nadia unplugs an old machine on the first step.](assets/images/security-program/comic-07-quick-wins-first.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Work is tiered: quick wins in days, then this year, next year, long term — early safety buys the right to do the multiyear work.*

![Comic panel: A tabletop exercise around scenario cards while a backup tape labeled Never Restored sits under glass behind them.](assets/images/security-program/comic-08-rehearse-before-real.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The program rehearses: tabletops, kill-chain mapping, and drills — a backup that has never been restored is a hope, not a control.*

![Comic panel: Vera holds an open risk register ledger with columns for owner, treatment, and review date as Nadia points at the top row.](assets/images/security-program/comic-09-show-me-the-register.jpeg)
**Panel 9:** *The closer: the test the program is held to — every major risk has an owner, a treatment decision, and a review date, and the program itself is reassessed.*
