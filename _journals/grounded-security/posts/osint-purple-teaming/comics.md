![Comic panel: a shadowy figure pins company photos and charts on a corkboard with strings while Vera and Nadia stand by the building.](assets/images/osint-purple-teaming/comic-01-they-are-already-looking.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the reconnaissance is happening either way — the only question is who looks at our exposure first.*

![Comic panel: a dumpster with documents, a team photo showing a badge, a password sticky note in a window, and an open door labeled ADMIN.](assets/images/osint-purple-teaming/comic-02-exposure-everywhere.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: exposure is a whole-organization property — dumpsters, badges in photos, sticky notes, breach dumps, and forgotten admin panels add up to one picture.*

![Comic panel: an engineer 'just checking' a system at night while the security monitor flashes an intruder alert with his silhouette.](assets/images/osint-purple-teaming/comic-03-the-unauthorized-favor.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the unauthorized favor — a test nobody approved is indistinguishable from an attack, and gets treated as one.*

![Comic panel: Vera holds an AUTHORIZED document with a scoped map and rules while a big red STOP button hangs on the wall.](assets/images/osint-purple-teaming/comic-04-the-permission-slip.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: nothing runs without written approval — defined scope, rules of engagement, privacy and legal bounds, and an emergency stop everyone can pull.*

![Comic panel: Nadia holds a mirror showing the company building covered in glowing tags on its weak spots while Vera writes a to-do list.](assets/images/osint-purple-teaming/comic-05-the-defenders-mirror.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Practice: OSINT as the defender's mirror — sweep what physical assets, email, systems, metadata, web content, social posts, and breach data reveal, and close what you find.*

![Comic panel: Nadia sorts sourced evidence cards into FACTS and ASSUMPTIONS trays while a second analyst reproduces the work behind her.](assets/images/osint-purple-teaming/comic-06-facts-in-one-bin.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Practice: investigation discipline — every finding sourced, facts separated from assumptions, no more personal data than necessary, and reproducible by another analyst.*

![Comic panel: a prediction card sealed before the test, then a red tester picking a lock while a blue defender watches one bell ring and one stay silent.](assets/images/osint-purple-teaming/comic-07-predict-then-drill.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Practice: the purple drill — expected detections recorded before testing begins; red simulates realistic attacker behavior in scope, blue proves alerts fire and logs support investigation.*

![Comic panel: Vera files away a glossy report and points at a board where every gap card has a name, a date, and a RETEST stamp.](assets/images/osint-purple-teaming/comic-08-owner-deadline-retest.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The finish line: not the report — the exercise ends when every gap found has an owner, a deadline, a fix, and a retest.*

![Comic panel: Vera and Nadia walk a circular garden path clipping new weed sprouts that pop up behind them around the building.](assets/images/osint-purple-teaming/comic-09-the-regrowing-footprint.jpeg)
**Panel 9:** *The closer: the loop repeats — footprints drift and exposure regrows with every hire, launch, and post, so the sweep is periodic and the first question is 'which gaps came back?'*
