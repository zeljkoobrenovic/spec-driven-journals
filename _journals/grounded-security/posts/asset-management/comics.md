![Comic panel: Nadia shines a flashlight on a dusty unknown server tagged with a question mark at 2 a.m.](assets/images/asset-management/comic-01-the-mystery-server.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the ownerless server — critical, running, and nobody's, discovered at 2 a.m. when no one can approve shutting it down.*

![Comic panel: a critical vulnerability alert arrives while Nadia digs through a mountain of binders and printouts.](assets/images/asset-management/comic-02-friday-night-research-project.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: when a critical vulnerability lands on a Friday night, 'which assets run this?' is either a two-hour query or a two-week research project.*

![Comic panel: three screens labeled CMDB, Spreadsheet, and Cloud Console show different counts while a colleague keeps a secret fourth list.](assets/images/asset-management/comic-03-three-competing-inventories.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the three competing inventories — when they disagree, every team keeps a private fourth, and each copy decays independently.*

![Comic panel: Vera points at a single cabinet labeled Source of Truth fed by pipes from smaller systems while Nadia secures its write drawer.](assets/images/asset-management/comic-04-one-source-of-truth.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: one authoritative system, and every team knows which one it is — a decision about where arguments end, not a tooling decision.*

![Comic panel: Nadia attaches owner name tags with criticality gauges to a server, laptop, cloud, certificate, and service account.](assets/images/asset-management/comic-05-owner-on-everything.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Every asset — including the service accounts nobody thinks they own — carries an owner, a criticality, and a risk rating; owner is the single highest-value field.*

![Comic panel: a conveyor belt through Procure, Deploy, Manage, Decommission with credentials replaced at deploy and a drive shredded and certified at decommission.](assets/images/asset-management/comic-06-lifecycle-edges.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *The lifecycle's dangerous moments are its edges: deploy hardened and scanned, decommission with revoked access and verified, documented data destruction — no eBay hard drives.*

![Comic panel: a discovery robot sweeps device icons into a ledger while Nadia inspects one with a magnifying glass and sets a stray gadget aside to investigate.](assets/images/asset-management/comic-07-the-trusting-robot.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Automation feeds the inventory; validation keeps it honest — and an unmanaged asset is a process failure to investigate, not a record to quietly add.*

![Comic panel: a wall calendar with monthly, quarterly, and annual review rings, next to a crossed-out picture of an annual inventory safari.](assets/images/asset-management/comic-08-the-review-rhythm.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The cost of staying honest: a standing rhythm — monthly discoveries and expirations, quarterly reconciliation, annual full review — not a heroic yearly spreadsheet safari.*

![Comic panel: Vera times Nadia with a stopwatch as one screen instantly shows affected assets, their owner, and what faces the internet.](assets/images/asset-management/comic-09-answers-in-minutes.jpeg)
**Panel 9:** *The closer: the test with a stopwatch — which assets are affected, who owns them, what faces the internet, answered in minutes and actually used for decisions.*
