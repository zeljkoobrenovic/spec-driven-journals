![Comic panel: coworkers all point a new employee toward an empty chair with an out-of-office sign when asked how something works.](assets/images/standards-and-procedures/comic-01-ask-marcus.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: every answer that lives only in one engineer's head is an outage waiting for their holiday — and a crisis waiting for their resignation.*

![Comic panel: an on-call engineer at 2 a.m. squints at dense cryptic notes with missing steps as question marks pile up.](assets/images/standards-and-procedures/comic-02-the-2am-reader.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the author already knows the assumptions and the recovery path — the reader at 2 a.m. does not, and that is who the document is for.*

![Comic panel: a monstrous document with layers labeled Why, What, and Commands crumbling at different speeds while Nadia props it up.](assets/images/standards-and-procedures/comic-03-the-everything-policy.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the everything policy — whys, whats, and command lines in one document, impossible to change safely and obsolete at three different speeds simultaneously.*

![Comic panel: a bookcase with four shelves labeled Policy why, Standards what, Procedures who when where, Work instructions how, with links between documents.](assets/images/standards-and-procedures/comic-04-four-shelves.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: a hierarchy with a division of labor — policy explains why, standards define what, procedures say who, when, and where, work instructions explain how.*

![Comic panel: two engineers build different machines from one vague blueprint while Nadia holds a precise blueprint stamped Must.](assets/images/standards-and-procedures/comic-05-must-shall-will.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Standards speak in must, shall, and will — a requirement that two competent engineers can implement two different ways is a suggestion wearing a checkbox.*

![Comic panel: a search party hunts a rule through ten scattered documents versus one glowing standard linked by threads to ten policies.](assets/images/standards-and-procedures/comic-06-write-once-reference-everywhere.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Requirements are written once and referenced everywhere — the copy you miss becomes the contradiction the auditor finds and the engineer follows.*

![Comic panel: a calm on-call engineer follows a numbered procedure card with verified assumptions, a real command, and a use-judgment signpost.](assets/images/standards-and-procedures/comic-07-executable-by-the-reader.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Procedures are executable by their reader: ordered steps, active voice, verified assumptions, real commands — and explicit judgment where prescription would be impractical.*

![Comic panel: a document passes through a quality gate collecting stamps for readable, no conflicts, SME reviewed, and approved, with a version-block tag.](assets/images/standards-and-procedures/comic-08-the-quality-gate.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *Every document carries its contract — version, owner, approver, purpose, scope — and nothing is published without the quality review, an approval on record, and a review date.*

![Comic panel: the new employee completes a task alone using the authoritative document while the once-indispensable engineer relaxes unneeded.](assets/images/standards-and-procedures/comic-09-without-asking-anyone.jpeg)
**Panel 9:** *The closer: the test the whole layer is held to — a new employee can find the authoritative document and apply it correctly without asking anyone.*
