![Comic panel: three vignettes of a laptop opening a wormy attachment, a phone left in a taxi, and a cobwebbed printer, Nadia pointing.](assets/images/endpoints/comic-01-where-the-attack-lands.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: the endpoint is where the attack actually lands — the laptop that opens the attachment, the phone that leaves the taxi, the printer nobody patched.*

![Comic panel: a green updates-enabled dashboard above a row of quietly rusting laptops, Nadia discovering one is hollow.](assets/images/endpoints/comic-02-the-green-dashboard-lie.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the unverified patch job — updates enabled everywhere and checked nowhere, so the fleet drifts to unpatched while the dashboard stays green.*

![Comic panel: a manager plants one tiny antivirus umbrella over a pile of devices under malware storm clouds, an exempt crowned laptop nearby.](assets/images/endpoints/comic-03-antivirus-as-strategy.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: antivirus as strategy — one agent installed, box ticked, program declared done — while the executive's laptop skips the controls entirely.*

![Comic panel: a queue of laptop, desktop, phone, and printer with race numbers approaching a single bar held by Vera, stamps reading patched, hardened, encrypted, managed.](assets/images/endpoints/comic-04-one-bar-every-device.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: one bar for every endpoint — patched and verified, hardened by subtraction, firewalled, encrypted, protected, managed, and monitored, whatever the device class.*

![Comic panel: Nadia scans a laptop at a gate as a padlock lights up confirmed active, recovery keys in a wall case, a sleeping laptop dreaming of keys.](assets/images/endpoints/comic-05-encrypted-before-it-ships.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Practice: full-disk encryption is a pre-deployment gate — confirmed active before the device ships, recovery keys centrally managed — and a sleeping laptop still holds keys in memory, so real physical threat means shut down.*

![Comic panel: a laptop carries its own firewall shield from office to coffee shop to home while an enrolled phone is remotely wiped.](assets/images/endpoints/comic-06-the-policy-travels.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Practice: protection travels with the device — the host firewall stays on across home and public networks, and mobile devices are enrolled, policy-managed, and remotely wipeable.*

![Comic panel: Nadia signs a document with HR and legal representatives while a telemetry monitor waits unplugged behind them.](assets/images/endpoints/comic-07-the-privacy-contract-first.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Practice: visibility with the privacy contract signed first — telemetry on processes, connections, and files is centralized for detection, but expectations are agreed with HR and legal before the monitoring starts.*

![Comic panel: Nadia opens a closet full of printer, camera, thermostat, door lock, and desk phone wearing default-password tags.](assets/images/endpoints/comic-08-the-forgotten-endpoints.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *Practice: the forgotten endpoints count — printers, cameras, HVAC, door locks, and telephony get inventoried, default passwords changed, firmware updated, and the ones that cannot meet the bar get segmented, not exempted.*

![Comic panel: Vera reviews a scoreboard of device classes all green-checked, the printer wearing a medal beside Nadia.](assets/images/endpoints/comic-09-green-including-the-printer.jpeg)
**Panel 9:** *The closer: the test the estate is held to — the final endpoint security review comes back green for every device class, including the printer.*
