![Comic panel: Nadia shows Vera hardened-server dashboards while a stranger walks through an open server room door behind them.](assets/images/physical-security/comic-01-physical-access-is-root.jpeg)
**Panel 1:** *The hook: physical access is root access — someone at the machine needs no exploit, so an unlocked server room hardens the estate on paper only.*

![Comic panel: cutaway of a building with a fortified lobby but completely open floors above it, Nadia pointing upward.](assets/images/physical-security/comic-02-the-lobby-perimeter.jpeg)
**Panel 2:** *The problem: the lobby perimeter — all the security budget at the front desk means one tailgate defeats the entire estate.*

![Comic panel: an employee cheerfully holds a secure door open for a confident badge-less stranger in a branded polo.](assets/images/physical-security/comic-03-the-polite-door-hold.jpeg)
**Panel 3:** *The wrong way: the polite door-hold — politeness becomes the attack surface when challenging a stranger feels rude.*

![Comic panel: Vera presents concentric defense rings labeled badge, PIN, camera, escort around a server rack while Nadia takes notes.](assets/images/physical-security/comic-04-layers-not-one-lock.jpeg)
**Panel 4:** *The principle: defend in layers — highly sensitive areas sit behind more than one control, so no single failed control reaches the data.*

![Comic panel: Nadia ticks a departure checklist as a badge is returned, a keypad recoded, and a shared PIN note crossed out.](assets/images/physical-security/comic-05-revocation-is-the-discipline.jpeg)
**Panel 5:** *Practice: granting access is easy, revocation is the discipline — badges recovered same day, shared codes changed, and shared PINs never guard sensitive rooms because shared secrets have no offboarding.*

![Comic panel: Nadia connects a badge log entry to camera footage of a face on two side-by-side screens.](assets/images/physical-security/comic-06-cameras-answer-who.jpeg)
**Panel 6:** *Practice: cameras are for correlation, not decoration — positioned to capture faces, protected from tampering, and reviewed against badge logs to turn an entry record into evidence.*

![Comic panel: Nadia escorts a visitor with an expiring badge while a technician logs a labeled backup tape into a locked case.](assets/images/physical-security/comic-07-escorts-and-tracked-media.jpeg)
**Panel 7:** *Practice: people and media at the perimeter — visitors and contractors are identified, escorted, and time-boxed, and removable media moves with classification labels, approvals, and a chain of custody.*

![Comic panel: a penetration tester tries a held door, a cloned badge, and a courier pretext, each blocked, while Nadia records results.](assets/images/physical-security/comic-08-test-the-doors.jpeg)
**Panel 8:** *The cost: the program is only real if it is exercised — controls are audited, and at least one assessment per cycle actually tries the held door, the cloned badge, the confident pretext.*

![Comic panel: an intruder past one open door is stopped by a second locked barrier under a camera as an employee approaches.](assets/images/physical-security/comic-09-no-single-failed-control.jpeg)
**Panel 9:** *The closer: the test the estate is held to — no single failed control, a held door, a cloned badge, or a confident pretext, is ever enough on its own.*
