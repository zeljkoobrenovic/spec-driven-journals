Grounded Engineering Management

Željko Obrenović's engineering-management operating model, written as records across the whole ladder — from being managed, through mentoring, tech lead, and first-line management, to managing managers and senior leadership — plus the engineer career expectations that management exists to serve. Each record ships its working checklist. Informed by practice, by Camille Fournier's "The Manager's Path", Julie Zhuo's "The Making of a Manager", and Gergely Orosz's "The Software Engineer's Guidebook".
