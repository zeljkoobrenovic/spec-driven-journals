# LISTED: Architecting Large-Scale Classifieds and Marketplace Platforms

Željko Obrenović

![LISTED: Architecting Large-Scale Classifieds and Marketplace Platforms — logo](listing-systems-architecture/assets/images/journal-logo.jpeg)

How architects and engineering leaders design, scale, govern and evolve the systems behind classifieds and listing marketplaces — listings, ingestion, search, trust, monetization and the multi-country platforms beneath them. This is a living journal and a work in progress: I revise it as I learn more through [my work](https://obren.io).
